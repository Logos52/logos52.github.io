- Rozenblit, L. & Keil, F. (2002): the illusion of explanatory depth.
- Craik, F. I. M. & Lockhart, R. S. (1972): relational versus isolated encoding.
- Roediger, H. L. & Karpicke, J. D. (2006); Kornell, N., Hays, M. J. & Bjork, R. A. (2009): generation with corrective feedback.
- Rohrer, D. & Taylor, K. (2007); Taylor, K. & Rohrer, D. (2010): interleaving forces discrimination.
- Sweller, J.: cognitive load theory; the extraneous / germane distinction; extraneous load, including load from the monitor itself.
- Kolb, D. A. (1984): the experiential cycle; the prediction-error engineering on this page is house.
- Broadwell, M. M. (1969) / Gordon Training: the four stages, kept as a popular model for sizing gains, not as a measured curve.
- Donker, A. S. et al. (2014): meta-analysis, learning-strategy instruction; metacognitive strategy instruction is trainable as a class.
- Hattie, J., Biggs, J. & Purdie, N. (1996): meta-analysis, study-skills interventions.
- Schwarz, N. & Clore, G. L. (1983): affect as information, the nearest field cousin to the cue-as-feeling claim.
- Smallwood & Schooler 2006, *Psychological Bulletin*: mind-wandering during reading is common and often unnoticed until probed.
- Schooler, Smallwood, Christoff, Handy, Reichle & Sayette 2011, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*: meta-awareness is intermittent; caught in the act versus caught by a probe.
- Reichle, Reineberg & Schooler 2010, *Psychological Science*: mindless reading; the eyes keep moving.
