# Common Traps

## Sources

- Bjork 1994; Bjork, Dunlosky & Kornell 2013, *Psychology of Learning and Motivation*, on desirable difficulties: methods that feel effective now often produce worse retention later, and the bound on reading heavy effort as a misfit.
- Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham 2013, *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*, utility ratings: rereading and highlighting high in felt effectiveness and low in actual; practice testing and distributed practice high.
- Wald 1943, on survivorship: the visible successes used the method, and the failures stay quiet.
- Nisbett & Wilson 1977, *Psychological Review*: people have poor access to the causes of their own behaviour.
- Cronbach & Snow 1977: aptitude–treatment interaction, the field's name for a method used on a problem it cannot solve.
- Adriaanse, Gollwitzer, De Ridder, de Wit & Kroese 2011: replacement intentions beat negation intentions.
- Cepeda, Pashler, Vul, Wixted & Rohrer 2006, *Psychological Bulletin*: spacing, the legitimate use of a retrieval schedule.
