## Sources

- Zimmerman, B. J. (2002). Becoming a self-regulated learner: An overview. *Theory Into Practice*. Forethought, performance, self-reflection: the page's six steps are that cycle unpacked.
- Dignath, C., & Büttner, G. (2008). Components of fostering self-regulated learning among students. A meta-analysis on intervention studies at primary and secondary school level. *Metacognition and Learning*. SRL training average d ≈ 0.69 in school interventions; trainable, moderate, not magic.
- Bjork, R. A. (1994). Memory and metamemory considerations in the training of human beings. In Metcalfe & Shimamura (Eds.), *Metacognition*. Desirable difficulties.
- Kornell, N., & Bjork, R. A. (2008). Learning concepts and categories: Is spacing the “enemy of induction”? *Psychological Science*; Kirk-Johnson, A., Galla, B. M., & Fraundorf, S. H. (2019). Perceiving effort as poor learning. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General*. Experienced difficulty used as a cue that the method is worse.
- Sonnentag, S., & Fritz, C. (2007). The Recovery Experience Questionnaire. *Journal of Occupational Health Psychology*. Psychological detachment from work.
- Dinsmore, D. L., Alexander, P. A., & Loughlin, S. M. (2008). Focusing the conceptual lens on metacognition, self-regulation, and self-regulated learning. *Educational Psychology Review*. The field's names tangle; this vault's SM/SR split is a coaching cut.
- Jia, M., et al. (2021) and related remote-learning SRL reviews (Hong et al. 2021; Chen 2020). When outside structure falls away, SRL demand rises; higher SRL is sometimes less damaged. A structure-collapse example, not a national experiment.
