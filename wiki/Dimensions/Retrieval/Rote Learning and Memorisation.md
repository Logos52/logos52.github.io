# Rote Learning and Memorisation

Memorisation is last-resort work on leftover facts with no hook. Those leftovers are names, constants, unordered lists — anything without a causal, functional, or conceptual connection — and they are reached for after the conceptual structure is built, never as the way the topic is learned. Rote works. It is the wrong first tool.

## What it costs early

Used too soon, isolated facts take the place of a structure. They forget faster. The pile grows past what the topic needs, and the hold snaps. Reaching for these tools on most of the material means the structure upstream was skipped.

Two tools remain for the leftover the structure cannot hold.
[[Flashcards]] mop up that leftover as prompt-and-answer cards, one fact at a time, outer layer only.
[[Method of Loci]] holds a leftover list as a drawn scene, from a few items to thirty or more.
They are a pair, not a program.

They sit on top of the encoding and retrieval already running, not in place of them.
[[Bear Hunter System]] is that encoding practice.
[[Revision]] is the retrieval loop that continues.
[[Higher-Order Learning]] is the conceptual core that must exist first.
[[Layers of Learning]] is where leftover arbitrary detail sits.

The tools manufacture a hold for facts the structure cannot hold. That is why they work, and why they are last.
