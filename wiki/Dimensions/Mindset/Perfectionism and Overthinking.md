# Perfectionism and Overthinking

## Sources

- Stoeber & Otto (2006), *Personality and Social Psychology Review*: perfectionistic strivings versus concerns.
- Hewitt & Flett (1991), *JPSP*; Frost, Marten, Lahart & Rosenblate (1990): the facet structures; Hamachek (1978): the older normal/neurotic split.
- Sederlund, Burns & Rogers (2020), *IJERPH*: fear of failure sits with the maladaptive facets.
- van Eerde (2003); Xie, Yang & Chen (2018): perfectionism and procrastination; the concerns facet predicts delay.
- Nolen-Hoeksema, Wisco & Lyubomirsky (2008); Watkins (2008): rumination versus reflection; abstract repetitive thought versus concrete problem-solving.
- Metcalfe (2017), *Annual Review of Psychology*; Bjork & Bjork: learning from errors, desirable difficulties.
- Lieberman et al. (2007), *Psychological Science*: affect labelling; D'Zurilla & Nezu: problem-solving therapy; Gollwitzer: implementation intentions.
