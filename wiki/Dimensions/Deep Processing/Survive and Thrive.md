# Survive and Thrive

Each new piece gets one test, whether it solves a problem or fits a pattern. Analogies, ties to prior learning, and simpler labels attach it to what is already known, and comparing or grouping attaches it among the new pieces. What sits in that network is kept, and what does not is ordinary forgetting.

## How attachments get made

Every method on this page is already one of those two.

Two kinds of relationship cover the work. An analogous relationship links the new piece to something already known — an accurate, comprehensive analogy, a tie to prior learning, or a simpler label that makes the term usable. A decorative analogy does not transfer; the structure has to match. An in-context relationship links the new piece to another new piece — comparing them, grouping them, drawing how they affect each other, placing them by what they connect to. Grouping by shared features is the weak form; [[Importance-Based Chunking]] is how the groups actually reduce load. Applying the piece to a problem immediately is the problem half of the test: retrieval plus use. Comparing and evaluating is the depth these links are for, the work [[Higher-Order Learning]] names.

One kind can carry a session; both is the target. Either kind can be primed before the session starts. Practice questions ahead of time, and a [[Prestudy]] pass that finds the main ideas and how they relate, surface the points that make both kinds easier to see.

## Memory is a symptom

Memory and understanding are symptoms of structure, not of intending to remember. Trying to hold an isolated item by effort is the move this page replaces. [[Cave Theory]] is the frame: isolated school facts slip; connected, purpose-tied ones stay. This page is that frame as a method.

## When nothing attaches

If no relationship will form at all, the material is too detailed for the current level. The move is not to push harder. A surface-level foundation through [[Prestudy]], then a return. A fact held alone, or even a concept understood in isolation, is forgotten faster — ordinary forgetting, not a nightly sweep.

The case against is a decorative analogy, a push through detail that will not attach, and treating the methods as a garnish on memorization. The price is both directions, on purpose, every piece — that is the work, not extra. The quit signal is still-isolated facts after a session, or a can't-relate answered by trying harder. The checkable expectation: for a new piece, one problem it helps with or one pattern it fits can be named.

What stays is what passed that test on purpose, attached to what was already known and among the new pieces. Isolated pieces still slip. The work is running the test before they do.
