## Sources

- Ausubel, D. P. (1960). The use of advance organizers in the learning and retention of meaningful verbal material. *Journal of Educational Psychology*. A low-load frame before high-density input.
- Ausubel, D. P. (1968). *Educational Psychology: A Cognitive View*.
- Mayer, R. E. (1979). Can advance organizers influence meaningful learning? *Review of Educational Research*.
- Mayer, R. E., Mathias, A., & Wetzell, K. (2002). Fostering understanding of multimedia messages through pre-training: Evidence for two types of pre-training. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied*. Pre-training principle, from multimedia learning research: a simple model first, then the dense material; a complex lesson lands better when the names and behaviours of its key elements are already known.
- Pollock, E., Chandler, P., & Sweller, J. (2002). Assimilating complex information. *Learning and Instruction*. Isolated elements: high-interactivity material is learnable once a simpler version exists.
- Adler, M. J., & Van Doren, C. (1972). *How to Read a Book* (rev. ed.). Syntopical reading as the fourth level: reading across authors to answer questions the reader set. Different procedure from the ease ladder.
