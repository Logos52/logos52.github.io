# Rules of Effective Memorization

There is a reliable way to tell whether study time is going to pay, and it shows up in the grain of the thing being memorized. Copying a character stroke by stroke while reciting its sound and meaning fills pages and leaves almost nothing behind, because strokes are the wrong unit — they carry no information about the word. The units that pay are the functional components: 糹 telling you the meaning runs through thread and cloth, 巠 telling you roughly how the word sounds. Finding that grain is encoding, and it is what the seven rules here are for. Four of them build the trace and three of them keep it.

## The meaningfulness ladder

Material encodes in proportion to how much it means to the learner. Nearly every other rule on this page works by manufacturing more of that meaning. Chinese characters chart the proportion as a four-rung ladder. Each rung up makes new items easier, old ones easier to recall, and unstudied ones partly predictable.

**Strokes are the weakest grain.** Building 經 stroke by stroke offers no chunking and no connection to sound or meaning. The pieces are the wrong size.

**Non-functional pieces are still weak.** Splitting 經 into 幺 + 小 + 一 + 巛 + 工 cuts the load to five chunks, and none of those meanings play a role in the character. Many references stop here. The chunks are dead.

**Functional components are strong.** A functional component is a piece that actually does a job — carries sound, or carries meaning — as opposed to a piece that is only easy to cut out by eye. In 經, 糹 carries meaning and 巠 carries sound. 輕 reuses the same sound component beside 車. Two meaningful chunks replace five dead ones.

**Knowing how the jobs are done is the strongest rung.** Sound-component variation is patterned: jīng and qīng share a place of articulation. Meaning components extend in patterned ways. The 金 series covers metals, then objects made of metal, then actions done with them. The system itself becomes the mnemonic.

Once the components are functional, the sound and meaning of the spoken word cue reconstruction of the written form, and an unknown item can be decomposed into a guess. Encoding and retrieval run over the same connections in opposite directions.

Copying from stroke-order diagrams while reciting pronunciation and meaning converts heavy effort into feeble results. That is rote at a level that carries no meaning. The hours look like study. The slot stays empty.

## Three ways to manufacture meaning

Organisation: the brain retains organised information better than the same content left unorganised. Regrouping alone manufactures meaning. The classroom demonstration is a letter-string in three cuts. Unspaced, the letters are noise. BUS HAW OR THIS is still opaque — the breaks are in the wrong places. Cut as the proverb those letters already were, the same string collapses into one familiar unit, and the load drops because the pieces now mean something.

Characters ship with two layers of pre-built organisation: functional components inside a character, and series across characters. A 方-series and a 目-series are keepable texture. Series order follows the script. Need follows the spoken language. Bulk-learning a whole series front-loads items that have no word to attach to. The move a reader acts on is the other one: compare each new arrival against known members of the series, rather than learning the series as a block.

Association: tying new material to known material transfers meaningfulness across the link and turns the link into a recall route. The right side of 嫩 refused to stick until it was bound to the top of 整 — a daily-use character that shares the component — after which recall never failed. The repair is recognisable because it uses a character already in the mouth, not an invented story.

Visualisation: picturable material encodes more easily because vision is a channel for dealing with the world. Anyone can count the doors in their own house by walking the rooms mentally. Modern glyphs lost their pictures. Ancient picture-like forms restore the bridge. 及, 禾, 秉, and 兼 are the set. That is not a claim that modern 日 "is" a sun. It is a claim that a once-picturable form can be consulted once, at first encounter, so the modern shape has something to sit on.

## The four carry more weight than the three

Seven rules decide whether deliberate memorisation works in any domain built on thousands of seemingly arbitrary items. Meaningfulness, organisation, association, and visualisation build the encoding. Attention gates whether encoding happens. Repetition consolidates. Interest funds the hours.

The first four carry more weight than the last three because they determine what any repetition has to work with. That ranking is this page's claim. It is consistent with depth-of-processing. It is not a measured result, and it is not someone else's finding borrowed as one. [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing|Deep Processing]] is the dimension those four apply to memorisation.

## The three that fund the work

Attention: learning happens only while attention is engaged. Unattended exposure converts study time into nothing. Attention is monitored while the work is on, because the empty-slot failure often starts here — the name at the introduction, heard and not held.

Repetition is necessary. Alone, it suffices only for very simple material. Stacked on the encoding rules, retention climbs. Spaced repetition raises it further. [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval|Retrieval]] is where this rule opens out, and [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval/Spaced Interleaved Retrieval|Spaced Interleaved Retrieval]] is the house system it plugs into. Handwriting exercises belong in the same frame: good exercise for the hand, separate from the encoding work. "I wrote it fifty times" is evidence of a tired hand, not of an encoded form.

Interest: time follows interest. Making the material more interesting raises effort and, through time-on-task and depth, results. Working out the reason behind a character's shape is itself engaging, and the engagement deposits meaning as a side effect. The seventh rule loops back into the first. Attention, repetition, and interest rank below the encoding four and still decide outcomes. A perfectly analysed component that is never repeated, or never attended, does not hold.

## Research Foundations

The portable move is to find the functional level before memorising. Learn atoms whole: form, sound, and meaning together. Anchor to live vocabulary — the spoken word supplies the cues reconstruction depends on. Build connections on purpose at encoding time; they double as recall routes. Frequency first, with one family exception: 秉 completes 禾 and 兼, so the rare member is taken with its family rather than deferred.

The failure list is the ladder run in reverse. Rote at the meaningless level. Stopping at non-functional decomposition. Bulk series study. Expecting exact sound matches — variation is patterned, and treating it as noise breaks trust in the system. Expecting transparent meaning links — 漢 carries water because it named a river first; opaque links have histories. Blaming storage for encoding failures: the name that vanished was never encoded.

The move generalizes wherever a domain has a level of analysis at which items stop being arbitrary. Anatomy, APIs, terminology. The job is to find that grain, then stop. Whether a grain is always findable stays open. The search is the instruction.

Meaningfulness, organisation, association, visualisation, and the attention cluster are published. Depth and the empty slot are established. Organisation aids memory. Dual coding is established. Spacing is established. Encoding specificity is established. Functional-component analysis is standard character pedagogy. The four-over-three ranking remains this page's claim.

The case against: already-meaningful material may not need the ladder, which is the open question. The character proof is a domain instance, not the only grain. Treating the ranking as a measured result. Using the page as a paleography course. The price is the time to find the functional grain before any repetition starts. The benefit in the same breath: later items get cheaper, and unstudied ones become partly guessable. Quit signals: still copying strokes after the components are known; bulk-learning a series with no spoken word to attach; "I wrote it fifty times" offered as evidence of encoding. Checkable: an unknown item can be decomposed to a guess; a spoken word cues the written form; a dead-chunk split has been replaced by jobs.

The hours spent working out why a form is the way it is deposit the meaning the first rule asked for. The empty slot fills from the far side.
