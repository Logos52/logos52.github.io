# Research bank — Report intro paragraph

What an intro paragraph is for a report, brief, or decision note — not a wiki page, not a personal page.

## Novelty note

Checked: Opening Moves Catalog (sentence-one moves for wiki pages); Opening Doors (paragraph-level doors; inventory fault, 2026-08-15); Intro QA (five tests for personal/political pages; nineteen-test grind killed 2026-08-19 because it did not converge); Claude intro-convergence intake 2026-08-19 (16 critic rounds, failures did not fall); owner strike 2026-08-13 that copular intros were not being executed. This lane is a different object: the first paragraph of a **report that answers a question**. Those files never named that object. Decision writing said "verdict in the first two sentences," and that got executed as a list of findings. That is the gap.

Do not rerun the nineteen-test grind. Do not add a detector.

## Subject in plain words

An intro paragraph tells the reader the answer to the question the piece was asked, in a few sentences that follow from each other. After it, they know the finding. They do not yet have the parts.

Ownership: half-owned. Everyone has seen an intro. The content here is which job it has, and which job it does not.

## Terminology

- **Lead / intro paragraph** — the first paragraph. The reader can stop after it and have the answer.
- **Inventory** — the struck fault: sentence one is fine, then the parts arrive as a list.
- **Body** — where the parts, mechanisms, dates, and quotes live.
