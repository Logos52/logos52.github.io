## Who should test the models: three answers given

1. **Musk.** Competing labs test each other's models before release, with advance access. The finder tells the builder, and goes public if it is not fixed. Harnesses could be open-sourced. It has to be something China could accept. Sacks and Chamath added that product-liability law already applies to unsafe AI.
2. **Nadella.** Outside testers, with broad access and no cozy arrangements over who tests whom.
3. **Huang.** Third-party evaluators like financial auditors, and several of them, so that none gets "pilled".
