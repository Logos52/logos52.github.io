# The Table Bank

Source bank for an optional page. The playlist's home subject is dating, and its levels are taught through dating examples. This bank holds those examples so the other pages can stay clean. Corpus table and method note are in [[wiki/Research/Levels of Thinking Bank|Levels of Thinking Bank]]. Kinds: **R** reasoning, **X** the speaker's own example, **W** a claim about the world, **L** the speaker's own life.

**This bank produces facts. It does not draft prose.**

## The subject in plain words

Being the table means only my needs count and the other person is there to fill them. The step out of it is the question what do I offer, and it is the same step as the move from the third level of thinking to the fourth.

## Claim ledger

- **T1** (R, X, v1, v3, v6, v8). The table. Men ask women what they bring to the table; some women answer I am the table, meaning only my needs matter, you do not have needs, I just deserve things and that is the way it is. The male version: I want her to like me for me, which asks which part, the part that plays video games six hours a day. The two versions are the same move.
- **T2** (R, X, v8). The realization: "I have nothing to offer a man." The speaker calls that joining the top half of humanity in development, moving from what will people put on the table for me to what do I have to put on it. Three steps: this is what I need from you and you and you, be quiet about what you need; then, suddenly, all these people also need things from me; then, a lot of people are still at step one. The question a shocking number of people never ask: why would people want to give me what I want.
- **T3** (R, L, v8). The speaker says he went from a four to an eight by asking what the women around him want, how they behave, and what he needs to do and have for them to want to give him what he wants. Dressing nicer as an example; the objection "I don't like being told what to do" misses the point, which is that people respond to how you show up and that is what your life is like. When people do not get what they want their reaction is "this part should change, everyone but me," and that part is not under their control; what they control is what they want, how they think about it, and how they show up.
- **T4** (R, X, L, v4). Wanting what is bad for you. Attraction is caused by the fun, dangerous thing, not by good financial decisions; choose by attraction and you get the good stuff now and the bad later. Society used to make the choice for you; now you must, which is a struggle between what you want and what is smart. No amount of value protects from hypergamy: the pull toward a man with more. A partner heard about a taller, richer client, noticed the pull, and told the speaker at once; she knew that replacing the man who wants you always ends at the man who does not. Adults talk about their feelings. The ick is the body saying maybe not this one; the zero-ick stretch lasts about three months at most, and switching every time ends in chaos, no marriages, no children. If feelings are in charge you live in hell; feelings say what you want, not what you should do.
- **T5** (R, X, v9). The circle: she cheated on the last man; we meet; things go well; engaged; married; a baby; no intimacy; "there has to be more to life than wife and mother"; girls' trips; a new career; he supports everything; she cheats; the circle starts again. Both people only think about the part of the relationship they are in. Two people at that level get the makeup-breakup cycle. The higher you go, the more you can see around corners.
- **T6** (R, W, v3). Men are more upset by physical cheating, because he spends his time and money on her and someone else gets the benefit. Women are more forgiving of physical cheating and hate a man giving his investment, money, time, support, bonding, to other women, because she gets less. The two sexes fight about which is worse every day, and it is like telling a fly that dog mess is bad for it. You cannot tell someone what matters to them, which is also why "my past doesn't matter to you" comes from a low level.
- **T7** (R, X, v5). Dating is what you offer matched to what the other wants. Be honest up front; photos from the neck up only, angles, and the iceberg waste both people's time and money and make the other person want to say something mean. Men put in work for dates too and do not charge for it. The ask for credit for makeup priced at $392 is entitlement fed by the hive mind.
- **T8** (R, X, v7). A husband confused by a wife who wants respect and also wants to be treated roughly, "chivalry is not dead but I have to choke it a little." Women want two things that seem to contradict and do not have to. The levels alternate between your own needs and others' needs, and at the understand level you see multiple valid perspectives that overlap and compete, which is confusing until you integrate them at the next level.
- **T9** (R, X, v11). The joke about hurting when a loyal admirer moves on. A joke communicates something that should not be happening; it does not mean it will not happen. She may keep using him if she can get him back.
- **T10** (R, X, v14). A woman messages a man from eight years ago, is blocked, pays a dollar to ask why, learns he has a wife and children, and wakes up mortified. A normal story now that was not always normal.

## What was left out and why

- The speaker's own dating history beyond what carries a claim.
