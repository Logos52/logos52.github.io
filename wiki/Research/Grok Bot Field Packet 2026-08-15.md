# Grok Bot Field Packet — 2026-08-15

**Verdict:** the pinned list confirms the 13 Aug bank on architecture and does not change the stack. Three mechanisms are new enough to absorb: official first-task shape (outcome / sources / constraints / deliverable / review point); Nate's "a Bot owns a theme, not a task"; Teach-a-task holds on short recordings and goes brittle on long ones.
