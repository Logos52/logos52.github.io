## The subject in plain words

A level of thinking is one more step back from your own reaction. There are nine. The bottom three are about getting what you want. From four up you can see yourself from outside, then your culture from outside, then the looking itself, then your own mind as something that happens to you, then your mind building a reaction as it happens, then the choosing of where to look from. Nobody lives at a level. Higher means more aware, not better or nicer.

## Claim ledger

### A. What a level is

- **A1** (R, v13, v3). Levels are stages of psychological development. Nobody is in a level. A person slowly gains the ability to think at higher levels with experience. Everyone uses the lower levels every day. Needing the bathroom is a level-one process, a feeling and nothing more.
- **A2** (W, v13, v3). Everyone is born at the bottom. Nearly everyone reaches at least level four by adulthood. Most people on Earth spend most of their time in levels one to four. Level five is common only in modernized places. In places with all the right ingredients the average is about four to five with about 15% at six. The world average is three to four.
- **A3** (R, v13, v3). What decides how far and how fast a person develops: surroundings, choices, culture, and the brain they were born with. Like a plant needing sun, air, water, and soil, not one thing.
- **A4** (R, v13, v1). Higher means more aware, not a better person, and not always more effective. Higher does not mean nicer. It is not a ladder of hugs. You cannot hug a bear into not being a bear; you need bear spray. If a mad dog is coming, compassion is no use.
- **A5** (R, v13). Lines. Each capacity develops to its own level. A person can be at nine on thinking and three on morals. Someone can be higher overall and worse than another person at one thing. Everyone's fingerprint of development is different.
- **A6** (R, v13). Why climb if higher is not always better: higher is usually better; a complex world with grocery stores and electricity needs complex thinking; some problems cannot be solved at the level they came from.
- **A7** (R, v1). Signposts, not a checklist. Feeling a feeling once does not mean living at that level. Confusing the map with the landscape; the finger pointing at the moon is not the moon. Pretending to be at a high level is the best way never to grow, because you never do the work to get there. People stuck at six fantasize they are at eight and nine.
- **A8** (R, v3). Each level includes more. What do I want. What do you want. What do they want. What do we want as a group. What do I want as part of the group. Which part of everything do I want to be. No one part will satisfy me.
- **A9** (R, v7). The levels alternate between your own needs and the needs of others: personal needs, the needs of others around you, negotiating a stronger position in your society, occupying the most useful position, standing out, recognizing how others want to stand out.
- **A10** (R, v1, v3). The count of persons. Levels one to three are first person. Level four is second person. Five is third person. Six is fourth person. Seven is fifth person. Nine is sixth person. Second person is multiple first persons; third is multiple seconds; and so on. Each level is one more layer of a thought about a thought. A mosquito can know it wants blood but cannot simulate your consciousness; higher beings can.
- **A11** (R, X, v1, v11). The same decision can be made at any level. The reasoning is what differs. A girl whose father warns her about boys can date the boy at every level: because I want to (one); because the screen said so (two, conform to whichever support she has); because I do not care what the boy wants, I want to live my life (three, third person); because I could choose to be cautious or to play fast and loose and I choose fast and loose (four, fourth person).
- **A12** (R, v1). Clarity increases at every level. At third person she sees the reasons each man has for wanting his own way, not just who to listen to.
- **A13** (R, v1). More complexity in thinking gives more power to choose, and that is where the floating feeling starts.
- **A14** (R, v3, v11). Names on the new chart, one row per level, what it does and what it wants: one desire (was survive), two appease (was connect), three control, four conform or belong, five achieve, six understand or include, seven harmonize, eight construct aware, nine complete. The names match the kind of behavior seen at each level.

### B. The nine levels

- **B1** (R, v13, v3). One, desire. What do I need right now. A feeling or urge. No such thing as someone else's mind. Everyone feels this all day: I want a cookie, that girl is pretty. An adult stuck here is basically homeless; even remote tribes connect.
- **B2** (R, X, v13, v3). Two, appease. Other people have desire too. Give them what they want so they give something back. Groups here appease gods and spirits: the volcano is angry, what would make me angry, not enough virgins. Small groups who only deal with people they know, because why talk to someone you are not trading with. Safety, security, dependable habits.
- **B3** (R, X, v13, v3, v2, v10). Three, control. Everyone around you is appeasing each other, so direct it toward you and get on top. Other people are aware that other people are aware, so relationships between others exist and a social situation can be stepped back from and worked. Seen in the mafia, sports teams, firemen, police, the military. "A friend of mine" means he works under me; "a friend of ours" means he is on our level. People stuck here commit crime, litter, and do not return the shopping cart, because other people not wanting a dirty lot does not exist for them, and they get angry when made to follow rules. The man who smokes in someone else's house says "it's cool" because his idea of where smoking is fine is the only idea in the room, and when told no he feels attacked. A woman who sees "men are bad" on her phone and then sees the man in her life. "It just is" and "you must" mean "I want." Self-protective and impulsive: do what you want, take what you want, get your way.
- **B4** (R, X, v13, v3, v2, v6). Four, conform or belong. Second person. You see yourself the way another person in your culture sees you. Walk a mile in his shoes. Children arriving here look in the mirror more. People get tired of the win-or-take-all world, pick a leader, and write things down: this is the way things are, not because I have power but because we wrote it. What I want could hurt others the way they hurt me, and rules fix that. Then this way is the one true way. Groups grow to include strangers, which makes nations, cities, agriculture. Rigid, resists new ideas: when Galileo said the earth is not the center, the church said you cannot have a different way, we just fixed that problem. People here show off their ability to submit to the rules. The move from three to four matters as much as the move from six to seven. A ten-year-old buys his mother flowers and soap; a five-year-old buys her a toy car because toy cars are cool. The father's rule now sounds like "do it because it is the way we do things." In one moral-development scale this is the good-boy stage and the law-and-order stage.
- **B5** (R, v13, v3, v1). Five, achieve. Third person. If I were a stranger, how would my culture look. Once life is stable people want to improve and advance. Cars, houses, money, fame. There is no one right way, they are all made up, so you can improve yours and need not fight other ways. Citizen of the world; whole-world consciousness starts here. Make your own way and succeed or fail on your achievements. A few hundred years old. Where science and the assembly line come from. The girl who has a thought about her father's thought about the thoughts of boys is here. The father's rule now sounds like "these are the consequences; it is up to you." The speaker calls this the minimum level to be an adult.
- **B6** (R, L, v13, v3, v1). Six, understand or include, the individualist. Fourth person. What is success, what am I getting out of life, who am I. No one part will satisfy me because I am different here and there and yesterday and today; infinity ways; how do I include them all. The floating feeling: I do not know what is behind any of these doors. Self-discovery. Anti-conformist, breaks rules to avoid being held back. Morals become I want to be who I want to be, not who you tell me, so everything must be included everywhere all the time. If I want to be who I want to be I must let you be who you want to be, which is why this level values inclusion and calls it empathy; a feminine level. A different perceiver gives a different perception; no fully objective observation; is my red your green. A few philosophers in the 1800s; mainstream in 1960s America. A very high degree of moral development. It hates the idea of levels and wants everyone equal; a test the speaker built shows a smooth bell curve with a dip at six because people there refuse the test. People here are smarter than average and have more money and power, so they are insulated from consequences. The speaker's own passage through it was stressful: too many ways to see the world, could not pick one, learned you never know until you try. He guesses six sits in the top 20% of IQ and says he does not have the data.
- **B7** (R, X, L, v13, v3, v1). Seven, harmonize. Fifth person. Second tier: the first level that understands and includes all earlier ones. Opens with "I am not my mind" (see the method bank). Fourth person extended through time: the self as a history; you remember seeing things this way, then this way; you see everyone else developing too. Everyone belongs where they fit, not anywhere. Interrelated systems that fit or clash. No more me; I am not telling anyone to think like me, but there is a place for them somewhere, hopefully not in Congress. People here recognize development on their own: a client answered a question by giving how he would have answered it at three earlier times in his life. No large group of them yet.
- **B8** (R, X, v13, v1). Eight, construct aware. Watching your own mind construct itself in the moment. The doughnut on a plate: hunger, guilt from a box eaten once, a remembered stomach ache, all pulling at once, and then do I want to listen. The hat: I notice my belief that this is a jerk hat and I do not need to engage with it; I can wait for new information and build a different picture. Which way of seeing things do I even pick. Understanding your own understanding. Rare; few people stable here.
- **B9** (R, L, v13, v1). Nine, complete. Sixth person. Watching the choosing. At the first three you are the main character; at four everyone is; then everyone watches a different show; at eight you watch the show without being it; at nine you watch yourself watch and what matters is how the looking is done and where it points. The question is the answer. The wise man on the mountain says obvious things because you forgot them and he did not. Accept, surrender, admit, detach, release, let go, which does not mean give up: I am at the part where I am trying. The kid who is not getting candy and says "that's disappointing, I'll sit with it." The speaker says he only steps in sometimes and hopes someone further along will correct him.

### C. Tests and tells

- **C1** (R, v1). The level test. If you disagree with someone, ask them to explain what you think and feel and why. If the answer is "you are evil and full of hate," you are dealing with an alarmingly low level of thinking: you do not think like me, so you do not matter.
- **C2** (R, X, v2). Egocentrism, red flag nine: thinking or acting from the lower three levels with no ability to move beyond them. The tell is "must" where the person means "I want," and no recognition of what the other person wants. A woman with six children by six men tells one father he must take all six to the beach or none; the siblings staying home is "not fair," with no thought of what is fair to him; "my son thinks his dad is the bad guy." The reply from second person: "I see what you want. I just do not want the same thing." If her way is right "because it just is," you are not the bad guy.
- **C3** (R, L, v2). The word narcissist is misused to mean selfish. Someone egocentric will call you egocentric when you do not give them what they want. The speaker at 22 set a boundary and was called controlling and abusive by the person crossing it. A person at five or six meets someone at three and mistakes non-conformity for high development.
- **C4** (R, X, v3). Levels cannot communicate. A Bitcoin enthusiast talks as if you know everything he knows. The higher you go, the less you assume everyone knows what you know. The five-year-old and the toy car.
- **C5** (R, v1). Understanding is not agreeing. A higher-level thinker asks why you think the opposite until they understand everyone, without conforming. Lower levels think listening is agreeing: "I don't want to hear it, then I might agree and be bad like you and lose my belonging points."
- **C6** (R, X, v11). A joke communicates something that should not be happening. It does not mean it will not happen. A woman's joke about hurting when a loyal admirer moves on runs through the levels: the need for validation (one), the feeling toward the man (two), knowing how to get the validation (three), knowing it is wrong and doing it anyway (four), constructing her own life (five), why do I choose this (six), the whole story of who she is (seven).
