# Four Quadrants Bank

Source bank for a page that does not exist yet, on the second map in the same playlist as the levels. Kinds: **R** reasoning, **X** the speaker's own example, **W** a claim about the world, **L** the speaker's own life.

**This bank produces facts. It does not draft prose.**

## The subject in plain words

Anything that happens to you can be looked at from four places. Your mind, what you think and feel. Your behavior, what you do. The culture, what the minds around you think. The system, what the world around you does. You control the first two and not the last two. An answer that uses only one box is almost always wrong.

## Claim ledger

- **Q1** (R, v13, v5, v6). The four boxes. Inside and outside, individual and collective. Your mind is the individual interior: what you think, know, feel, how smart you are. Your behavior is the individual exterior: the physical things you do. The culture or mass mind is the collective interior: what everyone around you thinks, the belief structures you live in. The system or society is the collective exterior: what everyone around you does and how the world works. The mind is the brain, culture is a lot of minds, society is what those minds do.
- **Q2** (R, v6). You control what is inside your mind and what you do. You cannot directly change the minds around you or the physical system you exist in.
- **Q3** (R, v6). Every answer has to include all four boxes or it is wrong. "Skill issue" means the problem is in your mind or your actions. "It's a systemic problem" means it is outside the individual. Answers that use only one box are almost always wrong because they leave out the other three.
- **Q4** (X, v3). A dream about Saturn is in the upper left, your mind. Saturn through a telescope is in the upper right, a thing in the world. The map holds anything you can know about.
- **Q5** (X, W, v5). A first date that lasted two minutes, walked step by step with a mark in each box for each person. Dating exists at all: culture, both. Apps have made dating worse: system, both. Profiles: she posted photos she knew did not show how she looks, a mark for her action and one for her mind, since omission is a lie by the culture's standard. Swiping: women swipe left a lot and men about half and half; women get almost twice the matches per thousand swipes; women match on 36% of their right swipes and men on 2%; the app puts everyone in a tough spot, a mark for the system for both, and a mark under culture for her for never thinking what the app is like for him. The match: he did not look closely at neck-up-only photos, a mark for his action. Getting ready: she wanted credit for makeup, which the culture expects and which guarantees nothing; a mark under culture and one under system for the online mindset that prices makeup at $392 a face, and two under her mind, entitlement and ignoring the effort he put in. The date: he was disappointed and said so about her weight; half a mark, action only, because feeling it is fine and saying it was half fine. After: she posted for sympathy, avoided responsibility, and framed it as "it did not go well" and "dating when you're fat sucks," in the third person, so the date did the bad thing to her; a mark in all four boxes for her. The speaker says he is mocking the concealment, not the weight.
- **Q6** (R, v5). The loop. A feeling becomes a post. The post goes into the system, the platform, whose algorithm favors that kind of post. The system presents it to the culture, the hive mind, which reinforces the feeling. The feeling makes more posts. Round and round, and each turn strengthens "girl right, guy wrong." Anyone who brings up the marks on the other side is put in the not-people zone, where they do not count.
- **Q7** (R, L, v6). Problems can originate in the culture or the system and affect you inwardly, and you use your mind and your behavior to deal with them. Telling a person their problem exists only in their mind ("own your situation") is never true; all four boxes have to be acknowledged. Immaturity is refusing to accept responsibility for your own actions. Maturity grows when you take responsibility for how you react to the world, not for what happens to you; the more you do, the more your inner guidance grows.
- **Q8** (R, v14). Levels of development take place in your mind, your behavior, your culture, and your society at the same time. Every large region has people at every level and a center of gravity where most people spend their mental time.
- **Q9** (W, v14). The West's center of gravity is moving into radical relativism, any way of thinking as good as any other. Good things came from recognizing unique subjective reality, but when any way is as good as any other, group norms break down, and centuries of development fall back to egocentric self-protection where others are means to an end.
- **Q10** (W, X, v6). The responsibility asymmetry. Humans tend to see a problem outside a woman as a man's fault and a problem outside a man as a man's fault. In staged street tests a man pretending to attack a woman draws help; a woman attacking a man draws bystanders who film. Men are told any problem that starts outside them is their responsibility; women are encouraged to believe their problems start outside them.
