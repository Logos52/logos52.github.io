# Decision Making

## Related pages

- [[wiki/Decision Making/Judging a Decision by Its Process|Good Decisions]]: the post-decision review, with six questions separating what was known then from what is known now, the resulting/hindsight failure table, and the control calibration. How much an outcome says about the process depends on how much of the outcome was controlled.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/Positional Decisions and Expected Value|Positional Decisions and Expected Value]]: choosing for a better future position instead of for the outcome, expectancy across repeated choices, and the downside-protection sequence.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/Expectancy in Wicked Environments|Expectancy in Wicked Environments]]: the expected-value arithmetic, and the three cuts that keep it usable when the win condition is undefined.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/The Uncertainty-Opportunity Tradeoff|The Uncertainty-Opportunity Tradeoff]]: why waiting for certainty gains comfort at the cost of opportunity, and the two-front sprint that converts delay into timed work.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/Changing Decisions|Changing Decisions]]: the difference between persistence and rigidity, the four-bias check, and the seven-step change review.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/Choice Throttling|Choice Throttling]]: the ordered chain of closed yes/no questions, and five ways of running it badly.
- [[wiki/Decision Making/Decisional Delays|Decisional Delays]]: the between-task gap, the week-long audit that finds it, and the planning output that removes it.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Mindset/Perfectionism and Overthinking|Perfectionism and Overthinking]]: the closed-loop model of overthinking and the full conversion from feeling to problem to action.
- [[wiki/Self Management/Flow State|Flow State]]: the state that the execution tier protects, with the interruption-cost model and the entry protocol.
- [[wiki/Self Management/Flow State|Focus Management: How to Enter & Recover Inside a Work Block]]: entry, drift-catching, and recovery inside one block.
- [[wiki/Self Management/Flow State|Attention Management: Preserving Flow]]: keeping attention on the work across transitions so that a new block does not have to start over.
- [[wiki/Self Management/Procrastination - a System Problem|Procrastination: a System Problem]]: what an unresolved "what next?" leads to, with the trigger-avoidance loop and the activation-energy fix.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation|Self-Regulation]]: the parent dimension for monitoring and adjusting in the moment.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Management|Self-Management]]: the parent dimension for systems that work without spending willpower.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Management/Kolbs Experiential Cycle|Kolbs Experiential Cycle]]: the four-step loop used for step 8, whose steps are act, observe, abstract, and test.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Mindset/Marginal Gains|Marginal Gains]]: picking the next small, high-probability, compounding improvement. Marginal Gains is used to choose the direction, and Kolbs is then used to carry it out.
- [[wiki/Red Team/Red Teaming|Red Teaming]]: a related stance that decision making overlaps with, covering surfacing assumptions, shifting perspective, and protecting dissent.
- [[wiki/Concepts/Suicidal Empathy|Suicidal Empathy]]: a named failure of downside protection, which is choosing based on one party's feelings without checking the second- and third-order effects.
