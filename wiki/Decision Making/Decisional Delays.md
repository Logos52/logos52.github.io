## Sources

- Mark, Gudith & Klocke 2008, CHI: 23 min 15 sec to return to an interrupted task after intervening tasks. Used only as a bound: that study measures resuming after other tasks, not a self-posed "what next?" after a task ends.
- Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006, implementation intentions: a specific next action reduces start friction. This supports writing the next action before the gap; it does not measure the gap itself.
