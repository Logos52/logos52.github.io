## Sources

- Arkes & Blumer 1985: sunk cost. People stay on a path because of what they have already spent on it.
- Samuelson & Zeckhauser 1988; Kahneman, Knetsch & Thaler 1991: status quo. People stay because the decision is already in motion.
- Kahneman & Tversky 1979: loss aversion. The typical laboratory coefficient is near 2:1. A 3–10× range is not what the study found.
- Duke, *Quit* (2022); CNBC 2023-06-11 interview: quitting can be the skilled move. Grit without an exit test keeps a person on a decision too long.
