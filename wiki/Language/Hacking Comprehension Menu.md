# Hacking Comprehension Menu

A menu of tools, techniques, and content choices can raise comprehension while immersing. The items fall into tools that sit on the media, techniques the learner applies, and choices about what to watch or read. In the list below, the language being learned is the target language, and the learner's first language is the native language.

## Tools on the media

Matching target-language subtitles make it easier to parse the words people are saying. A popup dictionary helps the learner look up words while immersing. Automatic pausing gives the time needed to read and understand. Blurred native-language translations let the learner check understanding and fill in gaps.

## Techniques

Priming is studying the language to gain extra information that raises comprehension during later immersion. Reducing speed gives more time to process the language. The speed should not go below 80 percent, or things get weird. Rewinding, in reading or in listening, gives more than one chance to understand something. Lookups of words, sentences, or grammar help explain unknown or confusing things during the session. Spoiling the plot means reading about the thing before consuming it, so the learner knows what will happen and can infer meaning from context.

## Content choices

Reuse known content, meaning things already consumed in the native language, or already used for interactive immersion or for freeflow, a later watch without lookups or rewinding. Content about topics already known well also helps, such as cooking, business, science, or fashion. Simple and simplified material is another option, including learner content, native content with simple plots or dialogue, or content aimed at children. Material with a lot of non-verbal information helps too, such as overdramatic acting, pictures, graphics, or visual diagrams.

## Open questions

- How does knowing what will happen change what a learner can infer from a scene that would otherwise be opaque?
- How does extra time to process a sentence change what a learner can catch?
