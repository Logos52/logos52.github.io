# YouTube Immersion Account

A second YouTube identity used only for the language being learned trains the feed toward that language instead of a mixed English life. The point is not more discipline on a shared account. Compelling content — material that would be watched even without a study goal — is what the feed is for.

## What the feed is for

**Target language first.** Clicks, searches, and subscriptions on this identity are in the language being learned. A mixed history mixes the signals; a separate watch-history keeps them apart.

**Dialect or writing system when it matters.** The feed can be trained toward a variety or a script. The page does not pick one.

**Beginner-friendly or visually rich, early.** Gist has to be reachable. [[wiki/Language/Hacking Comprehension Menu|Hacking Comprehension Menu]] is how to keep a found video in the zone.

**Compelling, and worth returning to.** Language immersion depends on repeated access to material that can hold attention. [[wiki/Language/Attention is Important|Attention is Important]] is why compelling content is not optional. Channels worth revisiting keep the account an immersion tool rather than a homework folder of learner videos nobody would watch anyway.

## How the identity is trained

**A YouTube identity that does not share history with the English one.** Create it or switch to it. The function is a separate watch-history. This year's menu name is not the instruction.

**Search in the language, then the catalogs.** [[wiki/Resources/Vietnamese Language Learning Resources|Vietnamese Language Learning Resources]] is the catalog for search and the dialect case. [[wiki/Resources/Mandarin Chinese Language Learning Resources|Mandarin Chinese Language Learning Resources]] is the catalog for search and the script/subtitle case. No channel list lives here.

**Seed, subscribe, refuse contamination, prune.** Watch enough to seed recommendations — "enough" is unmeasured. Subscribe to channels that are tolerable or actually compelling. Unrelated native-language content stays off this identity. Periodically click more of what works.

Vietnamese, as a worked case of dialect: tag channels Southern, Northern, mixed, or unknown when the difference is audible. No variety is assigned. Mandarin, as a worked case of writing system: tag a channel for simplified subtitles, traditional subtitles, audio-only listening, or character reading. Traditional is the vault target; the four uses travel without a personal memo.

A second identity is one more login, and a starved feed is worse than a mixed one for the first week. The price is a free identity plus the refusal to watch English on it. No paid YouTube feature is required. The quit signal: the account is being used for English, or it has become a homework folder. The checkable expectation: within a few sessions the homepage is mostly the target language, including something that would be watched anyway.

The trained feed is supply for [[wiki/Language/Noticing Game|Noticing Game]], [[wiki/Language/Freeflow Immersion|Freeflow Immersion]], and [[wiki/Language/Interactive Immersion|Interactive Immersion]] — what to do with the content once the feed is producing it, at volume, or with tools on. It is not a second study method.
