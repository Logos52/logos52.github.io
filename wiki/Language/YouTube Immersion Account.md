# YouTube Immersion Account

A YouTube immersion account is a separate YouTube account used only for videos in the language being learned. YouTube recommends videos from what an account has watched, searched for and subscribed to, so an account that only ever sees the target language soon has a home page full of it. That removes the daily work of finding something worth watching, which is where many learners stop.

## Takeaways

- Make a new account and use it only for the target language.
- Watch history drives the recommendations, so keep it clean.
- Seed it with playlists on topics you already enjoy.
- Pick one dialect and feed the account only that.
- Interesting videos make attention easier, and attention is required.

## How it works

The recommendation system learns fast from a new account with no history. Every video watched, every channel subscribed to and every search typed in the target language pulls the home page further toward that language. A single video in the learner's own language pulls it back. Because the account holds nothing else, the feed stays usable for months.

```
seed playlists --> watch --> recommendations
       ^                          |
       |____ subscribe, search ___|
```

- Set the account's language and location to the target language.
- Type every search in the target language.
- Subscribe to channels that hold your attention.
- Mark unwanted videos as not interesting to remove the topic.
- Never watch videos in another language on this account.

## How to seed it

A new account needs a few starting points. Playlists grouped by topic, such as cooking, films, comedy and news, give the system clear signals about what to recommend. The first topics should be ones the learner already enjoys in the native language, since interest is what keeps attention on a video that is only partly understood. Beginner channels made for learners work as seeds too.

- Topic playlists: cooking, films, comedy, news.
- Native channels: vlogs, web dramas, cartoons, travel, gaming.
- Beginner channels that speak slowly about simple things.
- Watch at least 10 minutes a day at first.

## Dialects and scripts

Some languages split into varieties that sound or look different, and a mixed feed makes early listening harder. The learner chooses one variety to focus on and seeds only that one. With no preference, the variety with the most content is the one to pick. For Vietnamese the choice is between northern and southern speech. For Mandarin it is between simplified and traditional characters, and the learner also notes whether subtitles are burned into the video or can be switched on and off.

- Vietnamese: pick northern or southern speech.
- Mandarin: pick simplified or traditional characters.
- Lookup tools can read switchable subtitles only.
