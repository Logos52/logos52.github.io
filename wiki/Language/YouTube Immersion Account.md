# YouTube Immersion Account

Immersing in content the learner finds interesting and compelling makes attention easier, because paying attention is easier when the content is enjoyable. That is why one setup step is to make a YouTube immersion account, then explore videos in the new language to find content that holds that attention.

Dialect choice comes next, because the account is only useful if the videos match the variety being learned. Choose content from the dialect being focused on. If no specific variety is the focus, choose the variety that offers the largest supply of videos. Keep playing the noticing game while watching, so the exploration is still high-attention work. The same session is also a time to try to figure out a learner type.

The time box keeps the search from turning into an endless setup task. Put in at least ten minutes of this exploration. If more time is available, do as much as feels right, without overdoing it. A session around this work is 20 minutes in total, with the other ten minutes going to the writing system, or to more time with the language once that system is comfortable.
