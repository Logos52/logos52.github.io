## Sources

- Schmidt, R. (1990). The role of consciousness in second language learning. *Applied Linguistics*, 11(2), 129–158. Conscious registration of a surface element as the condition for that element to become intake.
- Schmidt, R. (1995). Consciousness and foreign language learning: a tutorial on the role of attention and awareness in learning. In R. Schmidt (Ed.), *Attention and awareness in foreign language learning* (pp. 1–63). University of Hawai‘i Press.
- Tomlin, R. S., & Villa, V. (1994). Attention in cognitive science and second language acquisition. *Studies in Second Language Acquisition*, 16(2), 183–203. Detection, attention as registration, is the component that matters for acquisition.
- VanPatten, B. (1996/2004). *Input processing*. Learners process what they attend to; unattended exposure is not intake.
