# Three Pillars of Language Learning

## Related pages

- [[wiki/Language/Preparation|Preparation]]: the forms this mode takes, from sound and script work to tool setup, with the failure table for the week when preparation starts taking the input hours.
- [[wiki/Language/Interactive Immersion|Interactive Immersion]]: the activity list behind the tools-on setting, and the conditions that make a lookup-heavy session worth running at all.
- [[wiki/Language/Freeflow Immersion|Freeflow Immersion]]: the entry conditions for tools-off work, plus the short post-session note on comprehension, interest and focus.
- [[wiki/Language/Hacking Comprehension Menu|Hacking Comprehension Menu]]: the lists of levers for raising comprehension, sorted into tools, techniques and content, with the rule about changing one variable at a time.
- [[wiki/Language/Attention is Important|Attention is Important]]: what attention is for while comprehension is still low. The graded version of the passive-watching rule belongs there.
- [[wiki/Language/Refold Grammar Primers|Grammar primers]]: how a short, deliberately incomplete tour of common patterns is used to support recognition instead of as a rulebook.
- [[wiki/Language/Aim-Shoot-Skin for Language Learning|Aim-Shoot-Skin for Language Learning]]: the vault's other model of language study time. It bands comprehension differently and gives every lookup a destination.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation|Self-Regulation]]: the six-step monitoring loop that runs from intention through diagnosis to the next adjustment. Counting unknowns and balancing by bottleneck are one instance of that loop.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Mindset/Marginal Gains|Marginal Gains]]: the rate-limiter procedure. Balancing by bottleneck is the language-specific case of it. It includes a re-check every one to two weeks of which limiter is live.
