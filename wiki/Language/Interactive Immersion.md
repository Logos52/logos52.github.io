# Interactive Immersion

Interactive immersion is real language worked with tools while it sits between a lesson and background watching, used when the content is close enough to follow with help. Lookups stay limited. A stretch in which every sentence is a research project has left the mode.

## A category, not one activity

Interactive immersion is a category of activities, not a single activity. The members a session can actually run:

- [[wiki/Language/Noticing Game|Noticing Game]] with confirmation — the check that a noticed piece was really there is what makes the hunt interactive rather than freeflow.
- Intensive reading: close study of a short stretch of real text for both meaning and form.
- Intensive listening: the same audio more than once, with a transcript or subtitles.
- Writing down a short stretch by ear.
- Limited word, sentence, or grammar lookups.
- Searching for sentences that are almost understandable.
- Using subtitles and tools to confirm what was heard.

The default content shape is dual-channel: audio plus matching target-language subtitles. [[wiki/Language/Immersion Metalayers|Immersion Metalayers]] is the tool layer for pause, replay, lookup, and subtitle compare — a pointer, not a second review.

## The useful-difficulty zone

**Difficult enough to create learning, not so difficult that every sentence becomes a research project.**

A useful session usually has:

1. Some recognizable words or forms — [[wiki/Language/Preparation|Preparation]] is the priming that makes a few of those already available.
2. A reason to keep watching.
3. A limited lookup strategy.
4. A way to confirm meaning.
5. Enough attention to notice patterns.

## The stop

Every sentence is a research project → drop to [[wiki/Language/Hacking Comprehension Menu|Hacking Comprehension Menu]] or easier content. The name is house (intensive-plus-tools); intensive reading and listening are a real named mode. Dual-channel is a practitioner default, not a finding that it beats audio-only for everyone.

The mode does not apply when gist already lands without help — that is [[wiki/Language/Freeflow Immersion|Freeflow Immersion]]. It also does not apply when every line is research. The price is attention plus lookup time, paid on short stretches. Two sessions that were research projects are the quit signal. The checkable expectation is some recognizable forms, a reason to keep watching, and lookups that stay limited.

The same mode, read after the hour: help still unlocked the line, or the hour became a research project, or gist no longer needed help. Only the first reading is still this.
