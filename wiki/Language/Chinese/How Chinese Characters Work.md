# How Chinese Characters Work

The popular account treats each Chinese character as a picture of an idea that never needed to be spoken.

This page takes the opposite cut. The shape is there to trigger a spoken word — one form bound to that word's sound and meaning.

## What the parts do

That word is one item with three attributes — form, sound, meaning — and the pairwise links between them strengthen and decay on their own. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Three Attributes of a Character|Three Attributes of a Character]] opens the cluster on that contract: store one item, train three links, because a yes-or-no "do I know this character?" hides which edge has broken.

The written form itself comes apart. A **functional component** is a piece that does a job in this host: it pictures something, it contributes an abstract sense, it cues a range of syllables, it does two of those at once, or it does nothing. The first three are the ordinary analysis of the script. The fourth — leftover shape with no information about the word — is this cluster's extra role, and it is why [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Empty Components|Empty Components]] lands last: emptiness is a verdict after picture, sense, and sound have been ruled out.

The job is assigned per character, never as a global type of the shape. 立 contributes sense in 端, sound in 拉, and nothing in 音. Same strokes, three histories.

Once parts are read by the work they do here, the thousands of characters stop being arbitrary pictures. A learner can predict a meaning domain and a syllable range, rebuild a half-forgotten form from those two constraints, and throw out most folk glosses as derivable from the parts. The prediction is a domain and a range, not a reading. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Sound Components|Sound Components]] owns the single-character cycle that produces the range. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Form Components|Form Components]] owns the picture-contributing parts, depiction as origin even after the modern strokes have stopped looking like the thing. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Meaning Components|Meaning Components]] owns the sense-contributing parts, an abstract extension of a concrete original.

Rote copying spends the same hours at the stroke level, where none of that structure lives. [[wiki/Learning Craft/Rules of Effective Memorization|Rules of Effective Memorization]] is why functional decomposition pays and stroke-copying does not.

This page orients. The child pages carry the load. The 立 triple is the only worked case that stays here.

## Reading order and where this sits

Read the cluster in this order because each page needs a verdict the previous one taught how to make.

[[wiki/Language/Chinese/Three Attributes of a Character|Three Attributes of a Character]] first: one written word, three links that fail separately. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Surface vs Deep Structure|Surface vs Deep Structure]] is the stopping rule for decomposition — stop at the functional level, not at every stroke. Then the three working roles, in any order among themselves: form, meaning, sound. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Sound Series|Sound Series]] scales the sound side to families and related-initial groups. Empty Components comes after those, because a leftover is what remains when the other three jobs have been checked. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Meaning Trees and Original Meanings|Meaning Trees and Original Meanings]] is sense genealogies — the character-tree versus the component-tree. Practice comes last. The **IME Method** rebuilds a missing form by listing sound-side and meaning-side candidates and crossing them; [[wiki/Language/Chinese/The IME Method|The IME Method]] is that cross. **Pipelining** learns the spoken word first, both ways, then attaches the written form; [[wiki/Language/Chinese/The Pipelining Strategy|The Pipelining Strategy]] is that stagger. Rules of Effective Memorization sits under the whole cluster.

[[wiki/Language/Character Primer|Character Primer]] seeded the territory. This cluster replaces that sketch with a working model and keeps the same intake: real words, audio, popup dictionaries, traditional script.

Inside the three-mode immersion system, the cluster lives in [[wiki/Language/Preparation|Preparation]]: character study primes immersion, and immersion stays the main event. [[wiki/Language/Refold Language Learning System|Refold Language Learning System]] is that three-mode system. Pipelining anchors written forms to words already known by ear. IME guess-and-verify runs on real reading volume. Both assume immersion is already supplying spoken vocabulary and text.

The thousands stop being arbitrary once the jobs are readable. What that buys is a meaning domain and a syllable range, and a way back into a form that has slipped — not an exact modern reading, and not a substitute for the listening and reading already underway. Character study stays in service of words the ear already holds.
