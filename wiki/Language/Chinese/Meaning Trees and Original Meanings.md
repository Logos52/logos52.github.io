# Meaning Trees and Original Meanings

The original meaning of a character is the trunk of its meaning tree. It is the sole sense bound to the form, and it is the root that explains every later extension. A simple test for the basic modern sense is to ask what the character would mean if it were written on a whiteboard with no extra context.

Extensions can sit close to that trunk or farther away. Direct extensions are marked with a single arrow. Distant ones are marked with a double arrow. Sound-loan senses are marked so that no false bridge gets built from the form to that sense. About 18 percent of characters are sound loans. 我 pictured a weapon. 找 once meant "to paddle." 錢 in the sense of "money" is a loan path. Knowing the loan ahead of time helps the mind accept that the form and that sense do not match. Some old senses later moved to another character: 必 to 柲, 其 to 箕, 常 to 裳, and 文 to 紋.

## What to store

Do not memorize a sense that can already be derived from a more basic one. Rare senses can wait until they are actually met. The character 長 is the worked example of that pruning.

Two readings get two trees, with separate original-sense branches, as with 長 read cháng and 長 read zhǎng, or 看 read kàn and kān.

A character and the same shape used as a component keep two different trees. The senses of 又 as a word are not the same as its senses as a component, where it points at hand-actions. 日 as a component points at light or time. Using the component tree when the character tree is needed, or the other way around, means the analysis has already gone wrong. 游 is understood through 氵 as "actions involving liquids," not through "a body of water."
