# Three Attributes of a Character

A character for memory is one stored item whose form–sound, form–meaning, and sound–meaning links fail separately. Speech already carries sound and meaning. Writing adds form, and any two links can hold while the third is gone.

The script sits under the same contract as alphabetic writing: it records spoken words. The old Western habit of treating the glyphs as idea-symbols — the **ideographic myth** — fails here.

## One item, three links

For memory the character is one stored item with three bound attributes. Form–sound, form–meaning, and sound–meaning each strengthen or decay on their own. That independence is vault doctrine, not a field finding. Any two can hold while the third is gone, so a yes-or-no "do I know this character?" hides which edge has broken. Store one item. Train three links.

When depiction and sense differ, work out the bridge. That bridge is the content of the form-to-meaning link. 艸 coincides: the drawing is the plant. 大 draws a standing adult, and adults are big relative to children — the sense rides on the picture rather than repeating it.

Train today's Mandarin reading even though it is anachronistic. 艸 had an older pronunciation when it was carved. That reconstruction is illustration. It never enters a review card.

Two scheduling habits sit on top of the contract, also vault doctrine, not findings. **Ear-first anchoring** attaches a new glyph to a word already known by ear. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/The Pipelining Strategy|The Pipelining Strategy]] is that stagger at session scale: spoken word before form. **Per-link testing** runs separate checks for form→sound and form→meaning. Extra card volume versus diagnostic value is still open. If the split cards do not change which edge gets repaired, stop splitting them.

## 大 in three jobs

A character reused inside another brings all three attributes and can contribute through any one of them. Classify the contribution before explaining the host. 大 plays all three roles.

As a **form component** it contributes its picture. In 美, 大 supplies a person. The headdress has decayed toward 羊; meaning "big" and sound dà sit idle. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Form Components|Form Components]] owns the headdress analysis and the sheep reading as a folk error. This page names the person-plus-headdress parse and stops.

As a **meaning component** it contributes its sense. In 尖, 小 over 大 describes tapering to a point. The all-meaning-component pattern is a latecomer and never became common. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Meaning Components|Meaning Components]] is the sibling-falsifies test for stories hung on those parts.

As a **sound component**, original 大 in 達 cues dá. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Sound Components|Sound Components]] is the range-then-lookup cycle; 達 is this page's sound case, not that page's 棠.

Double duty is normal. The same 大 in 達 also depicts a person crossing 辶, giving "go through" and then "reach." Check every component for more than one function.

## What the list generates

Form components plus meaning components are **semantic components**: either part that feeds the host's meaning. **Empty components** contribute nothing. The top of 言 is leftover of a mark above a tongue; 口 does the form work. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Empty Components|Empty Components]] is the two routes into that leftover and the glass repair.

Early 達 showed a person walking a sheep across an intersection. 大 later degraded into 土, so the modern right side reads as 幸 and buries the structure. Form and Empty own the general corruption rule. The burial is this page's scene.

Component meanings split from character meanings. 水 as a word is a body of water; as 氵 it marks actions on liquids. 火 as a word runs to anger and firepower; as 火/灬 it marks light, heat, and fire actions. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Meaning Trees and Original Meanings|Meaning Trees and Original Meanings]] owns those two inventories.

Position can fix identity. Left 阝 is 阜, hills. Right 阝 is 邑, city.

Variant forms keep identity: 氵=水, 灬=火, 訁=言, 艹=艸, 人/亻/儿. 儿's two histories — component as a person versus standalone simplified 儿 as 兒 with the top deleted — belong on Form Components.

Three failure modes sit on the contract. The ideograph reflex explains a character purely as assembled ideas and drops the sound attribute. Meaning-component inflation defaults most semantic parts to meaning, or carves non-functional fragments and hangs stories on them. Meaning Components owns the test that kills those stories. Glyph-as-canonical-word treats writing as the real item and speech as a shadow; writing is durable storage for speech that vanished as it was uttered, which is why pre-literate cultures engineered memorability into the spoken line.

The three-link model is vault doctrine. It has not been tested as a named method. The price is three checks where one recognition card used to sit. The quit signal is the one already named: if the split does not change the repair, stop splitting. The checkable expectation is a character that can be read aloud and still not written, or written and still not read — a visible broken link.

[[wiki/Language/Chinese/How Chinese Characters Work|How Chinese Characters Work]] is the hub; this page opens that reading order. [[wiki/Language/Chinese/Surface vs Deep Structure|Surface vs Deep Structure]] is functional decomposition: where to stop cutting.

The yes-or-no question is retired. The three links are what get trained. Ear-first is how the form joins a word already held. Same item, now used.

## Open Questions

- Which of the three links fails most often is unanswered.
- The fallback when a character arrives through reading first — form before the spoken word — is the working tension with Pipelining.
- When modern readings start distorting sound-component judgments is a Sound Components question.
