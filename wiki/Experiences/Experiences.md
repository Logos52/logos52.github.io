# Experiences

Experiences is the section of this wiki for pages written from the owner's own time in particular places and trainings. Each page keeps what that time showed about how other people, other cultures and other public systems work, so that the observation can be used elsewhere in the wiki.

## What it holds

The section has two pages so far. One comes from a period of language study abroad. The other comes from a military training course in red teaming, which is the practice of arguing against a plan the way an opponent would. Both pages rest on the same skill, which is seeing a place or a problem the way the people inside it see it. The public journal keeps a version of the same section.

- Taiwan 2020: language study in Taiwan, and a side trip to Japan.
- Red Team Training: cultural empathy and breaking groupthink.
