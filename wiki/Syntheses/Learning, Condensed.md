# Learning, Condensed

Learning has two jobs: storing new material well the first time you meet it, and pulling it back out of memory later, often enough that it stays. Most study time goes on re-reading and re-watching, which do neither job well.

## Core takeaways

- How well you store material at first sets the limit on later recall.
- Re-reading and recognising feel like learning and store little.
- Guess an answer before you look it up.
- Connect each new idea to ideas you already hold.
- Test yourself without cues, then check against a real answer.
- A forgotten item needs storing again from a new angle.
- Using the material for real is the best review there is.

## How it works

Storing and recalling depend on each other. Each time you pull an idea back out, it gets stronger and better organised, and any gap you find shows what was stored badly. Better storing means fewer gaps and less review, though the review never drops to zero. The first study of a topic deserves calm and care, because a mistake stored then costs about four times as much to repair later.

```
first study --> recall later --> gap found
     ^                             |
     |                             v
     +----- store it again from a new angle
```

- Before the details, frame the whole topic. [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Prestudy|Prestudy]]
- Learn what a concept does before you learn its name.
- No link to anything you know means study the basics first.
- Group ideas by importance and draw how they relate. [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Mindmaps|Mindmaps]]
- Build your own notes, since copied notes only look finished.
- Recall on a loose schedule: same day, next day, week, month. [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval/Spaced Interleaved Retrieval|Spaced Interleaved Retrieval]]
- Nothing counts as learned until you have slept on it.
- Change the kind of question each time you review.
- Mix similar topics so you learn to tell them apart.
- After three misses on one item, study that item in depth.
- Judge a study method by its trend over several weeks. [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation/Measuring Learning|Measuring Learning]]
- Keep sessions small and fixed, with no choices to make inside them.
