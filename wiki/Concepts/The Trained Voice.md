# The Trained Voice

The trained voice is the way a language model writes when nobody has told it how, using habits it learned from the text it was trained on. Much of that text was written online to win attention, so the model learned the moves people make to look impressive, humble or honest. A person editing a model's drafts can predict those moves and cut them.

- A model's default style comes from the text it was trained on.
- Much web writing was shaped to win clicks and reactions.
- The model copies self-display along with the facts.
- It has no judgment of its own to put into the work.
- The result is smooth text that could be about anything.
- The fix is a person who edits the draft and cuts.

## How it works

Online writing is produced for an audience that rewards reaction. Being visible on a platform means performing, and the version of a writer that draws the most response is the one that gets repeated. A model trained on large amounts of web text picks up these habits as part of how English is written. It then reproduces them in any piece, whatever the subject, and the moves below are the ones that show up most.

- Signposting: telling the reader what is coming or what was said.
- Hedging and apology aimed at the writer's safety.
- Staged candour: a confession arranged to look honest.
- Rhythm and neat phrasing built to sound good.
- Sameness: many writers converging on one safe style.

## The two meanings of ego, applied

The word ego covers two different things: a person's judgment, which shows in what they select to keep and what they cut, and a wish to be admired. A model reproduces the second without having the first. It can copy a mention of credentials or a humble aside, but it holds no view of its own about which example matters. Its drafts therefore carry the self-display and lack the selection.

- Self-display is copied from the training text.
- Selection has to come from the person using the model.
- AI-assisted stories rated better singly and varied less as a group.

## What this desk does

On this desk, AI agents write the drafts and a person makes the final cut. The rules for that cut are kept in the writing standards, which list the moves that get struck and hold the record of past strikes. The reading of where these moves come from is this desk's own position. It rests on published work on self-presentation and on that record of strikes, and no audit of a model's training data was made.

- A draft line that fits any subject gets cut.
- A line that shows the author instead of the subject gets cut.
- Moves struck once are recorded so they are caught again.
