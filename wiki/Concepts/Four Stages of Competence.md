## Sources

- Burch, N. / Gordon Training International (1970s). Four stages for learning any new skill. The public model with the four stages.
- *Management of Training Programs*, New York University (c. 1960). Sometimes cited as an earlier public source for the same four stages.
- Fitts, P. M., & Posner, M. I. (1967). *Human Performance*. Cognitive, associative, autonomous: the related research model, the same movement in three phases, no claim of clean crossings.
- Kruger, J., & Dunning, D. (1999). Unskilled and unaware of it. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*. Relative ranking of the unskilled. Not a result about a confidence drop on entering CI.
- Gignac, G. E., & Zajenkowski, M. (2020). The Dunning-Kruger effect is (mostly) a statistical artefact. Later work narrowing the popular picture.
- Anderson, J. R. (1982). Acquisition of cognitive skill. Compilation and automaticity behind the last stage of the progression.
