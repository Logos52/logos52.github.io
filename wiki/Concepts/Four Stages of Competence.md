# Four Stages of Competence

A skill goes from wrong without knowing it, to wrong and aware, to right with effort, to right without thinking. The Four Stages of Competence name those waypoints, and each stage rewards a different kind of improvement. Most of the work sits inside the bands, and the next block is chosen by stage.

## The four waypoints

**Unconscious incompetence (UI)** is doing it wrong with no picture of what correct looks like. Often there is no sense that a correct even exists, so confidence comes cheap. Most skills sit here, including the skill of learning itself. School rarely hands working strategies. Habits assembled alone come with no standard to compare against. Cheap confidence plus no picture locks the stage shut.

A version of the same person ten years on would do this better, and that is already obvious. The gap is already visible. Unknowns are already known to exist. That is the only exit a lone reader has from UI. No coaching program is required to run it.

**Conscious incompetence (CI)** is seeing the wrong without yet being able to do the right. Errors become visible as patterns before they become fixable. This is the threshold where real improvement starts. Duration varies wildly. A simple mechanical fix can turn in minutes. An instrument or an athletic skill can sit here for years. Cognitive and learning skills under deliberate, well-sequenced training last longer than wanted and shorter than a musical instrument.

Entering CI usually drops confidence, because the mistakes are now visible. That felt drop is real. It is not a ranking law. [[Encoding and Retrieval]] is the dip as a memory fact. [[Confidence Calibration]] is how to read it and stay in the work.

**Conscious competence (CC)** is doing it correctly when effort is applied. The band is wide. At the bottom, two successes in ten arrive only when conditions align and concentration is total. At the top, nine in ten arrive with little effort. Those ratios are pictures of the width, not trial results. Crossing that width is most of the visible work of skill development. It takes many cycles of practice and reflection.

**Unconscious competence (UC)** is correct execution as the habit: minimal effort, high consistency, and doing it the old wrong way would itself now take effort. Attention is freed. Refinement, and any new technique, become affordable because the old one no longer occupies the bench. [[wiki/Concepts/Global Workspace and J-space|Global Workspace and J-space]] is the handoff: execution has left the deliberate workspace.

## The gain that belongs here

A gain that belongs to another stage produces frustration and no movement.

| Stage | The gain that belongs here |
|---|---|
| UI | A standard. See right versus wrong before execution improves |
| CI | Catch the mistakes. Surface as many distinct wrong ways as will show. Mistake count falls while the result is still not right |
| CC | Lower effort, raise consistency. Success rate and effort are the two dials |
| UC | Refine and personalize. Tune a working default into a version that fits, and build the wider system around it |

Under CI the gain is seeing mistakes, not getting the result yet. Outcome benefit arrives mostly at the top. Watching only outcomes is how this stage gets abandoned. Under CC the two dials move together: from two-in-ten with strain toward nine-in-ten with ease, as pictures of the same width. Under UC the working default is already running. The work is no longer "get it right." It is make this version yours, and make room around it.

## The work is the bands

Competence runs as a spectrum. Most of the work happens inside the bands rather than at the crossings. Reading that middle as failure is the usual mistake. The long stretch of CC, where it is right only under effort and only on some days, is the work. A gain that belongs to a different band — hunting fluency while the standard is still missing, or hunting a result while the mistakes are still being learned — is the wrong gain in this light. [[Metacognition as a Skill]] is the trainable skill of reading where the skill sits and sizing the next gain to that band.

## How many, and which diagnostic

A new technique is added only when the existing set is at least approaching unconscious competence. Five techniques all parked at low CC stack their effort costs into overload. Their low consistency drains motivation at the same time. [[Pacing Skill Development]] is the how-many-at-once rule this capacity limit sits inside.

Track skills with this model. Track knowledge with mastery levels. Mapping understanding onto competence stages muddles both diagnostics. Knowledge types and competence stages are different tools. [[Knowledge Mastery - From Recognition to Usable Knowledge]] owns the knowledge side. Do not map a fact onto UI.

[[Marginal Gains]] draws the path: the next improvement small enough to see, stacked. [[Kolbs Experiential Cycle]] is the engine that produces each gain: what happened, how it felt and why, what rule that suggests, what changes next time. The four stages say what kind of gain the path is made of. Zoom in on an improvement curve and it resolves into stacked cycles, each hunting the gain its stage rewards.

The automaticity signal in [[Rapid Skill Acquisition]] — execution speeding up without trying — marks the top of CC and the handoff into UC.

## A useful map, not natural kinds

The model is useful daily and is not scientifically robust for complex skill. No trial establishes the four boxes as discrete natural kinds. There is no psychometric validation the model needs. A subtly wrong automatic skill breaks the boxes: it looks like UC and is still UI in disguise. Automatic is a verdict on this version. It is not a verdict that the version is right.

The research cousin is a three-phase climb — cognitive, then associative, then autonomous — about the same movement, with no claim of clean crossings. It is a cousin, not a second model on this page. The months and the two-in-ten / nine-in-ten ratios are this system's teaching pictures, not findings. The popular ranking cartoon is not the name of the CI drop. The felt drop is real.

The quit signals are two. Adding a technique while every current one still takes full concentration. Reading a long middle as evidence the method failed. The checkable test: before a practice block, the stage and the gain that belongs to it can be named.

## This version, not done

Automatic is a verdict on this version, not a verdict that the version is right. The next block is still chosen by the stage the current version is in. A standard, a caught mistake, a lower-effort success, or a refinement of something already running — one of those, matched, is the hour.

## Sources
