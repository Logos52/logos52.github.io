# Cave Theory

## Sources

- Geary 2008, *Educating the Evolved Mind*; Geary & Xu 2022: school knowledge is evolutionarily novel and does not come for free through organic curiosity.
- Roussel et al. 2024, *Educational Psychology Review*: the timescales, including the 300,000-year figure (Eskelson 2020, cited therein). Writing is a few thousand years old, universal schooling a few hundred.
- Craik & Lockhart 1972; Anderson & Reder 1979: depth and connectedness of processing drive retention. The signal to keep a piece rests on how it was encoded, whatever its history.
- Dunlosky et al. 2013: elaborative interrogation and self-explanation rated moderate utility. Outside support for connection-building study.
- Kirschner, Sweller & Clark 2006: the limit Cave Theory respects. Minimally guided discovery underperforms. Cave Theory licenses attachment, never unguided exploration.
