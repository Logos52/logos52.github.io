# Are You Thinking, or Just Consuming

Thinking with material is comparing, predicting, explaining, retrieving, deciding, or building. The same visible act can be either, depending on what the head is doing. A highlight, a map, a watched video — none of those names the mode.

## What consumption looks like

Reading, taking notes, marking lines, watching a video, making cards, and drawing a map can all fail the moment they become a shallow routine. Highlighting and going over the text again are the usual pair. Cards fail when they only trigger recognition. A map fails when it is copied.

Passive work tends to look like this: consuming line by line; going over a text without changing its form; copying or marking without a judgment; following an explanation without generating one; measuring progress by pages, notes, or videos finished. That last tell is the best one. Coverage is a fake metric.

The common signal is low cognitive engagement. The fooling signal is ease. Consumption feels like thinking because it is easy and familiar. The hour can feel fluent and still leave little that can be used later.

For most people, passive is the default. Switching out of it is a choice.

[[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation|Self-Regulation]] is that choice.

## What working looks like

The useful cut is whether information is being manipulated into meaning. Active work tends to look like this: holding two ideas against each other; judging importance; generating analogies; teaching or preparing to teach; mapping how pieces relate; asking what problem the knowledge solves; testing recall in the setting where it will be needed.

The common signal is transformation. The form of the information changes, and it gets connected to something already known or to a use.

[[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing|Deep Processing]] owns how that transformation works.

What has to be watched is not which method is on the desk, but which thought pattern the method is causing.

[[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation/Metacognition - The Control Layer|Metacognition: The Control Layer]] is that watch.

A mind map made by copying nodes and arrows is consumption. A short note written after a hard comparison is thinking. The two sentences are the proof: same family of artifact, opposite cognition.

Retrieval is one of the six verbs.

[[wiki/Concepts/Memory Handling|Memory Handling]] owns that verb at full depth.

The six verbs are the checklist for the session. Compare, predict, explain, retrieve, decide, or build — if none of those fired, the hour was consumption, however finished the artifact looks.

Noticing the slip in the moment is a different skill.

[[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation/Building the Radar|Building the Radar]] is that skill.

Ease is the fool, not the tool. A mechanical map can be fluent busywork. The six verbs diagnose the hour.
