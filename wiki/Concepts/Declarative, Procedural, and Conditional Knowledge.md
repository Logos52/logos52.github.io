## Sources

- Anderson, J. R. (1982/1983). Acquisition of cognitive skill / *The Architecture of Cognition*. Declarative versus procedural stores; compilation can lose verbal access.
- Ryle, G. (1949). *The Concept of Mind*. Knowing-that and knowing-how.
- Paris, S. G., Lipson, M. Y., & Wixson, K. K. (1983). Becoming a strategic reader. *Contemporary Educational Psychology*. Declarative (what), procedural (how), conditional (when/why).
- Paris, S. G., & Jacobs, J. E. (1984). The benefits of informed instruction for children's reading awareness and comprehension skills. *Child Development*. Strategy instruction that treats the when/why as teachable: the bound on this page's subset stance.
- Shea, J. B., & Morgan, R. L. (1979). Contextual interference effects on the acquisition, retention, and transfer of a motor skill. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Learning and Memory*. Varied practice grows discrimination.
- Taylor, K., & Rohrer, D. (2010). The effects of interleaved practice. *Applied Cognitive Psychology*.
- Rohrer, D., Dedrick, R. F., & Stershic, S. (2015). Interleaved practice improves mathematics learning. *Journal of Educational Psychology*. Familiar blocked drills, collapse on mixed transfer.
- Driskell, J. E., Copper, C., & Moran, A. (1994). Does mental practice enhance performance? *Journal of Applied Psychology*. Mental practice can move procedure a little.
