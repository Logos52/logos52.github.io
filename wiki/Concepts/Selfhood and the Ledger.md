# Selfhood and the Ledger

## Sources

- Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom & Clark (2018), *Psychological Science* 29(11), 1742–1756: the liking gap.
- Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 78(2), 211–222: the spotlight effect; precursor Kleck & Strenta (1980).
- Savitsky & Gilovich (2003), *Journal of Experimental Social Psychology* 39(6), 618–625, with Gilovich, Savitsky & Medvec (1998), *JPSP* 75(2), 332–346: the illusion of transparency and the pre-speech debiasing result.
- Prentice & Miller (1993), *JPSP*: pluralistic ignorance on campus norms.
- Sparkman, Geiger & Weber (2022), *Nature Communications*: the policy-support survey (N = 6,119; 66–80% actual support vs 37–43% estimated).
- Bursztyn, González & Yanagizawa-Drott (2020): correcting misperceived norms changed behaviour.
- Stasser & Titus (1985), *JPSP* 48(6), 1467–1478: hidden profiles; meta-analysis in Mesmer-Magnus & DeChurch (2009).
- Hampton et al. (2014), "Social Media and the Spiral of Silence," Pew Research Center: the in-person versus platform figures (86% / 42%), the recruitment figure (0.3% of those unwilling in person), and the perceived-agreement multipliers (2.92× at work, 1.91× on the platform); N = 1,801, ±2.6.

Effect sizes reported in secondary summaries of several of these studies were left out deliberately: the citations are confirmed, the magnitudes were not re-verified, and only the direction of the findings is relied on.
