# Language Research

Language Research holds the pages that study how a language works, as opposed to how to learn one. So far it covers how foreign names and words are written in Chinese characters, which explains the tones and spellings a Mandarin learner meets in names, brands and games.

## What it holds

The section has one finished page and one research bank behind it. The page explains the subject for a general reader. The bank lists every fact on that page with its source and a note on how sure the fact is.

- [[wiki/Language Research/How Foreign Words Become Chinese|How Foreign Words Become Chinese]]: how names get characters and tones.
- [[wiki/Research/Transliteration Into Chinese Bank|Transliteration Into Chinese Bank]]: the sourced facts behind that page.

Pages on how to learn a language, such as study modes and grammar primers, sit under Language.
