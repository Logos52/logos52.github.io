# Language Research

Language Research is a home for facts about what a language is doing — spelling, sound, borrowing, orthography — banked with their confidence, including the claims that died, so the search is not rerun. The learning wing next door answers what a learner should do. This wing answers what the language is doing.

## What is in it

One note is in it. [[wiki/Research/Transliteration Into Chinese Bank|Transliteration Into Chinese (音譯)]] is the deposit: a foreign name rewritten one syllable at a time from a small pool of characters picked for sound, with tones that arrive whatever those characters already had. 可口可樂 is one worked example against the claim that transliterations come out flat. The same mechanism as a continuous read for a non-linguist lives on [[wiki/Language Research/How Foreign Words Become Chinese|How Foreign Words Become Chinese]].

The [[wiki/Language/Refold Language Learning System|language-learning wing]] has no front door of its own, so its members are listed here. Nothing moves. This wing links. That wing is a learner's wing: what to do, in what order, with what tool.

## What accumulates here

The wing exists so a research pass that cost real search time gets banked once and cited afterwards, with confidence intact and dead claims kept dead. One note is in it. The learning wing stays where it is. What accumulates here is the descriptive layer underneath. A hub that restates those children becomes a second, stale copy of them. A new deposit lands here only when it is a fact about what the language is doing, with confidence marked.

## Open Questions

No Chinese-language study of 音譯詞聲調 with Taiwan-specific data has been located. Solid loanword-tone findings are English-language and Mainland or general-Mandarin.

The Japanese-route mapping for one worked name is unsourced past aggregator sites. Syllable mapping is suggestive, not evidence.

[[wiki/Language/Vietnamese Grammar Primer|Vietnamese Grammar Primer]] is still draft, unchecked.

Transliteration is one borrowing mechanism. Calques, semantic loans, and graphic loans are uncovered. [[wiki/Language/The Han-Viet Bridge|The Hán-Việt Bridge]] is the nearest anchor.
