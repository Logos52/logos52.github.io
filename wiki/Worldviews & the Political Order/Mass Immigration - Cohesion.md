# Mass Immigration - Cohesion

Mass immigration here means large inflows of people from places whose way of life differs enough that most arrivals will not take on the local one. The question that settles the policy is whether an admitted person can support himself and will join the host society, and that question is decided one person at a time, at the point of admission.

## Core takeaways

- Select on the person: capacity, skill, self-sufficiency. Assimilation follows from selecting well.
- The wage and tax arguments are the half of the case that can be argued in public. Cohesion is the real concern.
- Cheap imported labour is cheap because the public pays the rest of the worker's upkeep. The employer keeps the saving.
- Aggregate numbers hide the split: total output rises with more people, output per person falls.
- The fiscal defense depends on who is admitted. Skilled arrivals pay in; low-skill and asylum arrivals cost, in every government study that separates them.
- Assimilation is a spectrum, and a group that does not assimilate is a lesser failure than one that lives off the host.
- Nobody publishes a measure of cohesion, so the wage and fiscal numbers are the only part of the case that can be checked.

## How the transfer works

- An employer hires a foreign worker at a low wage.
  - The worker accepts it because housing support, public services and roads cover the rest of his living.
  - The public pays that part, and pays for policing and repair of trust when the worker does not fit in.
- The employer books the saving as profit. A cost put on a third party is a negative externality, which free-market economics counts as a market failure.
- Worker leverage exists only when a worker can say no.
  - If wages rise, the employer imports more.
  - If workers demand better conditions, they are replaced.
- Wages fall, housing demand rises, service demand rises, the cost of keeping order rises.
- Add a minimum wage on top and the result is unemployment and dependency among locals.

```
employer pays:  low wage ─────────────┐
public pays:    housing, services,    ├─> worker's full cost
                policing, cohesion ───┘
employer keeps: the gap as profit
```

## The twelve defenses

| Defense | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Diversity is our strength | Unified cultures outperform fragmented ones; the useful diversity is of ideas |
| Nation of immigrants | Past US inflows came from nations that assimilated easily; the present ones do not |
| It cannot be stopped | Japan controls its borders with an aging population and a low birth rate |
| Jobs locals will not do | Only wages locals will not accept; the defense admits wage suppression is the goal |
| They pay more tax than they take | False for low-skill inflows, the majority in Europe |
| They commit fewer crimes | Excludes the second generation, ignores deportations, compares all natives to a self-selected subset |
| Higher GDP | Total rises, GDP per person falls |
| Saves the pension system | Delays the shortfall by decades and makes it larger |
| More entrepreneurial | Counts gig-app sign-ups as business owners |
| Moral obligation | A government's first duty is to its own citizens; refugees who can assimilate are a separate case |
| No human is illegal | Every nation has borders; nobody applies the slogan to his own house |
| Vital for technology | 80 percent of H-1B visas go to entry-level jobs, so the program mostly fills entry-level jobs |

## What the fiscal studies show

- Netherlands, 2023, all residents: labour migrants +125,000 euro over a lifetime; family migrants -275,000; asylum migrants -475,000.
- Denmark, Finance Ministry 2019: non-Western immigrants and their descendants cost 27bn DKK in that year; Western immigrants contributed 11bn.
- UK, Office for Budget Responsibility 2024: a low-wage migrant arriving at 25 costs 617,000 pounds by age 81; a high-wage one contributes over 1,000,000.
- US, National Academies 2016: an arrival without high school costs 117,000 dollars over 75 years; one with some college contributes 261,000 or more.
- Across 25 OECD countries the net total sits between -1 and +1 percent of GDP, so the sign depends on the mix.

The same studies support both sides, which is why the fiscal argument alone settles nothing. Each one turns positive when the arrival is skilled and negative when he is not, and that split is a selection result.

## What this desk holds

- Selection on the person is the criterion. When an inflow is too large to assess one by one, origin stands in as a guess about likely conduct and net contribution.
- This desk accepts employers hiring foreign workers. The objection is to the public covering part of the wage.
- Guilt by descent is rejected. The large unselected intake the United States admitted in the early 2020s is treated as a mistake of that period, to be reversed.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]] takes the wage, tax and crime points as settled here and asks what an admitted population does to the politics of the country that admitted it.
