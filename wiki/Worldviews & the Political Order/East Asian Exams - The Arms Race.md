- The owner's position, stated 2026-09-15: the exam system plagues East Asia and there is no good way to repair it from inside, so it would have to be replaced with something different and probably Western. Merit means personal ability rather than exam-taking. Exams may once have measured it. The system survives because it is how things have always been done. Removing it would take a change in the society and perhaps the end of the tradition. Judging people on evidence of exceptional ability is the better method, and the internet now makes that evidence visible for almost anyone.
