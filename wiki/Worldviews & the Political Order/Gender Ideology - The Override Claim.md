# Gender Ideology - The Override Claim

Gender ideology is the claim that a person's inner sense of being a man or a woman overrides the sex of the body. Ten arguments are made for it in public debate, and they differ a great deal in strength. Sorted by strength, one of them is worth debating, and the rest reach a wrong conclusion, fail on the facts, or work by putting pressure on the person who disagrees.

## Core takeaways

- An identity has to identify with something outside itself.
- "A woman is someone who identifies as a woman" is circular.
- Only adult autonomy holds, since an adult cannot be stopped without force.
- Others are equally free to decline to take part.
- Gender dysphoria is real, and surgery does not change sex.
- Most gender non-conforming children grow up identifying with their sex.
- Self-ID harms female athletes and women in shared bathrooms.

## The argument

The override claim says inner identity is more real than the sex of the body. The test is to ask what the identity identifies with. If it identifies with something outside itself, such as the body or a role tied to the body, the definition rests on the body's sex after all, and if it identifies with nothing outside itself, the definition is circular and says nothing. The only way out is to say the identity is a soul that has a sex, which is a religious position and should be argued as one. The claim belongs to a wider habit in which feelings are treated as more real than facts, and the same habit calls disagreement bigotry.

- Culture adds rules to the sexes.
- Culture grows out of biology.
- An imperfect match does not make culture and biology independent.

## The ranking

The ten arguments sort into four tiers, shown in the chart below. A coherent argument is worth debating. A flawed one starts from a true premise and reaches a wrong conclusion. A weak one fails when checked, and a manipulative one works by pressure.

```
coherent     | adult autonomy
flawed       | dysphoria is real
weak         | trans women are women
             | brain of the other sex
             | intersex proves a spectrum
             | children know their gender
manipulative | identity is primary
             | gender is a social construct
             | self-ID harms no one
             | living daughter or dead son (bottom)
```

## Coherent and flawed

An adult who wants to transition will, and cannot be stopped without force. People who want to keep their distance cannot be stopped without force either, and each side can find its own community. Wanting to be the other sex is a common dissatisfaction, like wanting to be smarter, and transition removes the advantages of the current sex without giving the life of the other. Gender dysphoria is a real, documented condition, and the disagreement is over how to treat it.

- Surgery removes one sex's features and imitates the other's.
- Satisfaction after surgery is low.
- The damage cannot be undone.
- Treating dysphoria as a psychological condition works reasonably well.
- It works especially well for the young, and leaves the body intact.

## Weak

These arguments fail on the facts. "Trans women are women" is a slogan, and asked what a woman is, it runs in a circle. Brain studies find overlap with the other sex in rare cases and never a full inversion, so at most a male brain has more feminine traits than average, with no guarantee of being trans. A child's certainty is a weak base for permanent medical steps, because most gender non-conforming children grow up identifying with their biological sex.

- Intersex conditions are rare.
- Most intersex people fit male or female.
- If biology set a spectrum, only intersex people could be non-binary.
- Most people who identify as trans have no intersex condition.
- Temporary confusion does not justify permanent medical steps.

## Manipulative

These arguments work by raising the cost of disagreeing. "Identity is primary" drops evidence in favour of feeling and needs a soul with a sex to work. "Gender is a social construct" uses the fact that culture adds rules to the sexes, and culture still grows out of biology. "Self-ID harms no one" asks for empathy for one group and drops it for the female athlete who loses to a male and the woman who shares a bathroom with one.

- "A living daughter or a dead son" sits at the bottom.
- It frames disagreement as killing a child.
- It puts fear on parents.
- Outcomes stay poor with or without intervention.
