# Gender Ideology - The Override Claim

Gender ideology is the claim that a person's inner sense of being a man or a woman overrides the sex of their body. The defenses of that claim that come up in public debate differ a lot in strength, and sorting them shows which ones need an answer and which ones only put pressure on the person who disagrees.

## Core takeaways

- An identity has to identify with something outside itself. "A woman is someone who identifies as a woman" is circular and says nothing.
- The only defense that holds: an adult who wants to transition cannot be stopped without force. Others are equally free to decline to take part.
- Gender dysphoria is a real, documented condition. The dispute is about treatment. Surgery removes the features of one sex and imitates the other; it does not change sex.
- Most gender non-conforming children grow up identifying with their biological sex, so a child's certainty is a weak base for permanent medical steps.
- "Self-ID harms no one" fails on the female athlete who loses to a male and the woman who shares a bathroom with one.
- "A living daughter or a dead son" puts fear on parents. Outcomes stay poor with or without intervention.
- The override claim belongs to a wider habit: treating what a person feels as more real than what is the case, and calling disagreement bigotry.

## The argument

- The claim: inner identity is more real than the body's sex.
- Test it by asking what the identity identifies with.
  - If it identifies with something outside itself, such as the body or a role tied to the body, biology is back in charge.
  - If it identifies with nothing outside itself, the definition is circular and the claim has no content.
- The way out is to say the identity is a soul that has a sex. A soul with a sex is a religious position and should be argued as one.
- Culture does add rules to the sexes, and culture grows out of biology. An imperfect match between culture and biology does not make them independent.

## The ranking

Four tiers. Coherent: worth debating. Flawed: true premise, wrong conclusion. Weak: fails when checked. Manipulative: works by pressure. The ten defenses, by tier:

```
coherent     | adult autonomy
flawed       | dysphoria is real
weak         | trans women are women
             | brain of the other sex
             | intersex proves a spectrum
             | children know their gender
manipulative | identity is primary
             | gender is a social construct
             | self-ID harms no one
             | living daughter or dead son (bottom)
```

- Adult autonomy, coherent.
  - An adult who wants to transition will, and cannot be stopped without force.
  - People who want to keep their distance also cannot be stopped without force. Each side can find its own community.
  - Wanting to be the other sex is an ordinary dissatisfaction, like wanting to be smarter. Transition removes the advantages of the current sex and does not give the life of the other.
- Dysphoria is real, flawed.
  - The suffering is real. Satisfaction after surgery is low, and the damage cannot be undone.
  - Treating it as a psychological condition works reasonably well, especially for the young, and leaves the body intact.
- Trans women are women, weak. A slogan. Asked what a woman is, the answer runs in a circle.
- Brain of the other sex, weak. Studies find overlap in rare cases and never a full inversion; at most a male brain with more feminine traits than average, and no guarantee of being trans.
- Intersex proves a spectrum, weak.
  - Intersex conditions are rare, and most intersex people fit male or female.
  - If biology set a spectrum, only intersex people could be non-binary. Most people who identify as trans have no intersex condition.
- Children know their gender, weak. Most gender non-conforming children grow up identifying with their biological sex. Temporary confusion does not justify permanent medical steps.
- Identity is primary, manipulative. Drops evidence in favour of feeling, and needs a soul with a sex to work.
- Gender is a social construct, manipulative. Culture does add rules to the sexes. It does not float free of biology.
- Self-ID harms no one, manipulative. Asks for empathy for one group and drops it for the athlete and the woman in the bathroom.
- Living daughter or dead son, bottom of manipulative. Frames disagreement as killing a child. Outcomes stay poor with or without intervention.
