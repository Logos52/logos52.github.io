# Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference

The disparity inference is the step from "groups differ in outcomes" to "so discrimination caused it." The Basic Logic video [Racial Egalitarianism: All Arguments Ranked & Debunked](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVZ2I4lzUlI&t=47s) argues that the step only works if all racial groups are the same underneath, a belief it calls racial egalitarianism. It then ranks six common defenses of that belief on a tier list. The video says it deals only with group averages, so individual exceptions in any group do not change the argument.

## Two answers the video rejects

The video sets the debate up as two camps. Mainstream leftism, as the video describes it, treats all groups and cultures as equal, so any gap in income or crime can only come from discrimination. The mainstream right answers that culture explains the gaps, which implies any group could adopt a culture that worked elsewhere and assimilate anywhere. The video calls both views nonsensical, because discrimination is one factor among several and a culture is produced by the people who sustain it. Its own question is why studies of group gaps do not look at the groups themselves, and its answer is that racial egalitarianism has become a sacred dogma.

## The six defenses

1. "Intelligence is not genetically linked." The video says twin and adoption studies show that intelligence is heritable, and says anyone who accepts evolution must allow that some groups selected for it more. Its example is the Border Collie and the Chihuahua, one species with very different trainability.
2. "Humans are genetically 99.9% identical." The video answers that a share of shared DNA does not measure how much the differences matter, since people share half their genes with a banana and 90% of their DNA sequence with a cat. It adds that gray wolves are 99.9% identical to dogs, having split only 20,000 to 30,000 years ago, and nobody brings a wolf to the dog park.
3. *There is more variation within groups than between them.* The video accepts the premise but says not all genes are equal, because a few matter so much that whole groups carry them. Its examples are the Duffy negative mutation, which it says is found in 95 to 100% of West and Central Africans and protects against malaria, the Tibetan EPAS1 gene for surviving at altitude, and the ABCC11 gene that it says leaves 80 to 95% of East Asians without sweat odor.
4. "Race is a social construct." The video calls this wordplay, because people build named concepts around real things like the sun and molecules, and naming something does not make it made up.
5. "IQ tests are unreliable." The video grants that IQ has issues but calls it the best predictor of success available, stronger than being born rich. If IQ only predicts success in the West, it asks, why do people insist on moving to Western countries, and it answers that no society exists where high IQ is not an advantage.
6. "Culture explains all disparities." The video calls this the favorite answer of people who reject the discrimination story but fear being called racist. It argues that genes came before culture, that the two feed back on each other, and that a culture working for one people does not mean it works for all.

## What the video concludes

The video's conclusion is that groups adapted to their own circumstances. Expecting a people moved to another part of the world to integrate and perform at identical levels therefore rests, in its view, on blank slate ideology, which it says survives only through fear of the racist label.
