# The Woke Mind Virus

# The Woke Mind Virus

Young men on the political left report worse mental health than young men on the right, in every American survey that asks. Woke ideology is a large part of the cause. It teaches a young man to read everyday pain as illness, and a man taught to call himself sick starts to live like a patient.

- Liberal young men report depression about twice as often as conservatives.
- The young men furthest left are the worst off of anyone.
- The ideology teaches that words are violence and setbacks are oppression.
- Therapy teaches the opposite: thoughts are not facts, people are not fragile.
- Boys who transitioned young show the harm most sharply.
- No study yet follows politics first and health later.

## The gap

Every dataset that splits young people by politics shows the gap. It is largest among women and clear among men. It shows up in doctors' diagnoses, in how people rate their own health and on symptom scales. It also rises step by step from the most conservative group to the most liberal.

| Measure | Liberal | Conservative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnosed, white men 18 to 29 (2020) | 34% | 16% |
| Diagnosed, white women 18 to 29 (2020) | 56% | 27% |
| Self-rated health, men 18 to 29, out of 100 | 46.9 | 59.3 |
| Depression score, high-school boys, 1 to 5 | 2.06 | 1.89 |

- High-school boys who call themselves radical score 2.50.
- That is worse than every liberal group of either sex.
- The gap holds among people who never go to church.

## How the belief does it

The ideology hands a young man a set of words for his own life. Hurtful words count as violence, a setback counts as oppression, and a bad month counts as a disorder. Once he accepts those labels he acts on them: he seeks treatment, avoids challenge and expects harm. Cognitive therapy works by teaching the opposite habits, so the belief trains a young man in the thinking that therapy exists to undo.

- A label changes how a man treats his own bad days.
- The men deepest in the politics carry the most labels.
- They also score worst on every scale.

## What the labels show

Asked to count his bad days in the past month, a liberal young man gives about the same number as a conservative, 4.07 against 4.13. Asked whether a doctor has diagnosed him, the liberal says yes about twice as often. In one randomised survey of 1,000 people, asking about "mental health" put conservatives 11 points ahead, and asking about "overall mood" erased the gap. The two men put different labels on the same days, and the labels come from the belief.

- Among young women the bad-days count also differs, 8.31 against 5.56.
- An adjustment for stigma explained almost none of the gap.

## The boys who transitioned

Gender transition in boys is the sharpest form of the harm. England's national review of youth gender services, the Cass Review of 2024, found that the treatment was designed for young boys and is now mostly given to girls. It found no reliable evidence that hormones reduce suicide. In the strongest study run to show a benefit, 315 young people on hormones for two years, depression fell in the girls by birth and did not fall in the boys by birth.

- All 12 Danish transgender suicides, 1980 to 2021, were males by birth.
- That was 4.5 times the rate for men in general.
- Males by birth stopping treatment: 4% Netherlands, 10.5% Finland, 19% US military.

## What is still open

Three gaps remain in the research. No one has measured young men's politics first and their health years later. No one has checked whether the depression scales mean the same thing to a liberal as to a conservative. No one has measured belief and transition in the same people, so the route from belief to transition to damage is unmeasured.

- Finnish survey, 5,878 people: woke attitudes predict no better than leftism.
- In 1989 to 1994, liberal boys already ranked worst of all groups.
- One survey series with young-men figures dropped its mental-health question in 2024.

The open questions concern the cause, and the gap itself is real in every dataset anyone has checked.
