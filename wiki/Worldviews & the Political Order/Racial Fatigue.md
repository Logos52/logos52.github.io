# Racial Fatigue

Racial fatigue, often called black fatigue, is the weariness many people feel toward the culture of permanent grievance in black American public life, and it is felt by people who were never victims of a crime and by people on other continents. It comes from an argument in which every problem is traced to white racism and which no fact or concession can end. That argument grew out of a chain that runs through welfare, the family and crime.

## Core takeaways

- Welfare after the 1960s paid more when no father was at home.
- In the 1940s about 78% of black children lived with both parents.
- Today fewer than half do.
- Black homicide rates nearly doubled in the 1960s and stay high.
- The welfare was justified as a debt owed by white Americans.
- Blaming everything on racism leaves no fact that could count against it.
- The fatigue is a response to fatherlessness, not to black people.

## The chain

The War on Poverty in the 1960s expanded federal welfare, and who qualified depended largely on whether a father was present. A single mother could receive more than a low-wage husband earned, so the rule paid families to lose the father. Fatherlessness is causally linked to crime, and crime rose. The public paying for the welfare was told it owed the money because past injustice had caused the poverty.

```
welfare paid only without a father
            |
fathers leave the home
            |
boys grow up fatherless -> more crime
            |
welfare defended as a white debt
            |
every cause traced to racism -> fatigue
```

## The figures

Black Americans in the 1940s lived under legal segregation and far higher poverty, and their families were more intact than today. The collapse of the black family started in the same years as the new welfare. Homicide victims among black men are used as a stand-in for offenders, because a body is counted whether or not anyone is charged. That rate rose while crime fell across most of the world.

- 1940s: about 15% of black children born to unmarried mothers.
- Today: 70 to 78%, among the highest rates of fatherlessness worldwide.
- Black male homicide victims per 100,000: 42 around 1960.
- Nearly double by 1970, a later peak of 278, about 50 now.
- Black Americans are about 13% of the population.
- Black offenders killed 55% of about 13,000 murder victims in a year.
- Every other group has more murder victims than murderers.
- Black Americans are the one exception.
- In New Orleans about 90% of black victims were killed by black offenders.

## The usual replies

Antonio Talks, a black American YouTuber, answered this chain with a string of objections. Most of them divide wrongly, count raw totals from groups of different sizes, or change the subject. The argument itself is Thomas Sowell's, a very well-known black American economist, so the objection that its presenter is a foreigner does not reach it.

| Reply | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Whites are most food-stamp users | Whites are the majority, and black Americans use them 3 times as often |
| Crime shares come from arrests | Murder figures count victims, so arrests play no part |
| Only 0.035% of black men murder | That is per year, and over a 72-year life it is about 2.5% |
| Lightning is a bigger risk | The same per-year error |
| Whites commit 69% of crime | Arrest data, which the same reply had already rejected |
| White men commit most child abuse crime | A different crime, so it answers nothing |

- Police killings of black men are far rarer than black homicide.
- Those killings still set off national riots.
- Even 1% of men being murderers would wreck a society.

## The warrant

Welfare paid by people who do not receive it needs a warrant, a reason that justifies taking their money, and the reason given was restitution for slavery, segregation and discrimination. That makes the payments depend on the cause lying outside the group. Every internal cause admitted, such as family breakdown, weakens the claim on the money, so the claim gives its holders a reason to reject internal explanations whatever the facts show. The list of what counts as racism grows until it covers everything.

- Street names and schools named after slave owners and Confederates.
- Suburbs, redlining and homeowner associations.
- Tipping at restaurants.
- An account that covers all of these fits any possible world.
- Antonio Talks conceded the murder rate, fatherlessness and the welfare rule.
- He still called the argument racist and its presenter an outsider.
- The fix available now is to end the conditional welfare.
- He wanted the welfare made unconditional instead.

## Why it wears people out

The demand has two features: no evidence reduces it and no agreement satisfies it. A listener can accept it, keep arguing on the demand's schedule, or stop listening. Fatigue is the third response, and it reaches people far from any crime because an argument travels while events stay local. The tie from a fatherless upbringing to a group's habit of blaming others rests on an analogy with no figure attached.

- A boy with no father to hold him to account looks outward.
- A whole group raised that way blames others by habit.
- The fatigue is with that habit, which came from societal fatherlessness.
