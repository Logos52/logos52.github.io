# Who Gets Called Racist

# Who Gets Called Racist

Being called racist is used to end an argument without answering it. The word shifts the question from whether a statement is true to whether the speaker is allowed to make it, and it is applied almost only to whites. A judgment about a group that is accurate and used in the right conditions is a tool for staying safe.

- A group judgment used well saves lives.
- Use one only when there is no time to judge the individual.
- It must be accurate, which means comparing rates per head.
- Denying the charge or returning it both accept its terms.
- Leave the label alone and ask whether the facts are wrong.
- Black Americans show the strongest preference for their own race.
- Nobody can name a current law that disadvantages black Americans.

## The seat case

A passenger boarding a train at night has a second to choose where to sit. With full information about each stranger he could judge them one by one, and he does not have it. Iryna Zarutska was stabbed to death on a Charlotte train in 2025 by a stranger she sat near. A stereotype is a tool: used in the wrong place it hides the individual from the person using it, and a person who refuses it altogether cannot act on what he knows.

- Split-second choices justify group judgment.
- So do decisions about large numbers, such as immigration policy.
- With time to talk to a person, judge the person.

## Getting it right

A group judgment is useful only if it is accurate. Made-up beliefs about a group are plain hatred and useless for safety. Accuracy starts with dividing counts by population, so that groups of different size can be compared. The next step is learning why a group behaves as it does, through its culture and history, and testing those explanations before reaching for a genetic one.

```
group A: 44% of murders, 87% of population
group B: 56% of murders, 13% of population
per head, group B's rate is about 8 times A's
```

- A genetic cause is confirmed only by ruling out the others.
- Knowing the causes also helps anyone trying to help the group.

## Answering the charge

There are three ways to answer the charge. Denying it fails, because the accuser redefines the word to cover colour-blindness and good intentions. Accusing him back fails too, because that is an argument over a word whose dictionary he controls. The third answer sets the label aside and returns to the facts, asking whether the crime statistics are wrong and how mass immigration is working out.

- A moral accusation is a way to avoid a rebuttal.
- The word has been used so often it has lost its force.

## The other direction

The label is rarely applied the other way. Studies of how strongly each group in the United States favours its own race find black Americans the highest and whites the lowest. Old grievances such as slavery and the Jim Crow segregation laws usually fade across generations, and other groups that faced hard discrimination, the Irish, the Italians and Asians, moved on. Anti-white hostility among black Americans grows because schools and media teach it, and because it pays, through donations, diversity, equity and inclusion programmes, and demands for reparations.

- Black Americans commit more interracial killings than they suffer.
- The 2020 riots caused billions in damage.
- Corporations then gave hundreds of millions to the groups behind them.
- A standing challenge: name one current law that disadvantages black Americans.
- An unequal outcome, such as prison rates, is not an answer.
