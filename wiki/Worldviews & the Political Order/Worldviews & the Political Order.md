# Worldviews & the Political Order

People argue over whether democracy is sacred, whether a gap between races proves racism, whether a man who feels he is a woman should count as one, and whether immigrants caused the end of the woke movement. Each page linked below takes one belief of that kind and tests whether the belief is true.

Read [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Validity and Truth|Validity and Truth]] before the others. That page is how the checking is done. A belief can rest on true facts and still be wrong. A reader who sees how that happens can run the same check on beliefs these pages do not cover. The writer's position on each belief is on [[personal/2026-08-17-positions|Positions]].

The same checks, gathered in one place and pointed at no particular belief, are on [[wiki/Argument Validation/Argument Validation|Argument Validation]]. Those tests work on any argument: whether the conclusion follows from the facts, whether a total is being used where a rate is meant, whether a loaded word is doing the persuading, and whether the reader's own judgment has ever been tested.

## Rule and belonging

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Democracy as Sacred Cow|Democracy as Sacred Cow]]: An election shows which side would win a fight, and the fight does not have to take place, so the side that would have lost accepts the result. That only holds while the vote count matches which side is actually stronger. It fails when an armed and motivated minority keeps losing elections to a majority that cares little about the result.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]]: Socialists name a small number of people owning too much, workers paid less than what their work produces, and medicine that costs too much, and they want a system without those faults. The faults are real. They come from the state and the market being joined together, so a policy that makes the state larger also makes the cause of the faults larger.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]]: A small group always ends up governing a country, including a country that chooses its government by voting. Governing is full-time work that most people cannot do, so most people leave it to the few who are able to act together, and those few keep the position by stopping everyone else from organizing.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]]: A country stays united only when the people it admits adopt its way of life. Whether they adopt it depends on which people are admitted and how many are admitted. What is done after they arrive does not decide it.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]]: The left dropped its support for pronoun rules and for men competing in women's sport. Some people say immigrants whom the left itself admitted forced the change, because those immigrants reject both positions and their votes now decide elections. The immigrants did not force it. Courts and state laws ended those positions, and the immigrant vote is small and split between the parties.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]]: The country gave something up when it banned refusing to serve a person because of that person's race. The ban ended legal segregation, which was worth doing. Everyone also lost the right to refuse to do business with anyone for any reason. After that, the only safe way left for an employer to show that he does not discriminate is to keep a workforce whose mix by race and by sex matches the mix in the population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response|What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response]]: When one country attacks another, the attacked country usually answers with an attack of the same size as the one it suffered, even when it could do more damage. Matching the size is not a moral rule. A larger response would lose the country its allies, would spend the biggest threat it still had in reserve, and would lead the enemy to treat the country's own captured soldiers the same way.

## Peoples and inheritance

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]]: The gap between black and white Americans in earnings, in schooling, and in arrests does not by itself prove discrimination. It would prove discrimination only if the two groups were alike in everything else that affects earnings, schooling, and arrests. Nobody has shown that they are alike in those things.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]]: People alive now are said to owe a debt for things their ancestors did. That debt cannot be handed down, because guilt comes from a person's own actions, and a person's actions cannot be inherited.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]]: Europe is blamed for slavery, conquest, and mass killing, and it does not deserve that blame as if those were its own. Every civilisation did those things. The one thing Europe did that no other civilisation did was to end slavery, and the people who blame Europe leave that out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Europe - The Slow Agony|Europe - The Slow Agony]]: In 2008 the European Union's economy was larger than America's. By 2025 it was ten trillion dollars smaller. The causes were a single currency set up in a way that its own economic theory warned against, a financial crisis answered with spending cuts, reliance on Russia for energy, company founders leaving for one market instead of staying in 27, asylum instead of skilled immigration, the lowest number of births ever recorded, and ten fewer weeks of work a year. A plan for recovery has been written down. One of every nine of its recommendations was carried out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Fatigue|Racial Fatigue]]: People everywhere, including people who live far from America, have grown tired of black Americans' grievances against the country. Nothing that anyone says or concedes ever ends the discussion, so the only option left is to stop listening. People who never had any part in the matter become tired of it as well.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]]: British police, social services, and prosecutors kept almost no record of the gangs that abused girls over twenty years. Anyone who said publicly that the men were mostly Pakistani Muslims lost his job. The failure to protect the children was shared by everyone at once, so no single person could be blamed for it, and keeping quiet was in each person's own interest.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]]: Words like shame and guilt keep a society cohesive. They affect people who still feel shame and guilt, whoever says the words. They have no effect on people who no longer feel shame or guilt.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]]: The page asks whether diversity programs do what they are promoted as doing. The companies that dropped their programs did not lose any money. Whether the programs correct past wrongs depends on whether a gap between a company's black and white workers proves that the company caused the gap. That is assumed without being shown.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim|Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim]]: The claim is that peoples differ enough genetically to build different kinds of society. It is set out in three versions, and none of them names a people. It depends on one measure of genetic distance, on one comparison from an unnamed source that ranks genes for the nervous system among the genes that differ most, and on one sentence that treats a difference in the nervous system as a difference in the kind of society. The steps needed to check the claim are written down. No position is held on it yet.

## Gender and family

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]]: Gender is not an ideology that a person's choice can override. People can do whatever they want in private. Forcing other people to take part in a self-delusion is awkward, and it takes away those other people's own choice. That alienates normal people. I am generally for letting people do what they want. I will not be forced to enable those choices.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]]: Letting people live as they choose does not oblige anyone to celebrate how they live. What adults do in private is nobody else's business, and that is a good reason to leave them alone. That reason does not settle what schools should teach or what anyone else must say.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]]: A school day spent sitting still did something different to boys and to girls. Sitting still suits girls, and boys do badly under it. The girls saw the boys do badly and concluded that boys are less able. The boys concluded that school, and every institution organized the way school is organized, is against them. Each sex grew up with a false belief about the other.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Mind Virus|The Woke Mind Virus]]: Young men on the left report worse mental health than young men on the right in every survey that asks. The position taken is that holding the ideology causes the harm, because a man who holds it learns that words are violence, that setbacks are oppression, and that his pain is an illness with a name.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband|Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband]]: Giving women the vote made the state grow. The state can provide for a woman and protect her, which is what a husband once did, so a woman without a husband has reason to vote for a larger state. Nothing can take the place of a wife, so the state gives men no matching reason, and the effect goes in one direction only.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Gen Z Gender War - The Split|The Gen Z Gender War - The Split]]: Young women and young men in every rich country have grown further apart in what they believe, whom they vote for, and whether they form couples. Women take most of the degrees and most of the student debt. Men leave school to work in the trades or to do nothing. On dating apps a few men get most of the interest and the women become exhausted. The content each sex sees on its phone says that the other sex is the problem.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry|Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry]]: American courts forced every large men-only club to admit women by treating it as a business. The same laws exempted women-only colleges, gyms, and a billion-dollar executive club, under exceptions for privacy, safety, and religion that do not apply to those women's spaces. The men who lost their lodges were given nothing to replace them. Among those men the results now are fewer friends, fewer jobs, fewer marriages, and more suicides.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Late Stage Feminism|Late Stage Feminism]]: A mother who murdered her three children was given more than a million dollars by supporters. A woman who carried six children out of a fire got a small fraction of that amount. The argument that led there starts from the case for abortion and extends a woman's autonomy past birth. That argument depends on the claim that feminism's opponent is biology. A simpler explanation is that women defend women whatever the act.

## Markets and the state

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism - The Calculation Problem|Socialism - The Calculation Problem]]: A price combines information held by millions of people. When the state owns the factories and farms there are no prices for them, so the planners guess. The guesses stay wrong, because admitting the errors would weaken the planners' authority. Every country that tried full state ownership ended in shortages, famine, or people fleeing. The Nordic countries that supporters point to are market economies with a welfare state added, paid for by a sales tax on everyone.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/China - The Temple and the Monks|China - The Temple and the Monks]]: In China the state is rich and the people are poor. Growth is set as a quota, and digging a hole or building a tower that stays empty both count toward it. The state owns every plot of land and the banks, so wealth moves to the state and is not passed back to the people. The high-speed trains run empty and are paid for with the savings of people who travel on the slow trains. The factories export because people in China cannot afford to buy their goods. The party that built this system had one member for every twelve thousand people, won power with a Soviet army and with an American arms embargo on its enemy, and caused at least 45 million people to starve.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/East Asian Exams - The Arms Race|East Asian Exams - The Arms Race]]: In China and South Korea one exam taken at about eighteen decides which university a student enters, and the university decides pay and senior posts for the rest of a life. Because the exam ranks students against each other, tutoring bought by one family forces tutoring on every other family, and no family can stop without giving up its own child's place. A child costs about $275,000 to raise to eighteen. Few couples have a second. Every ban on tutoring since 1980 has failed.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Britain - Poorer Than Mississippi|Britain - Poorer Than Mississippi]]: Income per person in Britain now ranks below every American state. Most Britons think it would rank seventh. After 2008 the country stopped investing, building, and manufacturing. It taxes its most productive workers hardest, charges its factories four times the American price for power, refuses permission to build on land it has, and runs a welfare state in which a worker can lose more from a pay rise than he gains.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/South Africa is a Warning to the West|South Africa is a Warning to the West]]: South Africa had the strongest economy in Africa. Its governing party put a president's allies at the top of the state's institutions. The power company was used for cash until the power had to be cut for hours a day, gangs stole the railways' cable, the police were underfunded, and people with money now pay for their own electricity and guards. Rhodesia went the same way a generation earlier, and Britain and America have started paying for the same replacements.

## Faith

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]]: I am secular and agnostic. I think some Christian values are good, without holding the belief they come from. I am not interested in an argument about whether any faith is true, because that argument only persuades people who already hold the faith. Christians want the law to enforce their faith, and Muslims make their own demands wherever their numbers grow. Either way, those demands affect you. The question about religion that affects people outside the faith is what each religion would have the state do. That page sets out those demands so you can judge them from outside the faith, without having to argue about God.

## Methods for any subject

The pages in this group describe methods of reasoning that apply to any subject. They were developed while working on the hardest subjects.

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Per Capita|Per Capita]]: Twenty poisoned apples in a thousand is safer than two in ten. A total tells you nothing until you know how many things it was counted out of. A person who uses rates when rates support his argument and totals when totals support it is not confused. He is choosing which one to use.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]]: A person's work can be taken for the whole of their life without anyone owning them. A debt that pays the lender more each time the borrower misses a payment, and that bankruptcy cannot cancel, does this to one person. A government's debt does the same to a whole population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]]: There is no answer to being called racist. The word is used to penalise a person for saying something, and it does not respond to what was said. The question then changes from whether what you said is true to whether you are the kind of person who is allowed to say it. Denying it does not work, because the word refers to which kind of person you are and not to you as an individual.
