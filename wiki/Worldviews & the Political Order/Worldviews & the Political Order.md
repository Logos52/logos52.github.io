# Worldviews & the Political Order

Each of these pages picks up a disputed position about how a society should be arranged, or about what is true of the people who live in it, and goes through the reasons given for that position to find out which of them hold. A page asks whether the reasoning works rather than whether the conclusion is comfortable, and several of the pages end with a reason still standing.

The method page is placed ahead of the others because it says what these pages can and cannot establish, and a reader who skips it will judge the pages by the wrong standard.

**[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Validity and Truth|Validity and Truth]]** — An argument can fail in either of two places, in its premises or in the step from those premises to its conclusion, and these pages test that step while taking the premises as given. The page lists the eight ways the step from premises to conclusion can break.

## Rule and belonging

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Democracy as Sacred Cow|Democracy as Sacred Cow]] — Seven reasons are given for rule by majority vote, and when each of them is examined two of the seven hold, each of the two holding for a different reason.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]] — Socialism is usually argued for as a matter of economics, but there is a moral case behind the economic one, and that moral case is the one this page examines.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]] — A small organized group rules over a large unorganized one, and the page explains why that happens and what follows from it for policy.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]] — The page asks who gets admitted to a country, and whether the country still holds together after they have been admitted.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]] — Since 2024 the American left has been backing away from the positions it held in the 2010s, and one claim says the immigrants of those years forced that retreat by rejecting those positions and by now deciding elections. The page tests the claim that the immigrants forced the retreat and finds no link between the immigrants and the retreat.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]] — The act ended refusing people because of their race, and in order to do that it ended the right to refuse. The page weighs what the act delivered against what it gave up.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response|What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response]] — Six reasons are given for a state answering an attack with a blow of the same size, and three of them hold, though they hold because of cost and not because of virtue.

## Peoples and inheritance

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]] — A gap in outcomes between two groups is often read as proof of discrimination, and the page examines what that step from the gap to the proof needs in order to hold.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]] — The page takes the claim that people alive today owe a debt for what their ancestors did, and asks whether a debt can be inherited in that way at all.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]] — Three charges are brought against European history, which are slavery, colonialism, and mass killing, and the page answers each of the three as a charge.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Fatigue|Racial Fatigue]] — A demand to keep paying attention to one group's grievances has no end, and the page follows how a demand like that wears down even people it was never about.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]] — Gangs of men abused girls across British towns for twenty years while the police, the social services, and the prosecutors kept almost no record of it, and the page follows why staying silent paid and what staying silent cost.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]] — A society keeps order partly by using moral words, and those words move the people who hold the values behind them no matter who is saying them.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]] — Diversity, equity, and inclusion programs are defended with six business reasons and four moral ones, and the page weighs the two sets of reasons separately.

## Sex and family

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]] — The page takes the claim that a person's sense of their own sex overrides their biological sex, and goes through the ten reasons given for that claim.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]] — Permitting a way of life and being required to affirm it are two different things, and the reasons given for permitting it are being used to argue for affirming it.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]] — A school day spent sitting still filtered out boys, and the page follows what that filtering taught each sex about the other.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband|Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband]] — Extending the vote to women changed what the electorate wants, and the page says which direction it changed in and why.

## Faith

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]] — The page sets out what Christianity and Islam each ask a state to do, and asks whether each of those demands follows from the faith's own text. The page says it lacks a case of a Muslim-majority state and how that state treated its non-Muslim subjects, and that case is still missing.

## Tools that travel

Three pages here are general reasoning tools, which were worked out on contested material but apply anywhere.

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Per Capita|Per Capita]] — A rate and a total answer different questions, and the page shows how to tell someone who mixes the two up from someone who picks whichever of the two suits their case.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]] — The page shows how a population's labour can be taken without anyone owning anyone, through debts that pay the lender more when the borrower fails and that nothing cancels.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]] — The page says what an accusation of racism does to an argument, and what answering that accusation requires.
