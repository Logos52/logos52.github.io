# Worldviews & the Political Order

These pages take contested positions about how a society should be arranged, and about what is true of the people in it, and each page goes through the reasons given for one position to find which of them hold. A page asks whether the reasoning works and not whether the conclusion is comfortable, and several of the pages end with a reason still standing.

The method page says what these pages can and cannot establish, so a reader who skips it will hold the pages to the wrong standard.

**[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Validity and Truth|Validity and Truth]]** — An argument can go wrong in two places, in its premises or in the step from the premises to its conclusion, and these pages test that step while taking the premises as given. The page lists the ways the step goes wrong, such as a word changing its meaning between the premise and the conclusion, or two things that happen together being treated as if one caused the other.

## Rule and belonging

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Democracy as Sacred Cow|Democracy as Sacred Cow]] — Seven reasons are given for rule by majority vote, and the page finds that two of them hold. An election shows which side would win a fight without the fight being fought, so the side that would lose gives way, and one vote for each person is the only scheme in which nobody has to decide whose vote counts for more.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]] — Socialism says that a few people own too much, that work is paid less than what it produces, and that medicine costs too much, and it asks for a system that would not do those things. The page accepts that those faults are real and finds that they come from the state and the market being joined together, so a remedy that makes the state bigger makes the cause bigger.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]] — A small organized group rules over a large unorganized one, because ruling is full-time work, and the many hand that work to a few who can act as one. That holds whatever name a government goes by, because a monarchy, a republic, and a one-party state differ in how the few are chosen and not in whether there are a few, and a ruling group works to stop any rival group from organizing, because a ruling group that stops defending its position is replaced by one that does.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]] — Who is let into a country, and whether the country still holds together after they are in.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]] — Since 2024 the American left has been moving away from the positions it held in the 2010s, such as rules about pronouns and men taking part in women's sport, and one claim says that the immigrants who arrived in those years forced that retreat, because they rejected those positions and now decide elections. The page tests that claim and finds no link between the immigrants and the retreat.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]] — The act ended the practice of refusing to do business with people because of their race, and to do that it ended everyone's right to refuse to do business with anyone. Segregation by law ended, which was worth doing, and a rule in the other direction took its place, under which the only safe defense an employer has against a charge of discrimination is a workforce whose mix by race and sex matches the population's, and the freedom that was given up has not come back.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response|What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response]] — Six reasons are given for a state answering an attack with a blow of the same size, and the page finds that three of them hold, and none of the three is about virtue. A bigger blow costs a country its allies, it uses up the biggest threat the country still had left to make, and it tells the enemy how that country's own captured soldiers may be treated.

## Peoples and inheritance

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]] — A gap in outcomes between two groups is often taken as proof of discrimination, and the page finds that this step only works if the two groups are the same in every way that matters, which is the thing that is assumed and never shown.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]] — The claim that people alive now owe a debt for what their ancestors did, and whether a debt can be passed down that way at all.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]] — Three charges are brought against European history, which are slavery, colonialism, and mass killing, and none of them marks Europe out, because each of those practices was common across human societies, and the one thing that belonged to Europe alone was the ending of slavery, which the slavery charge leaves out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Fatigue|Racial Fatigue]] — A demand to keep paying attention to black Americans' grievances against the country never ends, because no evidence makes the grievance smaller and no agreement satisfies the demand, and the page follows how that wears out even the people the demand was never about.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]] — Gangs of men abused girls across British towns for twenty years while the police, the social services, and the prosecutors kept almost no record of it, and keeping quiet was in each person's interest, because anyone who said out loud that the men were mostly Pakistani Muslims lost their job on their own, while the children were failed in large numbers in a way that nobody could be blamed for.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]] — A society keeps order partly through moral words, and those words move the people who hold the values behind them no matter who is saying them.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]] — Programs for diversity, equity, and inclusion are defended on two grounds, that they are good for business and that they put right past wrongs, and the page weighs the two separately. The business claims fail on their own evidence, and the claims about past wrongs stand or fall with one assumption, which is that a gap between groups proves that a system made the gap.

## Sex and family

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]] — The claim is that a person's sense of their own sex overrides their biological sex. The page finds that two of the reasons given for it hold, which are that the distress some people feel about their own sex is real and that an adult may do what they choose with their own body, and it finds that the strongest form of the claim, that being a man or a woman is a social role and not a biological kind, is the one form that its defenders never argue.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]] — Allowing a way of life and being required to affirm it are two different things, and the reasons given for the first are used to argue for the second.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]] — A school day spent sitting still suits girls and fails boys, and the boys' failure taught girls that boys are less able and taught boys that school, and the workplaces and rules that come after it, are against them, which left two populations trained to read each other wrongly.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband|Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband]] — Giving women the vote moved what the electorate wants toward a bigger state that does what a husband once did, which is to provide and to protect, and the page says why that shift goes in one direction only.

## Faith

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]] — What Christianity and Islam each ask a state to do, and whether each demand follows from the faith's own text. The page says that it is missing a case of a Muslim-majority state and how that state treated its non-Muslim subjects, and that case is still missing.

## Tools that travel

The three pages here are general reasoning tools that were worked out on contested material, and they apply anywhere.

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Per Capita|Per Capita]] — A rate and a total answer different questions, and the page shows how to tell apart someone who mixes them up from someone who picks whichever one suits their case.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]] — How a population's labour can be taken without anyone owning anyone, through debts that pay the lender more when the borrower fails to pay and that nothing cancels.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]] — The accusation of racism works as a price for saying something rather than as a reply to it, so it moves the dispute from whether the claim is true to whether the speaker is allowed to make it, and the page says what it takes to answer the accusation.
