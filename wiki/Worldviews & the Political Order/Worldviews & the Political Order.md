# Worldviews & the Political Order

Contested positions on how a society should be arranged and what is true about the people in it, each taken apart to see which of its defenses hold. The pages ask whether reasoning works, not whether a conclusion is comfortable, and several of them end with a defense left standing.

Start with the method page. It sets what these pages can and cannot establish, and reading one without it invites the wrong standard.
