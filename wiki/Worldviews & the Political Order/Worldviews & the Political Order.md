# Worldviews & the Political Order

Every page here takes one belief that people fight about, such as the belief that democracy is sacred, or that a gap between races proves racism, or that a man who feels he is a woman should or should not be one, or that immigrants killed the woke movement, and checks whether the belief is actually true.

Read the first page before the others.

**[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Validity and Truth|Validity and Truth]]** — This page sets out how the checking is done. A belief can rest on true facts and still be wrong, and a reader who understands how that happens can run the same check on any belief, not just the beliefs on these pages.

## Rule and belonging

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Democracy as Sacred Cow|Democracy as Sacred Cow]] — An election shows which side would win a fight without the fight ever being fought, so the side that would have lost gives way. That works only while votes track real strength, and it breaks when an armed and motivated minority keeps losing elections to a majority that does not much care.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]] — Socialism says that a small number of people own too much, that work is paid less than what the work produces, and that medicine costs too much, and it asks for a system that would not do those things. Those faults are real, but they come from the state and the market being joined together, so a remedy that makes the state bigger also makes the cause of the faults bigger.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]] — Why a small group always ends up running a country, even a country built on the vote. Running a country is full-time work that most people cannot do, so they hand it over to the few who are able to act together, and those few then keep their place safe by stopping everyone else from getting organized.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]] — A country stays one country only when the people it lets in take up its way of life, and whether they will is decided by who gets let in and how many get let in, not by anything done after they have arrived.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]] — Why the left gave up on pronoun rules and on men in women's sport. Some say that the immigrants the left itself let in forced the retreat, because those immigrants reject both positions and their votes now tip elections. The immigrants did not force it, because those positions were ended by courts and by state laws, and because the immigrant vote is small and splits between the parties.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]] — What the country traded away when it banned refusing to serve a person because of that person's race. The ban ended segregation by law, and that was worth doing, but everyone also lost the right to refuse to do business with anyone at all, so the only safe way an employer has left to show that he does not discriminate is to keep a workforce whose mix by race and by sex matches the mix in the population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response|What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response]] — When one country attacks another, the attacked country usually answers with a blow the same size as the blow it took, even when it could hit harder. It matches the blow not because matching is right in itself, but because hitting harder would cost the country its allies, would use up the biggest threat it still had left, and would invite the same treatment of its own captured soldiers.

## Peoples and inheritance

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]] — Why the gap between black and white Americans in earnings, in schooling, and in arrests does not prove discrimination all by itself. The gap would prove discrimination only if the two groups were alike in everything else that shapes earnings, schooling, and arrests, and nobody has shown that the two groups are alike in those things.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]] — People alive now are said to owe a debt for things their ancestors did, but no such debt can be passed down, because guilt comes from what a person himself does, and what a person does cannot be inherited.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]] — Why Europe does not deserve the blame laid on it for slavery, conquest, and mass killing. Every civilisation did those things, and the one thing Europe did alone was to end slavery, which the people laying the blame leave out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Fatigue|Racial Fatigue]] — Why people everywhere, including people who live far from America, have been worn down by black Americans' grievances against the country. Nothing that anyone says or agrees to ever closes the subject, so the only escape is to stop listening, and the tiredness spreads to people who never had anything to do with any of it.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]] — Why British police, social services, and prosecutors kept almost no record of the gangs that abused girls over twenty years. Anyone who said out loud that the men were mostly Pakistani Muslims lost his own job, while the children were failed all together, in a way that left nobody to be blamed for it, so keeping quiet was in each person's own interest.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]] — Why words like shame and guilt hold a society together, why they move the people who still feel shame and guilt no matter who is speaking the words, and why they do nothing to the people who have stopped feeling them.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]] — Whether diversity programs do the things they are sold as doing. The companies that dropped their programs did not lose any money. Whether the programs put old wrongs right turns on whether a gap between a company's black and white workers proves that the company caused the gap, and that is assumed and never shown.

## Gender and family

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]] — Gender is not an ideology to be overridden by choice. People can do whatever they want behind closed doors, but forcing others to participate in a self-delusion is awkward and robs others of their own choice. Paradoxically this alienates normal people. I'm generally for letting people do what they want, but don't force me to enable your choices.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]] — Why letting people live as they choose does not oblige anyone to celebrate how they live. What adults do in private is nobody else's business, and that is a good reason to leave them alone, but it says nothing about what schools should teach or about what anyone else must say.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]] — What a school day spent sitting still did to boys and to girls. Sitting still suits girls and fails boys, and because the girls watched the boys fail, the girls learned that boys are less able, while the boys learned that school, and everything built the way school is built, is against them, so each sex grew up with a wrong picture of the other.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Mind Virus|The Woke Mind Virus]] — Young men on the left report worse mental health than young men on the right in every survey that asks, and the position here is that the ideology does the damage, by teaching a man that words are violence, that setbacks are oppression, and that his pain is an illness with a name. Worldviews are generated from self-talk, and self-talk is one of the primary forces on mental health.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband|Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband]] — Why giving women the vote made the state grow. The state can do what a husband once did, provide for a woman and protect her, so a woman without a husband has reason to vote for more state, and because nothing can stand in for a wife, the pull runs in one direction only.

## Faith

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]] — I am secular and agnostic. I think some Christian values are good, but without the belief behind them. I am not interested in an argument about whether any faith is true, because that argument only moves people already inside the faith. Christians want the law to carry their faith, and Islam makes its own demands wherever it grows. The tendrils reach you either way. So one question about religion we actually have a stake in is what it would have the state do, and the page exists so you can judge those demands from outside the faith, without having to argue about God.

## Tools that travel

The pages in this group are ways of thinking that work on any subject, and they were worked out on the hardest subjects.

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Per Capita|Per Capita]] — Why twenty poisoned apples in a thousand is safer than two in ten. A total tells you nothing until you know how many it came out of, and a person who uses rates when rates suit him and totals when totals suit him is not confused, he is choosing.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]] — How a person's work can be taken for the whole of their life without anyone ever owning them. A debt that pays the lender more each time the borrower misses a payment, and that bankruptcy cannot cancel, does this to one person, and a government's debt does the same to a whole population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]] — Why there is no answer to being called racist. The word is a price put on saying something rather than a reply to what was said, so the question stops being whether what you said is true and becomes whether you are the kind of person who is allowed to say it, and no denial works, because the word is about the kind and not about the person.
