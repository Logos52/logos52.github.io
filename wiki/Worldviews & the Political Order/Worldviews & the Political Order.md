# Worldviews & the Political Order

Each page linked below takes one belief that people argue about, such as the belief that democracy is sacred, or that a gap between races proves racism, or that a man who feels he is a woman should or should not count as one, or that immigrants caused the end of the woke movement, and checks whether the belief is true.

Read the first page below before the others. The owner of these pages states his position on each belief on a separate page: [[personal/2026-08-17-positions|Positions]].

**[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Validity and Truth|Validity and Truth]]**: This page sets out how the checking is done. A belief can be based on true facts and still be wrong. A reader who understands how that happens can apply the same check to any belief, including beliefs that these pages do not cover.

## Rule and belonging

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Democracy as Sacred Cow|Democracy as Sacred Cow]]: An election shows which side would win a fight, without the fight taking place, so the side that would have lost accepts the result. This works only while the vote count matches which side is actually stronger. It fails when an armed and motivated minority keeps losing elections to a majority that cares little about the result.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]]: Socialists argue that a small number of people own too much, that workers are paid less than what their work produces, and that medicine costs too much, and they want a system without those faults. The faults are real. They are caused by the state and the market being joined together, so a policy that enlarges the state also enlarges the cause of the faults.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]]: Why a small group always ends up governing a country, including a country whose government is chosen by voting. Governing is full-time work that most people cannot do, so most people leave it to the few who are able to act together. Those few then keep their position by preventing everyone else from organizing.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]]: A country stays united only when the people it admits adopt its way of life. Whether they adopt it depends on which people are admitted and how many are admitted. Measures taken after they arrive do not decide it.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]]: Why the left dropped its support for pronoun rules and for men competing in women's sport. Some people say that immigrants whom the left itself admitted forced the change, because those immigrants reject both positions and their votes now decide elections. The immigrants did not force it. Courts and state laws ended those positions, and the immigrant vote is small and divided between the parties.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]]: What the country gave up when it banned refusing to serve a person because of that person's race. The ban ended legal segregation, which was worth doing. Everyone also lost the right to refuse to do business with anyone for any reason. As a result, the only safe way left for an employer to show that he does not discriminate is to keep a workforce whose mix by race and by sex matches the mix in the population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response|What Is the Virtue of a Proportionate Response]]: When one country attacks another, the attacked country usually responds with an attack of the same size as the one it suffered, even when it could do more damage. The reason is not that matching the attack is right in itself. A larger response would lose the country its allies, would use up the biggest threat it still had in reserve, and would lead the enemy to treat the country's own captured soldiers the same way.

## Peoples and inheritance

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]]: Why the gap between black and white Americans in earnings, in schooling, and in arrests does not on its own prove discrimination. The gap would prove discrimination only if the two groups were alike in everything else that affects earnings, schooling, and arrests. Nobody has shown that the two groups are alike in those things.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]]: People alive now are said to owe a debt for things their ancestors did. No such debt can be passed down to them, because guilt comes from a person's own actions, and a person's actions cannot be inherited.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]]: Why Europe does not deserve the blame put on it for slavery, conquest, and mass killing. Every civilisation did those things. The one thing Europe did that no other civilisation did was to end slavery, and the people who blame Europe leave that out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Europe - The Slow Agony|Europe - The Slow Agony]]: In 2008 the European Union's economy was larger than America's, and by 2025 it was ten trillion dollars smaller. The causes were a single currency set up in a way that its own economic theory warned against, a financial crisis answered with spending cuts, reliance on Russia for energy, company founders leaving for one market instead of staying in 27, asylum instead of skilled immigration, the lowest number of births ever recorded, and ten fewer weeks of work a year. A plan for recovery has been written down, and one of every nine of its recommendations was carried out.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Fatigue|Racial Fatigue]]: Why people everywhere, including people who live far from America, have grown tired of black Americans' grievances against the country. Nothing that anyone says or concedes ever ends the discussion, so the only option left is to stop listening. People who never had any part in the matter become tired of it as well.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]]: Why British police, social services, and prosecutors kept almost no record of the gangs that abused girls over twenty years. Anyone who said publicly that the men were mostly Pakistani Muslims lost his job. The failure to protect the children was shared by everyone at once, so no single person could be blamed for it. Keeping quiet was therefore in each person's own interest.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]]: Why words like shame and guilt keep a society cohesive, why they affect people who still feel shame and guilt regardless of who says the words, and why they have no effect on people who no longer feel shame or guilt.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]]: Whether diversity programs do what they are promoted as doing. The companies that dropped their programs did not lose any money. Whether the programs correct past wrongs depends on whether a gap between a company's black and white workers proves that the company caused the gap. That is assumed without being shown.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim|Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim]]: The claim that peoples differ enough genetically to build different kinds of society, set out in three versions, none of which names a people. The claim depends on one measure of genetic distance, one comparison from an unnamed source that ranks genes for the nervous system among the genes that differ most, and one sentence that treats a difference in the nervous system as a difference in the kind of society. The steps needed to check the claim are written down. No position is held on it yet.

## Gender and family

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]]: Gender is not an ideology that a person's choice can override. People can do whatever they want in private, but forcing others to participate in a self-delusion is awkward and takes away the other people's own choice. This alienates normal people. I'm generally for letting people do what they want, but don't force me to enable your choices.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]]: Why letting people live as they choose does not oblige anyone to celebrate how they live. What adults do in private is nobody else's business, and that is a good reason to leave them alone. That reason does not settle what schools should teach or what anyone else must say.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]]: What a school day spent sitting still did to boys and to girls. Sitting still suits girls, and boys do badly under it. The girls saw the boys do badly and concluded that boys are less able. The boys concluded that school, and every institution organized the way school is organized, is against them. As a result each sex grew up with a false belief about the other.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Mind Virus|The Woke Mind Virus]]: Young men on the left report worse mental health than young men on the right in every survey that asks. The position taken is that holding the ideology causes the harm, because a man who holds it learns that words are violence, that setbacks are oppression, and that his pain is an illness with a name.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband|Women's Suffrage - The Substitute Husband]]: Why giving women the vote made the state grow. The state can do what a husband once did, which is to provide for a woman and protect her, so a woman without a husband has reason to vote for a larger state. Nothing can take the place of a wife, so the state gives men no matching reason, and the effect goes in one direction only.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Gen Z Gender War - The Split|The Gen Z Gender War - The Split]]: Young women and young men in every rich country have grown further apart in what they believe, whom they vote for, and whether they form couples. Women take most of the degrees and most of the student debt. Men leave school to work in the trades or to do nothing. On dating apps a few men get most of the interest and the women become exhausted. The content each sex sees on its phone says that the other sex is the problem.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry|Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry]]: American courts forced every large men-only club to admit women by treating it as a business. The same laws exempted women-only colleges, gyms, and a billion-dollar executive club, under exceptions for privacy, safety, and religion that do not apply to them. The men who lost their lodges were given nothing to replace them, and the results now appear among those men as fewer friends, fewer jobs, fewer marriages, and more suicides.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Late Stage Feminism|Late Stage Feminism]]: A mother who murdered her three children was given more than a million dollars by supporters, and a woman who carried six children out of a fire got a small fraction of that amount. The argument that led there starts from the case for abortion and extends a woman's autonomy past birth. That argument depends on the claim that feminism's opponent is biology. A simpler explanation is that women defend women whatever the act.

## Markets and the state

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism - The Calculation Problem|Socialism - The Calculation Problem]]: A price combines information held by millions of people. When the state owns the factories and farms, there are no prices for them, so the planners guess. Their guesses remain wrong, because admitting the errors would weaken the planners' authority. Every country that tried full state ownership ended in shortages, famine, or people fleeing. The Nordic countries that supporters point to are market economies with a welfare state added, paid for by a sales tax on everyone.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/China - The Temple and the Monks|China - The Temple and the Monks]]: In China the state is rich and the people are poor. Growth is set as a quota, and digging a hole or building a tower that stays empty both count toward it. The state owns every plot of land and the banks, so wealth moves to the state and is not passed back to the people. The high-speed trains run empty and are paid for with the savings of people who travel on the slow trains. The factories export because people in China cannot afford to buy their goods. The party that built this system had one member for every twelve thousand people, won power with a Soviet army and with an American arms embargo on its enemy, and caused at least 45 million people to starve.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Britain - Poorer Than Mississippi|Britain - Poorer Than Mississippi]]: Income per person in Britain now ranks below every American state, and most Britons think it would rank seventh. After 2008 the country stopped investing, building, and manufacturing. It taxes its most productive workers hardest, charges its factories four times the American price for power, refuses permission to build on land it has, and runs a welfare state in which a worker can lose more from a pay rise than he gains.

## Faith

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]]: I am secular and agnostic. I think some Christian values are good, without holding the belief they come from. I am not interested in an argument about whether any faith is true, because that argument only persuades people who already hold the faith. Christians want the law to enforce their faith, and Muslims make their own demands wherever their numbers grow. Either way, those demands affect you. So one question about religion that does affect us is what each religion would have the state do. That page sets out those demands so you can judge them from outside the faith, without having to argue about God.

## Methods for any subject

The pages in this group describe methods of reasoning that apply to any subject. They were developed while working on the hardest subjects.

- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Per Capita|Per Capita]]: Why twenty poisoned apples in a thousand is safer than two in ten. A total is uninformative until you know how many things it was counted out of. A person who uses rates when rates support his argument and totals when totals support it is not confused. He is choosing which one to use.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]]: How a person's work can be taken for the whole of their life without anyone owning them. A debt that pays the lender more each time the borrower misses a payment, and that bankruptcy cannot cancel, does this to one person. A government's debt does the same to a whole population.
- [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]]: Why there is no answer to being called racist. The word is used to penalise a person for saying something, and it does not respond to what was said. The question then changes from whether what you said is true to whether you are the kind of person who is allowed to say it. Denying it does not work, because the word refers to which kind of person you are and not to you as an individual.
