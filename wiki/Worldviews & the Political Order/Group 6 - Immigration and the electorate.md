# Immigration and the electorate


Three arguments ask what large-scale immigration from cultures unlikely to assimilate does to social cohesion, wages, public safety and the electorate.

## Mass Immigration - Cohesion

Opponents of mass immigration draw the line at assimilation: immigration from cultures that blend easily into the host society makes a country stronger, whereas large-scale migration from cultures unlikely to assimilate weakens it. Assimilation marks the dividing line: newcomers who adopt local habits integrate, while large unassimilated settlement replaces the existing population.

Common defenses rely on slogans, economic assertions that omit public costs, or moral appeals nobody follows at home. Underneath them sits a transfer, where employers capture cheap labour and taxpayers pay the surrounding bill. The objection opposes large-scale migration, whether legal or not, from non-assimilating cultures, while welcoming newcomers who integrate. It does not oppose immigration itself. The case is made by a speaker who is himself an immigrant.

## The slogans

Saying diversity is strength has no better support than saying unity is strength. Variety of ideas helps, though people chanting the slogan rarely tolerate dissent, and unified societies (such as in Asia) outperform fractured ones, making assimilation a closer fit. Calling America a nation of immigrants leaves out where past arrivals came from: most came from Western Christian countries that fit smoothly into a society built by that civilisation. Calling no human illegal fails because nobody runs their own household without boundaries, and every country on Earth maintains borders.

Claiming mass immigration cannot be stopped is false. Japan keeps strict border control despite falling birth rates and an ageing population, so that fatalistic line serves chiefly to demoralise.

## The money arguments

Saying immigrants take jobs locals refuse confuses jobs with pay rates. Locals will do any work, though not at the pay levels left once cheap foreign labour floods the market.

## The Grooming Gang Cover-Up

The grooming gang cover-up holds that British institutions knew for decades about the organized abuse of young white girls and chose not to act. Reviewing Rupert Lowe's independent inquiry report puts a conservative minimum at 250,000 victims. In this account, fear of an accusation of racism mattered more to officials than protecting children, leaving remigration as the only moderate path remaining for Britain.

## Where the 250,000 figure comes from

That 250,000 figure is an extrapolation rather than a count of confirmed cases. Made first in 2018 to 2019, it scales local inquiries (such as 1,400 victims in Rotherham) up to a national total, adjusting for underreporting across dozens of towns and cross-checking witness and victim statements. Rejecting a number simply because it is extrapolated would mean rejecting other recognized atrocities estimated the same way when cover-ups prevent complete records.

## Who was targeted

Witness evidence contradicts the view that these were random crimes. Multiple victims testified that offenders targeted them for being white and Christian and justified the abuse by treating such girls as having less moral worth. Population ratios sharpen the pattern: Pakistani Muslims form roughly 2.3% of Britain's population, half women and a third minors, yet account for the clear majority of perpetrators, as official inquiries are said to confirm.

## The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

On the surface, the left dropped several cultural positions from the 2010s, which the right celebrates as a culture-war victory. Another reading sees a defeat for the right: third-world immigrants dislike woke ideas and are becoming a decisive voting bloc, so the left sheds the positions those voters oppose while preserving open borders and welfare.

That new alignment is Woke 2.0.

## What changed

Several visible retreats illustrate the shift. People can define "woman" again without a circular answer, and men competing in women's combat sports has lost backing. Drag queen story hours and invented pronouns have receded, and some gay and lesbian groups now urge transgender activists to act normally. While the right celebrates, the left treats its former convictions as an old phase.

## Why he expected it

An anti-woke pivot follows directly from demographic change. Large waves of newcomers from the third world, characterized in this account by tribalism and religious extremism, never adopted liberal norms. As those numbers grow, they form a decisive electorate and push out the progressive platform of the coalition that brought them in, a pattern said to be visible already in several cities and states.

Demographic growth rests on three drivers: low native fertility, continued immigration, and higher newcomer fertility backed by welfare. On that trajectory, several Western European nations are projected to lose their European majorities within one to three generations, and the United States shortly after.
