# Immigration and the electorate


The immigration group asks what mass immigration does to a country and to its politics. All three pages here report one channel's argument (Basic Logic).


## Mass Immigration - Cohesion

The case against mass immigration turns on assimilation. The Basic Logic videos argue that immigration from cultures that blend in easily strengthens a country, while large-scale migration from cultures that are unlikely to assimilate weakens it. The video draws the line between immigration and invasion at assimilation.

The common defenses of mass immigration are slogans, money arguments that leave out the costs, or moral appeals nobody applies at home. Underneath them sits a transfer, in which companies get cheap labour and the public pays the rest of the bill. The stance the videos take targets mass migration, whether legal or illegal, from cultures unlikely to assimilate.

It is not opposition to immigration as such, and the speaker who makes the case is an immigrant himself.

## The slogans

"Diversity is our strength" is no more true than "unity is our strength." Diversity of ideas is useful, though the people chanting the slogan seem allergic to that kind, and the video says unified cultures outperform fragmented ones, pointing to Asia. "Assimilation is our strength" would be closer. "We're a nation of immigrants" is mostly American, and it skips the question of where the immigrants came from. Most came from Western Christian nations that fit easily into a country built by the same civilisation. "No human is illegal" is meaningless, since nobody applies it to their own home, and every nation on Earth has borders.

"Mass immigration is unstoppable" is false. Japan controls its borders despite a collapsing birth rate and an ageing population, and the line exists mainly to demoralise.

## The Grooming Gang Cover-Up

The grooming gang cover-up is the claim that British institutions knew about organized sexual abuse of young white girls for decades and chose not to act. The Basic Logic video [Why Did The UK Cover Up The Grooming Gangs?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML76HE86wfk) reviews Rupert Lowe's independent gang inquiry report, which the video says cites a conservative minimum of 250,000 victims. The video argues that fear of being called racist outweighed fear for the country's children, and it ends by calling remigration Britain's last moderate option.



## Where the 250,000 figure comes from

The video admits that 250,000 is not a count of confirmed cases. It is an extrapolation, first made in 2018 to 2019, that scales detailed local inquiries such as Rotherham's 1,400 victims to the whole country. The scaling allows for underreporting and the spread across dozens of towns, and it is checked against witness and victim testimony. The video expects people to dismiss the figure because of the word extrapolation, and its defense is that this is the standard way to estimate victims of large crimes when cover-ups make full records impossible. Anyone who rejects it on method alone, it says, would have to reject other recognized atrocities estimated the same way.

## The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

Basic Logic says the left has dropped many of the cultural positions it held in the 2010s, and that the right has treated that as a win in the culture war. [Basic Logic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xthKh_ndHrc&t=138s) reads it as a defeat for the right. In his view, immigrants from the third world are hostile to woke ideas and are becoming a deciding vote. The left is therefore giving up the positions those voters dislike, while it keeps open borders and welfare.

He calls the result Woke 2.0.



## What changed

The retreat he describes is concrete: he says people can again answer "what is a woman?" without a circular definition, and it is accepted that men should not fight women in combat sports. He adds that drag queen story hours and new pronouns have faded, and that even some gay and lesbian groups now tell transgender activists to be normal. The right is celebrating, while the left looks back on its old convictions with some amusement.

## Why he expected it

Basic Logic says he predicted an anti-woke turn years ago, and his reasoning ran like this. The left imported large numbers of people from the third world, and those people, known in his words for tribalism and religious extremism, did not adopt the liberal worldview. As their numbers grow they become a deciding voting bloc, and they turn on the liberals who let them in. He says this has already taken place in a number of cities and states.
