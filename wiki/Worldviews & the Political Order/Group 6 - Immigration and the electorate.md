# Immigration and the electorate


The immigration group asks what mass immigration does to a country and to its politics. All three pages here report one channel's argument (Basic Logic), and no opposing source is included.

## Mass Immigration - Cohesion

# Mass Immigration - Cohesion The case against mass immigration turns on assimilation. The Basic Logic videos argue that immigration from cultures that blend in easily strengthens a country, while large-scale migration from cultures that are unlikely to assimilate weakens it. The video draws the line between immigration and invasion at assimilation. The common defenses of mass immigration are slogans, money arguments that leave out the costs, or moral appeals nobody applies at home. Underneath them sits a transfer, in which companies get cheap labour and the public pays the rest of the bill. The stance the videos take targets mass migration, whether legal or illegal, from cultures unlikely to assimilate. It is not opposition to immigration as such, and the speaker who makes the case is an immigrant himself.

## The Grooming Gang Cover-Up

# The Grooming Gang Cover-Up The grooming gang cover-up is the claim that British institutions knew about organized sexual abuse of young white girls for decades and chose not to act. The Basic Logic video [Why Did The UK Cover Up The Grooming Gangs?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML76HE86wfk) reviews Rupert Lowe's independent gang inquiry report, which the video says cites a conservative minimum of 250,000 victims. The video argues that fear of being called racist outweighed fear for the country's children, and it ends by calling remigration Britain's last moderate option.

## The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

# The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim Basic Logic says the left has dropped many of the cultural positions it held in the 2010s, and that the right has treated that as a win in the culture war. [Basic Logic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xthKh_ndHrc&t=138s) reads it as a defeat for the right. In his view, immigrants from the third world are hostile to woke ideas and are becoming a deciding vote. The left is therefore giving up the positions those voters dislike, while it keeps open borders and welfare. He calls the result Woke 2.0.
