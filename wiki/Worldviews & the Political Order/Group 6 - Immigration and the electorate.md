# Immigration and the electorate

The immigration group asks what mass immigration does to a country and to its politics. All three pages here report one channel's argument (Basic Logic), and no opposing source is included.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]] covers Basic Logic videos arguing that immigration from cultures that blend in easily strengthens a country, while large-scale migration from cultures unlikely to assimilate weakens it. The videos say that beneath the common defenses sits a transfer, in which companies get cheap labour and the public pays the rest of the bill.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]] covers a Basic Logic video on the claim that British institutions knew about organized sexual abuse of young white girls for decades and chose not to act. The video reviews Rupert Lowe's independent inquiry report, argues that fear of being called racist outweighed fear for the country's children, and ends by calling remigration Britain's last moderate option.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim|The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim]] reports Basic Logic's view that the left dropped many cultural positions because third-world immigrants, who dislike woke ideas, are becoming a deciding vote. He says the left keeps open borders and welfare while doing so, and calls the result Woke 2.0.
