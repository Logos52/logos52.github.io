# The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

Basic Logic says the left has dropped many of the cultural positions it held in the 2010s, and that the right has treated that as a win in the culture war. [Basic Logic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xthKh_ndHrc&t=138s) reads it as a defeat for the right. In his view, immigrants from the third world are hostile to woke ideas and are becoming a deciding vote. The left is therefore giving up the positions those voters dislike, while it keeps open borders and welfare. He calls the result Woke 2.0.

## What changed

The retreat he describes is concrete: he says people can again answer "what is a woman?" without a circular definition, and it is accepted that men should not fight women in combat sports. He adds that drag queen story hours and new pronouns have faded, and that even some gay and lesbian groups now tell transgender activists to be normal. The right is celebrating, while the left looks back on its old convictions with some amusement.

## Why he expected it

Basic Logic says he predicted an anti-woke turn years ago, and his reasoning ran like this. The left imported large numbers of people from the third world, and those people, known in his words for tribalism and religious extremism, did not adopt the liberal worldview. As their numbers grow they become a deciding voting bloc, and they turn on the liberals who let them in. He says this has already happened in several cities and states.

His case for the growth rests on three causes, which are low fertility among locals, steady immigration, and high fertility among newcomers that welfare supports. On that basis he expects many Western European countries to become minority European within one to three generations, with America a little behind. Only two positions then matter to him, the third world bloc and the dissident right that opposes it, and whichever wins will be anti-woke.

## The three paths for the left

A woke leftist, in this account, has three choices, each with its own logic:

1. Stay woke and push the old causes through every institution still under left control. He expects this group to lose ground until it is irrelevant, and to be the first removed once the real power struggle starts.
2. Become "based", which means dropping immigration and the most extreme cultural causes and taking up nationalism. The dissident right tolerates these people because it cannot be picky about allies. It exiles any of them who drift toward civic nationalism, which he defines as the belief that the state bureaucracy defines a nation.
3. Become Woke 2.0, a term he says leftist politicians coined themselves. It keeps the economic policy and the support for immigration but drops LGBT causes, which signals to the new bloc that the left is its ally.

The second and third paths differ on one point. The based leftist puts his own nation first and accepts that unlimited immigration makes a welfare state unsustainable, while Woke 2.0 stays globalist.

## What the bloc wants

Basic Logic grants that the third world bloc is not uniform but says its interests line up enough to act as one. In his list it wants more immigration so that whole communities can follow, along with more welfare, public housing and payments paid for by local taxes. It also wants to run its own communities under its own rules, more of its members in positions of power, and no pride flags or parades. The campaign strategy he describes follows from that list. In a city with many immigrants, the left runs a candidate of the main immigrant ethnicity and promises free benefits, which wins both the left-wing vote and the immigrant vote.

## Whether it was planned

Basic Logic finds it hard to believe that powerful leaders were blindsided by the values of the people they admitted, so he concludes that many of them planned it. His closing view is that the 2010s were a culture feint rather than a culture war, because while people argued about sport and pronouns, one of the largest demographic changes in history went ahead. Politicians, he says, never held the old positions. They took on what got them elected, and the woke voter was a stepping stone that is no longer needed.
