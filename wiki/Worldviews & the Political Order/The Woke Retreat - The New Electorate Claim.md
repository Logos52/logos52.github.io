# The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

# The Woke Retreat - The New Electorate Claim

Since 2024, Democratic and Labour politicians have backed away in public from the cultural positions their parties held from about 2014: pronoun rules, trans women in women's sport, and speech treated as harm. One explanation says the right's campaign against those positions did not cause the change. It says the left let in large numbers of immigrants from poor countries who reject those positions, their votes now decide elections, and the left is dropping the positions to keep them.

## Core takeaways

- The retreat is real among named politicians and party strategists.
- Party votes in Congress have barely moved.
- Courts, an executive order and sports bodies ended trans inclusion in sport.
- Naturalised citizens were 9% of 2024 US voters and split evenly.
- Immigrant groups vote in opposite directions, so they are no bloc.
- A smaller version holds: new voters change what a party can say.

## The claim

The account runs in four steps. The left imported populations from Africa, the Middle East, South Asia and Latin America, and those populations hold conservative views on sex and family. They grew into a bloc large enough to decide elections, so the left dropped its cultural causes to keep them. The left's leaders knew this would happen when they let the immigrants in. The account also lists what the bloc wants from the left.

- More immigration and more welfare.
- Lighter policing of its own communities.
- More of its own people in office.
- No Pride flags or parades.

## What holds

Two steps of the account hold. Attitudes in the countries the immigrants come from are far from Western ones, and the gap survives migration into the first generation. There are towns and seats where immigrant-origin voters turned on the left over exactly these issues. Those cases show that admitting an electorate whose values differ can bind the party that admitted it.

- Pew 2019, homosexuality should be accepted: Nigeria 7%, Netherlands 92%.
- Hamtramck, Michigan: an all-Muslim council banned Pride flags in June 2023.
- Birmingham, England: parent protests suspended LGBT lessons at Parkfield school in 2019.
- UK 2024: five pro-Gaza independents took seats from Labour.
- Newham, May 2026: a Gaza-focused party took 24 of 66 council seats.

## Where it breaks

The last step, that the new voters caused the retreat, fails on the numbers. Naturalised citizens are too few and too split to force a national party's hand, and the groups inside the supposed bloc moved in opposite directions in 2024. On trans athletes, the most prominent of these issues, white Americans are more opposed than Hispanic, Black or Asian Americans. The policies themselves were ended by Republican and judicial power, with no immigrant vote involved.

| Group | 2020, Dem / Rep | 2024, Dem / Rep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Latino men | 59 / 36 | 44 / 54 |
| Black women | 90 / 9 | 92 / 7 |

- Executive Order 14201 barred males from women's sport in February 2025.
- The UK Supreme Court ruled "woman" means biological sex in April 2025.
- The US Supreme Court upheld state sports bans in June 2026.
- Opinion moved 7 points in four years, too fast for population change.
- House Democrats voted about 206 to 2 against trans-related bills.
- Most European centre-left parties tightened immigration after 2015.
- Britain's record 860,000 net migration came under a Conservative government.

The retreat is a change of mind inside the existing electorate, pushed by right-wing state power. The immigrant vote is real where it is concentrated, and there it has already cost the left seats.
