# Sex, gender and family

The last group asks what the sources claim about men, women, identity and family.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Gender Ideology - The Override Claim|Gender Ideology - The Override Claim]] covers a Basic Logic video that ranks the defenses of the idea that inner identity can override biological sex and argues that they fail for a shared reason.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/LGBT - Permission vs Promotion|LGBT - Permission vs Promotion]] covers a Basic Logic video arguing that the best argument for LGBT is that what two consenting adults do in private is none of anyone else's business. The video says that argument supports leaving people alone but cannot support pride months, rainbow logos and school lessons.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Late Stage Feminism|Late Stage Feminism]] covers a Basic Logic video asking how, by its account, people online came to raise over $1 million in support of a mother who murdered her three children. It offers two answers, one through the abortion debate and a deeper one in which feminism, by rejecting patriarchy, ends up rejecting biology. Against that, a reaction video by Asmongold accepts part of this but gives a simpler cause, in-group bias.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry|Single-Sex Spaces - The Asymmetry]] covers a Front Page video saying that American law spent about 40 years opening men-only clubs while leaving women-only spaces legal, and that nothing replaced what men lost.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]] covers a Basic Logic video arguing that schools, by having children sit still while an adult lectures for hours, screen out boys and then blame them for failing.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Gen Z Gender War - The Split|The Gen Z Gender War - The Split]] covers a Front Page video that cites a 2025 King's College London survey of 30 countries. In that survey, young women are about 30 percentage points more progressive than young men, up from a gap of 6 points in 2000. The video's view is that each side's grievances are real and that the two sides now live in separate media worlds.
