# Late Stage Feminism

Late stage feminism is the name used here for the point where feminism, having set out to free women from the old order between men and women, turns on the instincts that order was built on, up to the instinct to protect one's own children. Two payments show the point. Lindsay Clancy, a mother in Massachusetts who murdered her three children, was given more than $1 million by supporters online, and a woman burned over 93% of her body carrying six children out of a fire was given $42,000.

- The thing to explain is public support for the mother.
- Ideologies justified murder before, and wide sympathy for the murderer is new.
- The abortion debate moved from personhood to hard cases to bodily autonomy.
- Bodily autonomy does not stop at birth.
- Every long-lasting society settled into male leadership.
- Feminism asks women to act against each female disposition.
- The instincts stay and get pointed at bosses, pets and causes.

## The two payments

The mother's money was raised on GoFundMe, a site where people ask the public for donations. Supporters posted videos of themselves with their own children, saying they felt for her. The site has a rule about who may raise money there, and it applied the rule to a man in a similar position and did not apply it to her.

- GoFundMe bars fundraising for anyone charged with a crime.
- The rule kept a man who murdered a teenager off the site.
- He raised more than half a million dollars elsewhere.
- GoFundMe did not apply the rule to the mother.

## The abortion arguments

The case for abortion has used a series of arguments in turn, and each was dropped when it broke. The first says the unborn is only cells and not a person, and it fails at late-term abortion, which the same side supports, where the child is the same as one already born. The second points to hard cases such as rape and incest, and even granted it covers only abortion in those cases. The third, in use now, is bodily autonomy, which concedes that abortion kills a child and says pregnancy causes stress, impairment and harm, so ending it is self-defence against a dependent.

- Rape, incest and non-viable pregnancies: about 3 to 4% of abortions.
- Money reasons: about 6 to 7%.
- Elective: about 92%.
- A born child also causes stress, harm and lost career.
- A born child also depends on the parent to exist.
- So bodily autonomy becomes autonomy, full stop.
- Autonomy is the reason given afterwards, and did not cause the support.

## The order every society settled into

Every long-standing society in every era became male-led. An arrangement that holds regardless of circumstance points to biological incentives. Exceptions were rare and did not last, which is why feminism needed a mass movement in the 19th and 20th centuries. Feminism's own history says men ruled women until about a century ago, and if that is true, the arrangement had a biological cause that humans adapted to.

- Rejecting male authority is rejecting human biology.
- Feminism calls the arrangement a social construct, and that misreads its cause.

## Inversion and its substitutes

Taken to its end, the ideology asks women to do the opposite of each female disposition. If women lean modest, the feminist must be loud, and if they lean toward accepting authority, she must be unruly. If women are the fiercest protectors of their children, the furthest feminist becomes the children's greatest threat. Women offered real choice pick what they are predisposed to, and the countries with the most equal opportunity show the largest differences between the sexes.

- Simone de Beauvoir said women would have to be forced.
- The instincts toward authority and children stay.
- A boss or a bureaucracy replaces a husband.
- Pets, causes, or migrant and minority groups replace children.

## The second reading

A simpler reading puts abortion second. Abortion moved the line on what a mother may do to her own children, and the main reason for the support is that women defend other women for anything, with loyalty to their own group ahead of the act. The goal behind the support is control and power with no accountability, and inversion happens where it serves that goal and stops where it does not. The culture that allows this is at fault, the same culture that cheered the assassination of Charlie Kirk.
