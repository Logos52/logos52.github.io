# Late Stage Feminism

Late stage feminism is the end state of a movement that set out to free women from male authority. The end state is a mother who kills her children and is given money for it.

In 2026 a Massachusetts mother who murdered her three children was given more than $1 million by supporters online. A woman who carried six children out of a fire, with burns over 93% of her body, received $42,000. Killings excused by an ideology are old. What is new is that the support was wide, open, and proud. Supporters filmed themselves holding their own children while saying they felt sympathy for her. The reaction is the more disturbing part.

There are two explanations for that support. The first is the abortion argument, which moved from "not yet a person" to "my body", and then took a further step that carries "my body" past birth. Underneath that first explanation is an older claim: feminism rejects the order between men and women that every lasting society settled into, so what it actually opposes is biology, and a movement that opposes biology ends in inversion. The second explanation is simpler. A woman gets defended by other women whatever she did, and abortion only made the defense sayable.

## Core takeaways

- The abortion argument was made in three steps: the unborn are not yet a person, then the hard cases, then bodily autonomy. The third step does not stop at birth.
- A born child causes stress, impairs a life, does harm, and depends on the parent, so the autonomy argument allows exactly what it was meant to rule out.
- Feminism rejects the order every lasting society settled into. On feminism's own account of history, that order rests on biology, and the movement does not win against biology.
- Where equal opportunity goes furthest, the sexes differ most, because free people choose what they are predisposed to.
- The instincts do not vanish. They are redirected, from a husband to a boss and a bureaucracy, and from children to pets, causes, and migrant groups.
- The second explanation is that women defend women whatever the act, and abortion only made that defense sayable.

The nearest explanation for the support is the abortion debate, and it is a fair one. If killing a child in the womb is acceptable, killing one outside it is a small step. Abortion has been defended three ways, one after another.

The first argument said the unborn are not yet a person, only developing cells, so ending them cannot be murder. That argument fails on late-term abortion, which the same side supports. A child at that stage is functionally identical to one outside the womb. So the first argument never supported abortion on demand until birth.

The second argument was made once the first was seen to be broken. It leaned on the hard cases, sexual assault above all. Those cases are real and rare. Even granted in full, they do not support abortion until birth. They bought time.

The third and current argument is bodily autonomy. It concedes that abortion kills a child. It says pregnancy causes stress, impairment, and harm, so ending it is self-defense against an unwanted parasite that depends on you.

Every long-standing society in every era and every culture became patriarchal because it is the most stable arrangement. An arrangement that is most stable regardless of circumstance can only rest on biological incentives. If patriarchy had been a one-off, it could be called a social construct. Feminism's own history says men ruled women with an iron grip everywhere until a century ago. On that history there was a biological cause for the arrangement starting, a biological reason it continued, and thousands of years of adaptation to it. So on its own account of the past, feminism's rejection of male authority is a rejection of human biology. The anger is aimed at men. What it opposes is nature.

The plainer version of the same claim goes through violence. All law derives from violence, and men hold the monopoly on violence, so men make the laws. A society that is truly matriarchal gets taken over by the men of its own tribe or of the next one. Where a matriarchy exists, it exists under a patriarch who protects it. On that account every society will always be patriarchal, men are the more stable half, and women have been selected over those thousands of years to live under the arrangement.
