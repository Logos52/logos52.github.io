# Late Stage Feminism

Late stage feminism is the name used here for the point where feminism, having set out to free women from the old order between men and women, turns on the instincts that order was built on, up to the instinct to protect one's own children. The name gives a reader a way to explain two payments: a mother in Massachusetts who murdered her three children was given more than $1 million by supporters online, while a woman burned over 93% of her body carrying six children out of a fire was given $42,000.

## Core takeaways

- The thing to explain is the public support for the mother, not the crime. Ideologies have justified murder before; wide public sympathy for the murderer is new.
- The first explanation is the abortion debate. Three arguments were used in turn, that the unborn is not a person, then the hard cases such as rape, then bodily autonomy. Each was dropped when it broke, and bodily autonomy does not stop at birth.
- Once a dependent child may be killed for causing stress, harm and lost career, a born child qualifies on the same grounds.
- Every long-lasting society settled into male leadership. Feminism rejects that order, and on feminism's own account of history the order had a biological cause that humans adapted to.
- Taken to its end, the ideology asks women to do the opposite of each female disposition. The instincts do not go away; they get pointed at a boss, a bureaucracy, pets, or a cause.
- A simpler second reading: women defend other women regardless of the act, and the aim is power without accountability, with inversion as a by-product.
- The owner leans pro-life, with exceptions after rape or similar damage, and holds that the fix is in the culture, not in laws and courts.

## The argument

- The two payments
  - The mother's money was raised on a fundraising site. The site has a rule against raising money for anyone charged with a crime. The rule kept a man who murdered a teenager off the site; he raised more than half a million dollars elsewhere. The rule was not applied to the mother.
  - Supporters posted videos of themselves with their own children saying they feel for her.
- The abortion arguments
  - Argument one: the unborn is not a person, only cells. It fails at late-term abortion, which the same side supports, where the child is the same as one already born.
  - Argument two: hard cases, rape and incest. Even granted, they cover only abortion in those cases, not abortion until birth. Rape, incest and a non-viable fetus together are about 3 to 4% of abortions; money reasons about 6 to 7%; the rest, about 92%, are elective.
  - Argument three, in use now: bodily autonomy. It concedes that abortion kills a child, and says pregnancy causes stress, impairment and harm, so ending it is self-defence against a dependent.
- The step outside the womb
  - A born child also causes stress, impairs a life and a career, causes harm, and depends on the parent for its existence.
  - So bodily autonomy becomes autonomy full stop. It is the reason given after the fact, not the cause of the support.

- The order every society settled into
  - Every long-standing society in every era became male-led. An arrangement that holds regardless of circumstance points to biological incentives, not to a construct.
  - Exceptions were rare and did not last, which is why feminism needed a mass movement in the 19th and 20th centuries.
  - Feminism's own history says men ruled women until about a century ago. If that is true, the arrangement had a biological cause and humans adapted to it over that time.
  - So the rejection of male authority is, on that account, a rejection of human biology.
- Inversion and its substitutes
  - If women lean modest, the feminist must be loud. If they lean toward accepting authority, she must be unruly. If they are the fiercest protectors of their children, the furthest feminist becomes the children's greatest threat.
  - Women offered real choice pick what they are predisposed to: the countries with the most equal opportunity show the largest differences between the sexes. An early feminist thinker said women would have to be forced.
  - The instincts toward authority and toward children stay. They get redirected: a boss or a bureaucracy in place of a husband; pets, causes, or migrant and minority groups in place of children.

## The second reading

- Abortion moved the line on what may be done to one's own children, but it is not the main reason for the support.
- The main reason is that women defend other women for anything, own-group loyalty ahead of the act.
- The goal is control and power with no accountability. Inversion happens where it serves that goal and stops where it does not.
- The culture that allows this is at fault, the same culture that cheered a political assassination.
