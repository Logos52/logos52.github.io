# Late Stage Feminism

## Reasoning

### Abstract

Late stage feminism is the end state of a movement that set out to free women from male authority. The end state is a mother who kills her children and gets paid for it. In 2026 a Massachusetts mother who murdered her three children was given more than $1 million by supporters online. A woman who carried six children out of a fire, with burns over 93% of her body, received $42,000. The horror is less the crime than the reaction, because supporters filmed themselves with their own children to say they felt for her. This page walks the road to that pair of numbers. The road runs through the abortion argument, which moved from "not yet a person" to "my body". Then it runs through the step that carries "my body" past birth. Under the road sits an older claim: feminism is a rejection of the order every lasting society settled into, so its real opponent is biology, and a movement at war with biology ends in inversion. Beside it sits a plainer reading: a woman gets defended by other women whatever she did, and abortion only made the defense sayable.

### Core takeaways

- The road from abortion to infanticide has three steps: the unborn are not yet a person, then the hard cases, then bodily autonomy, and the last step does not stop at birth.
- A born child still causes stress, impairs a life, does harm, and depends on the parent, so the autonomy argument licenses exactly what it was meant to fence off.
- Feminism rejects the order every lasting society settled into, so on its own account of history its opponent is biology, and it does not win that fight.
- Where equal opportunity goes furthest, the sexes differ most, because free people choose what they are predisposed to.
- The instincts do not vanish. They get redirected, from husband to boss and bureaucracy, from children to pets, causes, and migrant groups.
- The plainer reading runs beside the road: women defend women whatever the act, and abortion only made that defense sayable.

### Essay

Two payments frame the whole thing. A mother who murdered her three children was given a fortune by people who had never met her. A woman who carried six children out of a fire, burned nearly everywhere, was given a small fraction of it. The crime is not the new part. Killings excused by an ideology are old. The new part is that the support was wide, open, and proud, from people holding their own children as they gave it.

The nearest explanation is the abortion argument, and the way it has changed. It began by saying the unborn are not yet a person. That could not survive its own side's support for abortion late in pregnancy, when the child is the same child it would be outside. It moved to the hard cases, which are real and rare and never supported abortion on demand. It arrived at bodily autonomy. That argument concedes that a child dies. It says the pregnancy's stress, impairment, and harm make ending it self-defense against something that depends on you.

That last argument does not stop where its makers want it to. Birth changes nothing on the list. A born child is stressful. A born child impairs a life. A born child does harm. A born child depends on you completely. So bodily autonomy becomes plain autonomy. A mother who kills her three children is what the argument looks like when someone follows it all the way. That is the rationalization behind the support. It is a conclusion, and the cause sits underneath it.

The cause, on the first account, is what feminism is. It is a rejection of the order between men and women that every lasting society settled into. The exceptions were few and short-lived. An arrangement that every era and every culture arrives at is the most stable one, and stability regardless of circumstance can only rest on biology. Feminism's own story of the past concedes the point. If men ruled everywhere for thousands of years, then people adapted to being ruled that way. So the movement's real opponent was never men. It was nature. A movement at war with nature reaches for inversion: loud where women are inclined to be modest, unruly where they are inclined to submit, and, at the end, a threat to the children they are inclined to protect above everything. The plainer version of the same claim runs through force. Law comes from violence, men hold the monopoly on violence, and any matriarchy exists under a patriarch's protection.

Inversion never finishes, because the instincts do not go away. Where opportunity is most equal the sexes differ most, since people who are free choose what they are inclined to. So the instincts get redirected instead. A boss or a bureaucracy takes the husband's place. Pets, causes, and migrant groups take the children's place, cared for the way a mother cares for her own. An early thinker of the movement saw the problem from inside and said women would have to be forced into it, because offered a choice they would not come.

The second reading is shorter and does not need the history. Women defend women, whatever the act. Own-group loyalty came first, and the abortion argument only moved the window far enough that the defense could be said out loud. On this reading inversion is not a goal either. Power without accountability is the goal, and inversion happens where it serves power. The two readings agree on where it ends. A movement at war with biology loses.

### Conclusion

So the pair of payments is not a freak. It is the argument followed to its end by people who were going to defend her anyway. The road explains why the defense could be spoken. The loyalty explains who spoke it. Both readings say the end is a war with biology that the movement cannot win, and both point at the culture rather than the courts as the place it gets settled. That is where the position held here sits. Pro-life in the lean, except after rape or other such damage. The fix in the culture that produces it, the motivation and the customs around it, rather than in laws and courts.

It is the same culture that cheered a political assassination. Supporters filmed themselves holding their own children to say they felt sympathy. One comment under the account of it put the whole shift in a line: it went from they are just fetuses to they are just kids.

The default explanation is the abortion debate, and it is a fair one. If killing a child in the womb is fine, killing one outside it is a small step. Abortion has been defended three ways, one after another. The first said the unborn are not yet a person, only developing cells, so ending them cannot be murder. That argument fails on late-term abortion, which the same side supports. A child at that stage is functionally identical to one outside the womb. So it never supported abortion on demand until birth. The second, once the first was seen to be broken, leaned on the hard cases, sexual assault above all. Even granted in full, those cases do not support abortion until birth. They bought time. The third and current argument is bodily autonomy. It concedes that abortion kills a child. It says pregnancy causes stress, impairment, and harm, so ending it is self-defense against an unwanted parasite that depends on you.
