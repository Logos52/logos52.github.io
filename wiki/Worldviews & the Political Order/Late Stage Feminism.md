# Late Stage Feminism

A [Basic Logic video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3SOWFC2SvFY) asks how a society reached the point where, by its account, people online raised over $1 million in support of a mother who murdered her three children. According to its own description, the fundraiser was for her parents. By the video's figures, a woman who saved six children from a fire, with burns to 93% of her body, received about $42,000. The video says the crime is less shocking than the public reaction to it. It offers two answers, a short one through the abortion debate and a deeper one in which feminism, by rejecting patriarchy, ends up rejecting biology itself. In a [reaction video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtli77ZzhT0), Asmongold accepts part of this and gives a simpler cause.

## The two payments

The video treats the fundraising gap as the sign of a new problem. It says murders justified by ideology are old, while wide public support for them is new, and it points out that supporters filmed themselves with their own children while expressing sympathy. It sets aside whether the killing was evil and asks only how the culture got here. Asmongold adds that the fundraising site has a rule against raising money for killers, and he says the site ignored that rule in this case. He says "It's the same thing as Charlie Kirk," and adds that the problem is a culture that allows and enables such support.

## The road through abortion

The video traces three stages in the pro-choice case. The first said the unborn are only developing cells and not yet human, so ending them cannot be murder. The video calls this wordplay and says it fails on late-term abortion, which the same side supports, since the baby then is functionally the same as one outside the womb. The second stage leaned on rare and emotional cases such as sexual assault, which the video says do not support unrestricted abortion up to birth either.

The third stage, bodily autonomy, is the one in use now. On the video's account it concedes that abortion kills a baby and justifies it because pregnancy causes stress, impairment and harm, much like self-defence against a dependent parasite. The video then argues that the same reasons apply after birth, because children also cause stress, impair careers, do harm and depend on their parents. In its words, the autonomy argument becomes a "no autonomy argument," and that supplies the rationale for supporting the mother.

Asmongold agrees that abortion moved the line of what is acceptable. He calls himself pro-abortion and still offers his own figures on why abortions happen. By his count, rape, incest and non-viable pregnancies make up about 3 to 4% of abortions and financial reasons about 6 to 7%. He puts elective reasons at about 92%.

## The argument from biology

The video calls the abortion logic the conclusion and looks for the cause in feminism itself. It defines feminism as a rejection of patriarchy, the order between men and women since the dawn of civilisation. It asks why every long-lasting society became patriarchal and answers that patriarchy is the most stable arrangement. Because that held everywhere and in every era, the video reasons, the cause must be biological. If men really ruled women for thousands of years, as feminists say, then humans adapted to it through natural selection. On that reading, feminism rejects human biology, and its true opponent is nature.

From there the video expects feminism to invert female traits, so modesty becomes loudness, deference becomes defiance, and the protective mother becomes the threat. It cites Simone de Beauvoir as saying women would have to be forced into feminism because choice alone would not do it. It also says the societies with the most equal opportunity show the most difference between the sexes, because people given freedom choose what biology inclines them to. In its account, the instincts toward authority and motherhood stay as strong as ever. In its account feminism redirects them, so a boss or a bureaucracy takes the husband's place, and pets or social causes take the children's.

## The plainer reading

Asmongold thinks the main cause is simpler. He says women defend other women whatever they do, which he calls in-group bias, and that this explains the support better than abortion does. He also doubts that inversion is the goal and says the aim is power and freedom from accountability, with inversion as a side effect. On matriarchy, he argues that men's monopoly on violence means any matriarchal society survives only under a patriarch's protection.
