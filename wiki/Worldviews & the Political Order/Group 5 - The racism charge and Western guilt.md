# The racism charge and Western guilt

The guilt group asks what the charge of racism does in an argument and whether European history carries a special guilt. All four pages here report one channel's argument (Basic Logic), and no opposing source is included.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]] covers three Basic Logic videos arguing that people use the word "racist" to end an argument and not to answer it. The second of them says the right response to the accusation is to ask whether you are right.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]] covers a Basic Logic video arguing that words like "racism" and "discrimination" get aimed at the West by people who do not apply them at home. The video says they do it because the words win resources and better treatment. Its conclusion is that the only useful response is to stop being moved by the words.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]] covers a Basic Logic video that ranks the arguments for the belief that Europe's history and heritage are uniquely evil. His test throughout is that an argument for guilt needs Europeans to have done something others did not.

[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Indictment of Europe|The Indictment of Europe]] covers a second Basic Logic video, which answers the charges of slavery, colonialism and mass killing with one test. In that test, a people can be singled out for punishment only if what it did was unique. He argues that on every charge Europe did what other civilisations did, except that it ended slavery, and the page draws some details from the white guilt video.
