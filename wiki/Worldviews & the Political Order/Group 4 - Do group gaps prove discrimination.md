# Do group gaps prove discrimination


The gaps group asks whether a gap in outcomes between groups proves discrimination, and its opening page names the step the others test. All five pages here report one channel's argument (Basic Logic), and no opposing source is included. The pages give different explanations: the Distance Claim video points to genetic distance, while the Racial Fatigue video blames fatherlessness rather than race.

## Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference

# Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference The disparity inference is the step from "groups differ in outcomes" to "so discrimination caused it." The Basic Logic video [Racial Egalitarianism: All Arguments Ranked & Debunked](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVZ2I4lzUlI&t=47s) argues that the step only works if all racial groups are the same underneath, a belief it calls racial egalitarianism. It then ranks six common defenses of that belief on a tier list. The video says it deals only with group averages, so individual exceptions in any group do not change the argument.

## Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim

# Genetics and Unequal Societies - The Distance Claim A [Basic Logic video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vWS59L52h1M) argues that human groups differ genetically enough to build different kinds of society, so no single political system suits every people. The video avoids naming real groups. It calls them circles, squares and triangles, and says it uses these stand-ins because biology at the scale of whole groups is a sensitive topic. Its chain runs from geography and isolation, to measured genetic distance, to differences in the nervous system, to the kind of society each group builds.

## The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association

# The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association Freedom of association is the right to choose whom you keep company and do business with, free from coercion. The Basic Logic video [An Honest Review of Civil Rights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iw8YDdx8mhY) argues that the Civil Rights Act of 1964 was right to end Jim Crow laws, which forced private owners to segregate. It says the Act was wrong to replace them with laws forcing owners to integrate. The video calls that the same violation of liberty in reverse and gives the Act one out of 10.

## Racial Fatigue

# Racial Fatigue The Basic Logic video [This YouTuber Gave Me Black Fatigue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7TSJkkY-D5c) describes "black fatigue" as tiredness from a culture it sees as hostile and hypocritical, more than from any crime done to the people who feel it. This page files it as racial fatigue. The video is a reply to a commentator who called its earlier video racist, and it argues that he ended up agreeing with every one of its claims. In the end the video puts the cause somewhere other than race, saying "it's not black that we're fatigued by. It's societal fatherlessness."
