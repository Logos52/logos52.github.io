# Baltazar Zuniga Ruiz, @baltaaazr

## Who they are

Baltazar Zuniga Ruiz, who posts as Balta, is a senior engineer at SpaceXSI on Grok Bot. That work includes early work on Grok Bot's foundations and later features that spare everyday users manual steps, such as logging into sites. ([X profile](https://x.com/baltaaazr), [event page](https://luma.com/j2rvaxil), [engineering guide](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering))

Last checked: 2026-11-19.


## Setup

Baltazar Zuniga Ruiz builds Grok Bot with Grok Bot, which is how the foundation came together in four weeks. Sign-in work lets the bot log into sites through a password manager login it never sees.
