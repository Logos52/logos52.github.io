# Peng Zheng, @pengzheng_

## Who they are

Peng Zheng designs Grok Bot at SpaceXSI and previously worked at Cursor, Zoom and IDEO. The public work moves between personal tools, such as a self-updating website, and the design of the product itself. ([X profile](https://x.com/pengzheng_), [How I AI video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zJEHbvT5_Yg))

Last checked: 2026-11-19.


## Setup

Peng Zheng uses Grok Bot as the entire back end of a personal site, with no separate CMS or design file. A podcast bot turns any link into an episode for a podcast app, and later posts pitch handing restaurant bookings, doctor appointments and insurer hold times to a bot.
