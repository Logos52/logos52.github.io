# Builders' Logs 2026

Builders' Logs 2026 follows what thirteen people who build with AI agents did from January through 19 November 2026. Every dated line on a person page links to a public X post, video, or article by that builder, with no trust grading so you can read the sources and decide for yourself.

People are ordered by how much they bridge heavy agent engineering and everyday use (email, errands, meetings, team chores). The hub opens with two labs, **Grok** (xAI and SpaceX Super Intelligence) and **Anthropic**, followed by builders whose public work does not sit with either lab right now.
