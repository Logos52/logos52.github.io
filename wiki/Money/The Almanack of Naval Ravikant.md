## Sources

- Ravikant, 2018 "How to Get Rich" tweetstorm: wealth versus money versus status; assets that earn while you sleep; specific knowledge, accountability, leverage; permissionless leverage as code and media; long-term games; the claim that you do not get rich by selling your time. Public thread. No purchase is needed to read it.
- Jorgenson, *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant* (2020): a compilation of the thread and public interviews. Happiness as a skill; desire as a choice to stay unhappy until the wanted thing arrives. Jorgenson compiled the material; Ravikant originated it.
