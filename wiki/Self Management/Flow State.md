## Sources

- Mark, Gudith & Klocke, ["The Cost of Interrupted Work"](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf) (*CHI*, 2008)
- Mark, Gonzalez & Harris, ["No Task Left Behind? Examining the Nature of Fragmented Work"](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/CHI2005.pdf) (*CHI*, 2005). Order-of-magnitude: twenty-plus minutes to return to a complex task after an interrupt; texture, not the five-versus-thirty picture.
- Trafton & Monk, ["Task Interruptions"](https://www.interruptions.net/literature/Trafton-Reviews_HFE-3.pdf) (*Reviews of Human Factors and Ergonomics*)
- Leroy, ["Why Is It So Hard to Do My Work? The Challenge of Attention Residue"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/46489122) (*OBHDP*, 2009)
- Leroy & Glomb, ["Tasks Interrupted… and How a 'Ready-to-Resume' Plan Mitigates the Effects"](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2017.1184) (*Organization Science*, 2018)
- Masicampo & Baumeister, ["Consider It Done! Plan Making Can Eliminate the Cognitive Effects of Unfulfilled Goals"](https://users.wfu.edu/masicaej/MasicampoBaumeister2011JPSP.pdf) (*JPSP*, 2011)
- Gollwitzer & Sheeran, ["Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-Analysis"](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c4deb3507fe725ce6363c1735f1ba83bab20d665) (2006), with Gollwitzer, Peter M. (1999), implementation intentions. Pre-committing to a specific cue-and-response raises follow-through versus holding a goal alone; strongest at initiating action and shielding an ongoing goal. Confirmed at [Implementation intention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Implementation_intention).
- Fong, Zaleski & Leach, ["The Challenge–Skill Balance and Antecedents of Flow"](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/17439760.2014.967799) (*Journal of Positive Psychology*, 2015)
- Ariga & Lleras, ["Brief and Rare Mental 'Breaks' Keep You Focused"](https://content.lesaffaires.com/LAF/lacom/mental_breaks.pdf) (*Cognition*, 2011). Full title: Brief and rare mental breaks keep you focused: Deactivation and reactivation of task goals interrupt habituation. Early, brief, controlled disengagement protects vigilance.
- Albulescu et al., ["'Give Me a Break!' A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis on the Efficacy of Micro-Breaks"](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0272460) (*PLOS ONE*, 2022). Full title: "Give me a break!" A systematic review and meta-analysis on the efficacy of micro-breaks for increasing well-being and performance.
- Hagger et al., ["A Multilab Preregistered Replication of the Ego-Depletion Effect"](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1745691616652873) (*Perspectives on Psychological Science*, 2016)
- Ruiz Pardo & Minda, ["Reexamining the 'Brain Drain' Effect"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0001691822002323) (*Acta Psychologica*, 2022)
- Dunlosky et al., ["Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques"](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/stl-0000024.pdf) (*PSPI*, 2013)
- ["Unlocking Flow Through Mindfulness: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of RCTs"](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41165041/) (*The Journal of Psychology*)
- ["Relationship Between Learning Flow and Academic Performance Among Students"](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10657807/) (2023)
- *How To Improve Your Focus Permanently*. Public video (Sung, 2025-11-01). Output first; remove forces; capture instead of obeying; return practice. The speaker's paid program is not a source. No focus-music app is required.
- Basso, J. C., et al. (2019). Brief daily meditation; attention measures move at 8 weeks, not 4. Texture for the compressed sit.
- Kim, J., et al. (2025). *eNeuro*. 10–15 minutes a day for 30 days; attentional control.
- Garcia-Argibay, M., Santed, M. A., & Reales, J. M. (2019). *Psychological Research*. Consumer binaural / isochronic products as a focus treatment: small, heterogeneous. Sound as a noise mask survives; a neuroscience heading does not.
- Parkinson, C. Northcote. "Parkinson's Law." *The Economist*, 19 November 1955; later *Parkinson's Law, or the Pursuit of Progress*. John Murray, 1958. Work expands to fill the time available, written as satire about civil-service staffing, later adopted as a management heuristic. Confirmed at [Parkinson's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkinson%27s_law).
- Csíkszentmihályi, Mihály. *Beyond Boredom and Anxiety* (1975); Nakamura & Csíkszentmihályi six-factor list. Flow as full immersion with focused concentration, merged action and awareness, and distorted time. Confirmed at [Flow (psychology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_(psychology)).
- Task switching as a laboratory finding: residual cost survives even with warning that a switch is coming (Jersild 1927; Rogers & Monsell 1995). Sub-second effects in trials; the leap to a whole workday is not a finding of that literature. Confirmed at [Task switching (psychology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_switching_(psychology)).
- Kaplan, S. (1995). The restorative benefits of nature: Toward an integrative framework. *Journal of Environmental Psychology*. Attention restoration as a class for greenery and long view.
- Balban, M. Y., et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. *Cell Reports Medicine*. Breathwork as a class (cyclic sighing); no branded method required.
