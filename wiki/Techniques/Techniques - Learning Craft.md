# Techniques - Learning Craft

> Not yet reviewed by Wedge.

A technique here is a concrete method you use to train one of five dimensions of learning, which are self-management, self-regulation, deep processing, mindset and retrieval. The dimensions say what has to get stronger, and the techniques are the repeatable actions that make it stronger. Encoding methods, among them the Bear Hunter System, importance-based chunking and inquiry-based learning, build understanding as you take material in. Retrieval methods such as revision and the multipass system then keep that understanding available, while habit methods such as PEER make the work happen without waiting on motivation. Because a technique still costs effort until it becomes automatic, a new one should wait until the ones you already use are close to that point.

## The Five Dimensions

Each technique trains one or more of the five dimensions of learning:

- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Management|Self-Management]]: setting up habits, a daily rhythm and a working environment that support steady action, so less energy goes into just getting through the day.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation|Self-Regulation]]: monitoring and adjusting your own learning as it happens, so performance holds up when teaching gives less guidance or the going gets hard.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing|Deep Processing]]: how well the brain turns information into meaningful learning, through analysing, linking and critiquing what you take in.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Mindset|Mindset]]: the filter on how you read experience, where a growth mindset treats abilities as improvable and a fixed mindset treats them as static.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval|Retrieval]]: recalling and using knowledge from memory, practised so that knowledge fades more slowly and holds up under pressure.

- Encoding methods organise new information while you take it in. The [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Bear Hunter System|Bear Hunter System]] has three steps, Aim, Shoot and Skin, which ask questions, answer them to build a map and then refine the grouping. [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Importance-Based Chunking|Importance-Based Chunking]] groups information by why it matters, and [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Inquiry-Based Learning|Inquiry-Based Learning]] lets questions you create direct what you read and how you think.
- Retrieval methods slow the decay of what you have already learned. [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval/Revision|Revision]] goes back over the material, spotting gaps and closing them with true recall, and the [[wiki/Dimensions/Retrieval/Multipass System|Multipass System]] works through a big topic in layered passes that put logic and concepts ahead of details.
- Attention and scheduling methods protect the conditions the other techniques need. [[wiki/Self Management/Time Management, Attention & Scheduling|Time Management, Attention & Scheduling]] gathers the pages on deciding which work gets time and keeping attention on it.
- Habit methods make the work happen by default. [[wiki/Self Management/Habits, Productive Routines & PEER|Habits, Productive Routines & PEER]] sets out a five-part setup of Prep, Easy, Exit, Reward and Peer, so motivation is kept for the days that need a push.

## Getting Started Material

A new learning project starts better when new information has somewhere to land. [[wiki/Dimensions/Deep Processing/Prestudy|Prestudy]] is work done ahead of a main learning event, for example a class or lecture, and it builds a rough outline that makes later detail easier to retain.

The [[wiki/Dimensions/30-Day Challenges|30-Day Challenges]] then turn the dimensions into practice, with one four-week track for each trainable part of learning, and each week asks a little more than the last.

## Philosophy

Competence with a technique grows in stages, from doing it correctly only with effort to doing it almost automatically. Several high-effort techniques at once add up to overload and drain motivation, because results that come only now and then feel like a lack of progress. A new technique therefore waits until the ones already in use are approaching unconscious competence, the top of the [[wiki/Concepts/Four Stages of Competence|Four Stages of Competence]].
