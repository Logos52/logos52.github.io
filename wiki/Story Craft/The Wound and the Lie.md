# The Wound and the Lie

Interiority runs on one causal chain: a past event installs a false belief; the belief drives an external goal; chasing that goal blocks inner truth. The chain does nothing on the page until it bends a decision the reader is watching, under pressure, at a cost.

Under a vocabulary ceiling, the word list decides **when** each link can be spoken. Behavior and silence carry the Lie at levels where feeling-words do not exist yet.

## Core takeaways

- Four links: **Ghost** (one event), **Lie** (unexamined belief), **Want** (concrete external goal), **Need** (truth the Want blocks).
- **Fear** and **persona** sit between Lie and surface; arc is persona giving way to essence.
- Want is sayable from ~250–400 headwords; Need waits for high bands (~1,800–2,500 in generic ladders; [[projects/tsumugu|Tsumugu]] books map their own floors).
- Silence and idiolect cost no headwords.
- Full chains are expensive: two or three load-bearing leads per cast under a four-voice scene cap.

## The four links

**Ghost** names one event in one sentence: the shop burned, the parent left on a Tuesday. Mood is not a Ghost.

**Lie** is belief acted on without examination. If the owner can quote the creed, it is already examined.

**Want** is photographable: debt cleared, seat won, envelope sent. **Need** is what changes the person. Same sentence for both → no arc.

A backstory that never distorts a present choice is biography, not Ghost. Per scene: what presses the false belief? If nothing, the scene may do other work, but not arc work.

## Fear, persona, contradiction

Lie → fear (one clause: what must never happen) → persona (how they behave in a room) → essence (Need in plain terms).

Derive contradictions instead of inventing them:

```text
trait     feeds everyone, panics when cared for
fear      needing to be looked after
Lie       care taken is debt owed
Need      to be looked after anyway
```

[吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html): red-bean bun nobody ordered; blank where being cared for should go.

Roundness test: surprise must convince, and only early specifics supply latency for a late turn.

## Moral weakness and ensemble

Psychological weakness costs the owner; **moral weakness** costs others, often the same trait pointed outward (wall that carries family shuts partner out). Moral need is what that outward damage makes necessary. Argument lands in **what someone does with an object**, not a line about it.

One moral question, many answers: friction is value-clash, not fact disagreement. [將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html) holds one note so depth stays on leads.

## Vocabulary schedule

| Band | Affords | Chain on page | Waits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 250–400 | actions, objects | Lie as behavior | cause stated |
| 700–1,400 | reasons, sequence | Want named | Need |
| 1,800+ | feeling, abstraction | Need sayable | none |

**Deliberate withholding** (topic dodge) versus **unsayable** (below awareness, where the Lie lives). [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html): 嗯 in flat bass. [星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html): 超 and piled exclamations, assignable with tags stripped.

Payoffs: PLANT → SPARK → PAYOFF; confiding two-handers ahead of crisis; three to four voices per scene before listeners lose thread (audio drama sources).

## Change arc (what the chain spends)

Positive **change arc**: fix Lie, Ghost, Want, Need before scenes; move escalating cost → glimpse of truth → low point → climax acted from new self. **Moment of truth before low point**. Merge them and the climax has no new self to act from. Climax must **spend** Want to **buy** Need.

Flat arc holds truth and tests the world. Negative shapes (fall, corruption, bleak disillusionment) use the same six pieces; type is relationship to Lie at climax only. Partial resolutions (real gain, crisis still open) are valid. Forced clean self-revelation over-resolves.

Under vocabulary floor, objects carry Lie until words arrive; recurring scene template (same speakers, rising cost at flat vocabulary) makes widening gap readable.

## Scene economics

Every engine needs setup: one or two calm two-handers, object carrying Need through low bands, persona tightening once before crack. Setup competes with shine and banter.

Arithmetic: scenes per unit × speakers × engines per lead. Too many full chains → everyone confiding, nobody acting.

[阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s refused hands and [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html)'s camera are early-band carriers: the reader watches Want before any line can state Need. When workplace language arrives, the same objects re-read without replacing the plant. Vocabulary is the second half of the reveal.

## Failures and tests

Weak: `"Ever since my father left, I can't trust anyone."` Strong: counts change twice, folds receipt.

Want = Need removes conflict. Solved character (every trait traces to one wound) stops surprising.

**Tests**

- Earliest band: envelope + refused meal → reader infers she carries alone.
- Payoff after object beat needs no explanation line.
- Tag-stripped group scene: speakers nameable.
- Re-read after late turn: latency visible in small specifics.

## How to practice this

1. Ghost in one sentence (event, not mood).
2. Name what in a scene presses the Lie.
3. Want and Need as separate sentences.
4. Derive fear → Lie → Need from an on-page trait.
5. Strip tags; check voice.
6. Re-read early chapters after a late turn.
