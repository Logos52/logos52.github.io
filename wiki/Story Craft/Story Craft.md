# Story Craft

Story Craft collects published writing craft on character, structure and scene, from screenwriting teachers, novelists and television writers' rooms. The owner opened the section in July 2026 to find the names for techniques he had used by instinct while building a cast for a graded-reader story, and to work out why one character read thin. Each page takes one technique, says how it works, and gives a published example.

- Start with [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Craft, Condensed|Story Craft, Condensed]] for the short version.
- Character pages explain what a person is built from.
- Structure pages explain how the parts of a plot connect.
- Scene pages explain how to show feeling and place.
- Some pages cover limits: a small vocabulary, or audio only.

## Character

These pages cover what a character is made of and how a cast fits together. A past hurt, the false belief it taught, and the gap between what a character wants and what they need come first, since a character's change over the story is built from them. The ensemble pages then treat the cast as a set of different answers to one question.

- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Wound and the Lie|The Wound and the Lie]]: the past event, the false belief, want and need.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Change Arc|The Change Arc]]: a character moving from the false belief to the truth.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Arc Types|Arc Types]]: the kinds of arc a character can have.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Moral Core|The Moral Core]]: the line a character will not cross, and its price.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Round Characters and the Telling Detail|Round Characters and the Telling Detail]]: one contradiction, shown before it is named.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Character Voice|Character Voice]]: making speech recognisable without a name tag.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]]: the cast as answers to one question.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Foils and Pairings|Foils and Pairings]]: two characters set against each other.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Companion Arcs and Party Banter|Companion Arcs and Party Banter]]: side characters' arcs and their talk.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Blueprint|The Character Blueprint]]: how a character's first scene introduces them.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Rogue's Code|The Rogue's Code]]: the outlaw who keeps his own stated rules.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Diagnosing a Character|Diagnosing a Character]]: a checklist for a character that reads thin.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Reading Frames and What They Hide|Reading Frames and What They Hide]]: how the chosen checklist shapes the verdict.

## Structure

These pages cover how a story is broken into small units of change, called beats, and how the beats connect. The pages treat cause between beats as the test of a working plot. Tools for planning beats and for scheduling a long series sit here too.

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Breaking the Story|Breaking the Story]]: settling structure before writing lines.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]]: planning beats on cards.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Premise and Controlling Idea|Premise and Controlling Idea]]: the one sentence the story proves.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Seeding and Payoff|Seeding and Payoff]]: placing details early for later use.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Spotlight Rotation|Spotlight Rotation]]: which lead carries each episode.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Tonal Modulation|Tonal Modulation]]: varying heavy and light beats.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Stakes Without Mortality|Stakes Without Mortality]]: tension with no one dying.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Low Point and Catharsis|The Low Point and Catharsis]]: the worst moment and its release.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Burnout Arc|The Burnout Arc]]: a worked example from Kiki's Delivery Service.

## Scene and world

These pages cover what goes on the page inside a scene. They favour showing feeling through objects, actions and places over naming it. Two pages cover special limits: a reader who knows few words, and a listener who has only sound.

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Interiority Through Action and Object|Interiority Through Action and Object]]: feeling shown through things and gestures.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Motif and Symbol|Motif and Symbol]]: the repeated object.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Iceberg and World as Pressure|The Iceberg and World as Pressure]]: omission, and the world pressing on people.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Setting as Character|Setting as Character]]: a place that acts on the cast.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]]: story for readers who know few words.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Structure for the Ear|Structure for the Ear]]: writing for audio.
