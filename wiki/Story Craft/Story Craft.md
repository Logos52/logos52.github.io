# Story Craft

This wing collects the methods this project uses to build a group of people who stay believable across a long story, told under two constraints that shape everything else: a tight word list, and nobody dies. The constraints are not obstacles the pages work around. They decide the methods — feeling reaches the reader through objects and gestures because the words for it are rationed, and tension comes from what a person can lose short of their life. Some of the group change over the course of the story and some do not, and the pages here treat both as things to be built.

Start with [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Craft, Condensed|Story Craft, Condensed]] when the whole wing needs to sit on one page. The three pages that hold the rest of the map are [[wiki/Story Craft/Arc Types|Arc Types]], five shapes on one skeleton so a whole cast can change in different ways; [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]], each recurring person a distinct answer to one controlling question; and [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]], the word-list treated as a reveal schedule rather than a complaint.

Diagnose as you write. [[wiki/Story Craft/Diagnosing a Character|Diagnosing a Character]] runs the wing as an eleven-check audit. Next to it sits [[wiki/Story Craft/Reading Frames and What They Hide|Reading Frames and What They Hide]], because a checklist that is good at catching bad behaviour can still miss a chosen restraint — a choice only shows up as something the person could have done and didn't.

## Character & Interiority

- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Wound and the Lie|The Wound and the Lie]] is the chain from a past wound through a false belief to a surface want that fights a buried need, and a character reads as a person when that chain drives an observable choice under pressure.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Round Characters and the Telling Detail|Round Characters and the Telling Detail]] is a person when one interior contradiction governs an observable choice under pressure, nameable from what they do.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Interiority Through Action and Object|Interiority Through Action and Object]] moves inner life into a changing object-state and a watchable gesture.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Character Voice|Character Voice]] assigns a line before the tag, because each person meets the same pressure in a different default register — the wound's audible print, and an interiority tool that survives a hard ceiling.

## Arcs — Into the Dark and Out

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Arc Types|Arc Types]] keeps five shapes on one skeleton, with the type fixed at the crux, so a whole cast can run different arcs while no one dies.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Change Arc|The Change Arc]] stages positive change as an internal argument paid for by rising external cost, and ends when the character trades Want for Need at the Moment of Truth.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Low Point and Catharsis|The Low Point and Catharsis]] earns catharsis only when the reader believes the character can no longer become who the story promised, and discharges it through a recognition the reader can name.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Moral Core|The Moral Core]] is the short list of laws a character will pay for; arcs forge the core, the core is tested by the arcs, and a law is real only when events price it.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Burnout Arc|The Burnout Arc]] has no villain: the gift fails from the inside under a joy deficit, repair comes from inside, a gauge stays visible, and the world is kind enough to occasion the return without awarding it.

## Ensemble Architecture

- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]] makes each recurring character a distinct, defensible answer to one controlling question, so the ensemble argues one theme through collisions.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Foils and Pairings|Foils and Pairings]] share ground and diverge in method, and one scene does the relationship and the revelation together.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Spotlight Rotation|Spotlight Rotation]] is A/B/C plus a ledger: shine and shadow inside a window.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Companion Arcs and Party Banter|Companion Arcs and Party Banter]] lets a crisis land only if the detonating trait was planted in lived-through downtime, with one closing beat prepaid.

## Theme & the Spine

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Premise and Controlling Idea|Premise and Controlling Idea]] has every arc argue one causal proposition from a different position, proved by which value wins, not by a speech.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Motif and Symbol|Motif and Symbol]] means more on the return in a charged scene than it did on the first plain appearance, and no line spends the meaning.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Seeding and Payoff|Seeding and Payoff]] keeps a long-gap setup alive only on a concrete repeated object and fires it on a held value; bookkeeping before inspiration.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Stakes Without Mortality|Stakes Without Mortality]] threatens something valued: the gap between expectation and result, not a mortal threat.

## Serialized Structure

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Tonal Modulation|Tonal Modulation]] lets a heavy beat land in proportion to the relief around it, puts breathers in the sequence, and never stacks three heavy readings.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Breaking the Story|Breaking the Story]] decides what happens, in what order, to whom, and treats that decision as a finished job before anyone writes pages.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/Structure for the Ear|Structure for the Ear]] builds the story out of sounds that carry information, because on a recording a person is only there while speaking or named.

## Worldbuilding for Character

- [[wiki/Story Craft/Setting as Character|Setting as Character]] is directional force through enacted habit, not narrator explanation.
- [[wiki/Story Craft/The Iceberg and World as Pressure|The Iceberg and World as Pressure]] lets a world earn its place only by pressing on people, through omission and hard limits, never explanation or power.

## Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling

[[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]] is a reveal schedule. A full arc stages at 250 characters. Interiority arrives as the language arrives, and the constraint becomes the instrument.

The map names the doors. The pages behind them do the craft. A map that hides the newest rooms has already failed, which is why three later doors sit on it now.
