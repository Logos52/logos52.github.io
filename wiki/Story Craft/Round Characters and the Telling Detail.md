## Sources

- E. M. Forster, *Aspects of the Novel* (1927). The surprise test (capable of surprising in a convincing way; never surprising is flat; unconvincing surprise is flat pretending to be round). The tragedy line: only round characters are fit to perform tragically for any length of time.
- Robert McKee, *Story*. Characterization as the observable surface; true character as what a choice under pressure reveals; the greater the risk, the truer the revelation.
