# Diagnosing a Character

This audit runs eleven fixed checks on a character build in the same order. The first check you cannot answer with on-page evidence is the fault. The pass grades structure, not prose: a character that passes every item can still read flat if the sentences are dull.

Use it on a lead who feels wrong, or on a principal before lock. Peripheral cast are supposed to fail most checks. A full pass takes twenty to thirty minutes and is not a standing gate on the whole ensemble.

For [[projects/tsumugu|Tsumugu]], diction is capped by the graded-reader schedule. Voice, contradiction, and plant chains are tested the same way, and only the vocabulary ceiling changes what can be said when.

## The eleven checks

Present means enough evidence on the page. Thin means some, not enough. Absent means none.

1. **Ghost** (one concrete backstory injury that installed a false belief. Event, never mood. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Wound and the Lie|The Wound and the Lie]] owns the chain.)
2. **Lie** (the false belief at page one, shown as behavior. A stated creed with no observable choice is not present.)
3. **Want versus Need** (external goal nameable and distinct from inner truth. If winning the Want would also grant the Need, the arc has no gap.)
4. **One defining contradiction** (shown before named. A trait pile is not a contradiction, because a character needs one interior tension spent on a costly choice. Provision against presence (envelope home versus hand refused) is the house teaching case.)
5. **Voice** (distinct diction, rhythm, and refused topics. Strip tags from a two-hander so a fresh reader has to assign lines. Under a Han ceiling, refusal and silence carry what rare words cannot.)
6. **Arc type** (named against the full cast. Must not duplicate the ensemble's dominant type. This check most often explains "well-built but thin.")
7. **Signature object or motif** (returns with work to do.)
8. **Plant-to-payoff** (crisis prepaid. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]] holds PLANT, SPARK, and PAYOFF.)
9. **Home lesson** (one scene owns the pivotal beat, not a bible line and not a slot shared three ways.)
10. **Web edges** (relationship-work and revelation-work to more than one other character. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]] is the ensemble those edges make.)
11. **Submerged backstory** (worked out, though most never ships.)

Leads may carry a **second axis**: a second contradiction with its own root, not another relationship on the same root. Support holds one memorable note ([將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html)'s rank gag, [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html)'s deadpan feed-first manner) so roundness budget stays on the two or three arcs that load-bear.

## Voice and roundness (what the checks assume)

**Voice** is default register under pressure, especially what the character will not say. One plain center in the cast ([沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)) lets marked registers read: clipped deadpan, bright exclamation runs, and four-syllable bursts. At ~250 shared characters, cap speaking voices at three or four per reading, and when the sentence is teaching new words, carry interiority through [[wiki/Story Craft/Interiority Through Action and Object|object and action]] instead of mannerism.

A character feels round when they can surprise the reader in a way that still fits who they are. One contradiction on a particular (cracked hands, uneaten mushroom, camera hoarding proof of a freedom feared) beats a page of backstory. Flat support is correct casting when the gag or note does relationship work without an arc ([將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html), adults held light).

## What to do with each mark

Thin → deepen that check. Absent → build the missing piece. "Strong, but duplicate type" → recast elsewhere instead of adding more texture on this character.

If every check is present and the character still reads thin, the fault is prose. If the fix list is longer than the character's page count, cut checks to rank. Two leads with near-identical marks point to an ensemble fault on [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]].

## Worked example: [星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html)

Symptom: "missing something I can't place." Historical pass, not a live scorecard.

Present: Ghost (dreaded Osaka OL future), Lie ("the assigned life can be outrun"), Want vs Need (escape versus being known and rooted), voice (bright Kansai, 超/真的假的, camera), signature object.

Thin then: defining contradiction (brightness over dread on one beat), plant-to-payoff (trigger optional), home lesson (pivotal beat shared), web edges (only foil to [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html)).

Pattern: strongest *arc*, thinnest *scaffolding*. Felt thinness was not arc failure. Subsequent pass added second axis and edges. Cast cards illustrate people, and they do not carry these marks.

## Running the pass with an agent

Port as a prompt with an adversarial clause where flattering labels get challenged and inventions are labeled as proposals. Output includes the eleven checks with evidence, three to four candidate additions, six to ten show-not-tell texture beats, three to four designed interactions, submerged backstory, and a revised outline. The checklist does not write the character.

## How to practice this

1. Run the eleven checks in order, and stop at the first one you cannot answer.
2. Read checks 6 and 10 against the whole cast.
3. Sort marks: thin marks on 4, 7, and 11 point to scene texture, while absent marks on 3 or 6 point to structure.
4. Re-run the checks after revising to verify which marks actually improved from absent or thin to present.
5. Strip tags on one two-hander if check 5 is thin.

## Open questions
