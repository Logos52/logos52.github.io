# Diagnosing a Character

When a character reads as thin, the instinct is to reread the scene where they read thinly, and that is almost always the wrong place to look. Thinness travels: a piece missing from the build shows up scenes later as a person who does not appear to want anything. So instead of another pass you run a short fixed list — the same questions every time, in the same order — and the first one you cannot answer about this person is the fault. The list is short on purpose. Its value is that it ends somewhere specific.

The hole sits two pages over — a duplicated arc type, an unbuilt web edge, a payoff with no plant. This page grades a *build*. [[wiki/Story Craft/Reading Frames and What They Hide|Reading Frames and What They Hide]] is the neighbouring page for the other use of the word: a clinical frame on a *person* is the wrong instrument.

## The eleven checks

Each check is marked **present**, **thin**, or **absent**, against on-page evidence. Present means the evidence is on the page. Thin means some evidence, not enough. Absent means none. Checks 6 and 10 are cross-cast, not local. A character can pass every local check and still read thin.

1. **Wound / Ghost is a concrete backstory injury that installed the false belief.** Event, never mood. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Wound and the Lie|The Wound and the Lie]] owns the chain.
2. **The Lie is the false belief at page one, shown as behavior.** Same owner. A stated creed with no observable choice is not a mark of present.
3. **Want stands in for Need and fights it.** The external goal is nameable. If winning the Want would also grant the Need, the arc has no gap. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Change Arc|The Change Arc]] owns the test.
4. **One defining contradiction, shown before it is named.** A trait pile is not a contradiction. [[wiki/Story Craft/Round Characters and the Telling Detail|Round Characters and the Telling Detail]] owns the distinction.
5. **Voice is distinct in diction, rhythm, tics, and refused topics.** A line is assignable before the tag. [[wiki/Story Craft/Character Voice|Character Voice]] owns the print.
6. **Arc type is named, and it does not duplicate the cast's dominant type.** This is the single check that most often explains "well-built but thin." It is readable only against the full cast. [[wiki/Story Craft/Arc Types|Arc Types]] owns the five shapes.
7. **A signature object or motif returns.** [[wiki/Story Craft/Motif and Symbol|Motif and Symbol]] owns the return.
8. **A plant-to-payoff chain is on the page.** The crisis is prepaid. [[wiki/Story Craft/Companion Arcs and Party Banter|Companion Arcs and Party Banter]] owns the plant in downtime.
9. **The crisis owns a home lesson.** A home lesson is the scene that owns the pivotal beat — not a bible assertion, and not a slot shared three ways. [[wiki/Story Craft/Spotlight Rotation|Spotlight Rotation]] owns the window.
10. **Web edges are built to more than one other character.** A web edge is a relationship that does relationship-work and revelation-work at once. [[wiki/Story Craft/Foils and Pairings|Foils and Pairings]] is one scene doing both; [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]] is the ensemble those edges make.
11. **Submerged backstory is fully worked out.** Most of it never ships. [[wiki/Story Craft/The Iceberg and World as Pressure|The Iceberg and World as Pressure]] owns the mass.

Lead-only extra, not a twelfth check: a second axis. A second axis is a second contradiction with its own root, not a relationship on the same root. Leads carry it. Peripheral characters do not.

## How to read the marks

Thin wants deepening. Absent wants building. "Strong, but a duplicate" is a recast elsewhere, not more texture here.

A cluster of thins on 4, 7, and 11 is texture, and texture is cheap. Absent on 3 or 6 is a structural rewrite. The arc-type check is readable only against the full cast; a local pass on every other item does not excuse a duplicate type.

## The surround, not the arc

[星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) is the method example, read as it stood on the pass, not as a live scorecard. The felt symptom was "missing something I can't place."

Wound was present: the dreaded Osaka OL future, the un-burdensome-daughter household. Lie was present: "The assigned life can be outrun." Want versus Need was present: Want was escape — visa hacks, another year — and Need was to be known and rooted. Voice was present: bright, quick, genki, Kansai; 欸／超／真的假的; vlog narration; drops flat when seen. Signature object was present: the camera that hoards proof of a freedom she fears losing.

The then-diagnosis on type was disillusionment, and it marked **strong**. That label is historical. It is not re-asserted as current, and she is not typed here as the cast's only non-positive. Defining contradiction was thin: bright-over-dread carried by one beat, the camera. Plant-to-payoff was thin: the triggering beat was still optional. Home lesson was thin: the pivotal beat shared its scene with two other leads. Web edges were thin-to-absent: only [星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) ↔ [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html) was built. Submerged backstory was absent.

The pattern: the *arc* was strongest; everything *around* the arc was thin. Felt thinness was not the arc failing. Both structural gaps closed in the authoring pass that followed — a second axis, several new edges. The cast cards illustrate the people. They do not carry these marks.

## What the pass costs

The checklist ports to a fresh agent as a prompt. The adversarial clause is load-bearing: flattering labels are called into question. Everything invented is a labeled proposal.

The prompt produces six things, against the project's canon files: (1) the eleven-check with on-page evidence; (2) three to four candidate additions; (3) six to ten shown-not-told texture beats; (4) three to four designed interactions; (5) submerged backstory, most of which never ships; (6) a revised outline. The checklist diagnoses. It does not write. A character that passes all eleven can still be lifeless if the prose is flat.

The audit biases toward leads. A peripheral character is supposed to fail most checks. Audit depth matches rank. A full pass takes twenty to thirty minutes. It earns that cost on a felt-off character, or on a lead before lock. It is not a standing gate on the whole cast. A project-level pass of the same audit exists; this page is the portable form.

Quit signals:

- Every check present and the character still thin — the problem is prose, not structure.
- The fix list is longer than the character's page count — the pass is over-scoped; checks cut to rank.
- Two leads return near-identical marks — the fault is ensemble, and belongs to [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]].

Checkable: "thin, can't place why" should return at least one **absent**. If every check is present, the symptom was misread. A second pass should move the worked marks up a grade. If the marks do not move, the additions were decoration.

The list finds holes. It does not write the person.
