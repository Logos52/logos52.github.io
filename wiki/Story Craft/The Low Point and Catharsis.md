# The Low Point and Catharsis

## Sources

Aristotle, *Poetics*: peripeteia, anagnorisis, katharsis. Public domain. No translation camp is picked for katharsis.

K.M. Weiland, the free series starting at [Character Arcs (Part 1)](https://www.helpingwritersbecomeauthors.com/character-arcs-1/): midpoint glimpse and third-act low, held apart.

Blake Snyder, *Save the Cat!*: All Is Lost, Dark Night of the Soul, whiff of death.

Christopher Vogler, *The Writer's Journey*: Ordeal.

Joseph Campbell, *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*: Belly of the Whale.

John Yorke, *Into the Woods*: Crisis as a knowledge turning point.

Robert McKee, *Story*: internal value-stakes.

Sophocles, *Oedipus Tyrannus*: the published picture, recognition that is the reversal.
