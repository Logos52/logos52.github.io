# Interiority Through Action and Object

Bring the reader to a feeling without naming it. Two channels do the work: a **belonging** that changes condition across returns, and a **gesture** for hands or eyes at the peak. The method began as a Tsumugu constraint because the first-book list has almost no feeling-words; it remains the better default even when every abstract noun is available.

## One object per lead

Book one sits near **250 Han characters**—actions and things, rarely emotions. Assign each lead one concrete noun from the floor vocabulary; re-show it in changed condition at least twice before payoff. One appearance never pays; three changed states across books is a full earn.

| Object | What changes |
|---|---|
| Mushroom (阮草) | How freely it is given; warmth in the giving—care vs. duty, unnamed |
| Camera (星野) | Proof of a life she is not sure she keeps |
| Sprout-green eyes (小圖) | Shape shifts with mood; face simplified like a comics amplification |
| Photo from home (沈文) | Subject in frame changes before explanation—provision without presence |

The **gap**—reach, miss, harder choice—stages in 250 characters: one wants presence, another gives envelopes and empty evenings; a second job turns tending into obligation. No abstract noun required.

Writers with full dictionaries still reach for the abstract line. Eliot’s **objective correlative** and Hemingway’s iceberg say the same thing: externals first, omission only from knowledge actually held.

## Vocabulary ramp and coverage

[[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]] schedules when interiority can be named. The same object spine deepens as lemmas arrive; withheld characters can themselves be reversals.

**Seeds** must be re-showable behavior or object, never a stated theme—[[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|Seeding and Payoff]] across long gaps.

Hold peak-emotion scenes near **~98% known words** (Nation 2000, small sample; later replications treat 95–98% as design band, not cliff). New-word clusters at the peak make readers decode where they should feel. Cap **three to four speakers** per audio-first reading; with a small cast the object carries what the ensemble cannot. [[wiki/Story Craft/Diagnosing a Character|Diagnosing a Character]] names who is round enough to hold an object arc.

## When to tell once

Pure showing at the lowest floor can lose readers. One plain sentence—「以前，家裡很安靜。」—is the cheap exception when the changed thing is not enough. It does not license naming every feeling the object was hired to carry.

Motif returns use the same machinery: 阮草’s mushroom changes **how** it is offered, not the noun; charged returns must still read as plain events to a first-pass reader who forgot the plant. [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Craft#Motif, symbol, and payoff|Motif discipline on the Story Craft hub]] forbids explaining the object on return—explanation spends what repetition built.

## Downtime and crisis pairing

[[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|Companion banter]] exists partly to plant objects in low stakes: a photo slid across a café table, a mushroom offered in jest, binoculars aimed wrong. Crisis spends those plants without new vocabulary. If the crisis introduces the object for the first time, interiority reads as authorial insert, not reader discovery.

## Lie and ghost on objects

[[wiki/Story Craft/The Wound and the Lie|The Wound and the Lie]] when the list has no creed-words: ghost object (kept letter, refused hand) introduced early, accounted late. The lie shows as behavior around the object—counting envelopes, refusing rides—not as a thesis statement the character would sign.

## Failure modes and checks

Atmosphere without want–obstacle–change is mood with no gap. Scenes that exist to drill the word list raise anxiety and block input—affect filter is contested theory, still a useful quit signal.

**Fix:** second showing or one-clause tell if test readers cannot state the feeling; cut or reload objects that change no choice; reload faint first-floor seeds that cannot be re-shown.

**Pass:** object arc resolves without naming the emotion; reader feels provision vs. presence without lecture.

## How to practice

1. Assign one floor-vocabulary object per lead; return it twice in changed condition before payoff.
2. Stage want–obstacle–change in ~250 字 with no feeling-words; let one missed reach be the gap.
3. Place hands/eyes at peak emotion; delete feeling labels unless step 5 fails.
4. Measure peak-scene coverage (~98% known words); relocate new lemmas to the calm open.
5. Test-read: reader states the feeling unaided; else add second showing or one-clause tell.
6. Plant object in calm [[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|companion]] scene; crisis must spend, not introduce, the noun.
7. Log each return (book, condition, choice bent)—no bent choice means cut or reassign.

## Case against pure show

Purists call any tell weak writing. Under a ceiling, a misplaced tell is cheaper than a lost reader; above the ceiling, objects should do the work because they cost one noun and pay across books. The discipline is **default show, priced tell**, not show as moral law.

## Open questions

Whether a one-clause tell at the first floor spends a later reveal or saves the scene.
