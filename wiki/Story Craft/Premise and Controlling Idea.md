# Premise and Controlling Idea

A **premise** is one causal sentence the story sets out to prove: `[trait] leads to [end]`, true inside this work only. The **controlling idea** is the same spine at the final act: **value** the climax leaves plus **cause**. If either needs two sentences, the spine is unclear or two stories are bundled.

Structure is the argument. Who wins at the climax is the proof; a speech about the moral gives the proof away.

## Core takeaways

- Ending shapes: idealistic (neg→pos), pessimistic (pos→neg), ironic (both) — ironic hardest; ensemble may stack ironic capstones.
- Climax order: battle → self-revelation → moral decision → new equilibrium. Theme lands in revelation and decision ([[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]] low point / catharsis beats).
- **Counter-idea** must be equally weighted or the work is propaganda.
- Four-corner opposition: hero, main opponent, two secondary opponents — distinct value-approaches, not echoes ([[wiki/Story Craft/The Character Web|The Character Web]]).
- Series: dominant sentence + named subordinates; restating one sentence thrice does not count.
- Objects deliver meaning without naming it; word-list is reveal schedule ([[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]]).

## Premise vs controlling idea

Specimens (Egri): poverty → crime; frugality → waste; ruthless ambition → self-destruction. Controlling idea specimens (McKee): justice prevails because…; love dies when pride outlasts need.

## Locked and open for Tsumugu

**Founding pair locked:** provision without presence starves the love it means to prove. Climax controlling idea: bond survives **changed** because presence replaces the failed fix and love is chosen again — not restored.

Climax mapping: battle (provision vs presence) → revelation (can be carried) → decision (presence over fix) → equilibrium (changed bond).

**Series-level sentence unlocked.** Candidate question (not picked): how a person meets a life they did not choose. Subordinates: founding pair sentence, care-over-capability thread — map honestly, do not flatten.

Cast answers (candidate question): [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) flees, camera proves; [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html) stays, sends six onward; [阿迪](/tsumugu/cast/adi.html) dodges workshop money; [蘇老師](/tsumugu/cast/teacher-su.html) faces end of life; [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) carries alone; [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) needs presence inside it. Sharpest collision: 星野 vs 林薇. [將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html): gag + payoff, no arc.

Objects: [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s mushroom, [金多恩](/tsumugu/cast/kim-da-eun.html)'s empty chair — correlative dies if prose names what they stand for. Plant/payoff rhythm on [[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]].

## Moral core (what a character will pay for)

Separate from story premise: a character's **moral core** is the short list of rules they pay for, not recite. Forged by events; tested by charging the rule against what they love most. Speech stating the core is a **receipt** only if scenes already paid; otherwise soapbox.

**Eight diagnostic shapes** (name after writing choice under pressure): Steadfast, Idealist, Oathkeeper, Steward, Tender, Maker, Redeemer, Hidden Law (concealed rule; contrast honest-dishonesty rogue who states rule, refuses virtue credit — deeper treatment was retired with merged pages).

Villains use same loop; belief rides on price paid, not morality of law.

House cores (compression, not verified by cast cards): 沈文 freedom/presence; 阮草 keep-alive-by-love; 星野 author assigned life; 金多恩 mend honestly; 阿迪 old inside new; 白龍 speak on drift; 小圖 care not capability. One shape per lead; support may run on wants.

Loop fails when test hits what they do not love, when law is costume, or when price never lands.

## Series scale without flattening

A multi-book run honestly carries **dominant sentence + subordinates**. Restating the founding-pair line every book is not series premise work — it is repetition. Subordinates stay named: care versus capability, assigned life versus authored life, presence inside constraint versus escape with proof. Each thread gets its own controlling idea at its own climax; the series sentence, when chosen, should predict how those climaxes rhyme, not replace them.

## Costs and quit signals

Stated premise enforces cut: scenes that work but do not argue get removed.

Quit: no one-sentence compress; climax pulls multiple values; character preaches moral; two cast members same answer; object explained.

Checkable: post-climax reader states value won and why unaided; removing a character breaks argument or proves they carried nothing.

Skip apparatus when discovering draft, mood/slice without argument, or series spine still open (this project's series sentence — valid skip).

## Check against objects

After a climax, list every load-bearing object (mushroom, chair, envelope, photo). If removing one does not change what value won, either the object was scenery or the controlling idea was never argued through action. For Tsumugu, the object test is the earliest falsifier — it fails at B1 before theme-words exist.

## How to practice this

1. Premise in one causal sentence; controlling idea as value + cause.
2. Map climax to battle → revelation → decision → equilibrium.
3. List four corners; distinct answers.
4. Find object naming — cut naming.
5. Name one moral core; locate forge event and priced test.
6. Reader recall after climax.
