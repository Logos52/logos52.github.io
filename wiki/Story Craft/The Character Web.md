# The Character Web

A character web designs the cast together: one question underneath, each member a **stance** (defensible answer played in behavior). Meaning comes from **collisions** between answers, not from any one speech. An umbrella packed unasked and an envelope sent home are two positions before the question can be named. That is the pattern [[projects/tsumugu|Tsumugu]] runs at A1.

## Core takeaways

- Controlling idea in one sentence: value plus cause (*when/if/because*). See [[wiki/Story Craft/Premise and Controlling Idea|Premise and Controlling Idea]] for spine lock and series-level splits.
- Minimum **four corners**: hero, main opponent, and two more value systems, rather than a hero-versus-villain slogan.
- Spotlight sets A/B/C thread weight rather than play order, budgeting one or two round beats per scene so shine is balanced by shadow.
- Speaker cap (3 to 4 voices per reading) forces pairs and rotates the cast across readings.

## The house question

Locked love-web sentence: **love given in a language the beloved cannot receive reads as no love, until each learns to receive the other's.**

[沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) shows care through physical presence (packing an umbrella without being asked), while [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) shows care through material provision (sending an envelope home and refusing help with his hands). Neither wins, and the series keeps the argument open.

Dual-spine test: central members should answer **love** and **authorship of an assigned life** in the same body, or the reader has two casts under one title. Both the presence lead and the provision lead carry both axes in this design.

## Collisions and corners

Theme lands when moral arguments collide in action rather than speeches. Each side's dispute is argued at full strength so there is no cheap winner, and the full statement may wait for wordless reconciliation once vocabulary allows.

Round members surprise convincingly, whereas flat members hold one note so leads have budget ([將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html), and light adults [蘇老師](/tsumugu/cast/teacher-su.html) and [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html)).

## Foils and pairings

Build: common premise (same loss, bind, goal) → split on method → show in parallel actions.

Founding pair: same family-hunger, provision vs presence, each reading the other as absence of love.

[星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) vs [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html): assigned life (flee with camera vs stay and repair), trading envy and gifts, with no tidy escape rewrite later.

[沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) vs [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html): reach vs close. [小圖](/tsumugu/cast/xiao-tu.html) vs [金多恩](/tsumugu/cast/kim-da-eun.html): care vs capability.

Common failures include a one-directional foil (prop rather than interior), an interchangeable ensemble where swapping names changes nothing, and over-resolution that flattens small wins.

## Playing at A1

Stance = action/object until words arrive. Front-load Han for key value terms so climax dialogue is readable. [[wiki/Story Craft/Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling|Story Under a Vocabulary Ceiling]] and [[wiki/Story Craft/Interiority Through Action and Object|Interiority Through Action and Object]] carry technique.

## Spotlight rotation

**Shine** (competence seen) needs **shadow** (cost or flaw), or loyalty beats read as mascot.

**A-story** carries plot and arc on the series theme, while **B/C** threads carry foils, ensemble, and breathers. Across a book band, set a falsifiable target for the A-line (such as double availability for the lead defined by presence), since repeating the same beat does not count.

**Runners** (~three beats): gags ([將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html) rank game) and bottle units as vocab breathers. **Ledger**: every B/C minute is A-line debt, so plant payoff heaviest across the vocabulary ramp.

Five rules (compressed): ration round beats, give one loyalty close per arc-worthy member, mark initials on the beat board to show gaps, let forced rotation yield in single-POV scenes, and place comedy beats after a crest rather than on it.

## Companion energy and banter

**Party banter**: a line fires because *both companions* are in the room, not because plot needed dialog. It registers against silence, because wall-to-wall chatter cheapens it.

**Bond never resets**: approval eases later trust checks without erasing them. Downtime plants habits, and stress spends them. Give one big arc-closing beat per load-bearing member (a cat gets a gag payoff rather than a full loyalty mission).

Plant → spark (curious friend one to two readings early) → payoff, shared with [[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]].

## Name-swap and schematic corners

When two members can trade justifying lines with no loss, the web has collapsed to one answer with two faces. When a corner exists only to fill Truby geometry (*mend versus make* on authorship, never paid in scene), keep it thin rather than invent glossy detail. The reader should feel a person missing rather than a lecture wearing a name tag.

## Failures and fixes

- Hero/villain only → slogan.
- Identical voices or values → interchangeable ensemble.
- Abstract schema instead of lived behavior → debate instead of cast.
- Two-hander or single-POV piece → skip four-corner tax.

Merge when a justifying line moves mouths with no loss. Redistribute when all four corners must share one room. Re-cast when test reader picks clean winner in value dispute. Reset when reader mid-run cannot name the question.

## Reader test

Mid-first-book, a reader who knows only concrete verbs and action vocabulary should still place [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) and [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) on opposite sides of care, because umbrella and envelope never share a justification speech. Failure: both read as "nice." Success: dispute feels like two rights.

## How to practice this

1. Write a one-sentence question and assign stances in behavior.
2. Count four corners and check both spines (love and authorship) on leads.
3. Draft a foil two-hander with one variable swapped in action.
4. Mark A/B/C on an episode row and verify B/C debt on the vocabulary ledger.
5. Cut banter until lines register against silence.
6. Ask mid-run reader to name the question.

## Open questions

Whether two spines stay one web for readers who only have action vocabulary at entry.
