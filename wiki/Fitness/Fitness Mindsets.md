# Fitness Mindsets

## Related pages

- [[wiki/Dimensions/Self-Regulation/How to shift your brain to be motivated (when you don't feel like it)|DFUZ]] gives four moves for starting one session when the feeling is absent. Each move has a matching rule here: noticing that a craving is a feeling, acting as the lean person, starting smaller than you want, and taking the snack out of the room.
- [[wiki/Concepts/The Accretion Frame|The Accretion Frame]] agrees that a missed day should cost nothing. It keeps the body untracked, while the tracking here runs for a learning period and then stops.
- [[wiki/Fitness/Movement as Accretion|Movement as Accretion]] rebuilds a movement habit around something that grows with each session.
- [[wiki/Fitness/The Treadmill Library|The Treadmill Library]] fills walking time with audio made for walking.
- [[wiki/Fashion/The Personal Uniform|The Personal Uniform]] keeps few things, each one used in daily life, with the look as a side effect. The dressing rules here arrive at the same wardrobe.
- [[wiki/Minimalism/Environment Design|Environment Design]] arranges a room so the wanted action is easy and the competing one takes one more reach.
- [[wiki/Dimensions/Mindset/Mindset, Condensed|Mindset, Condensed]] treats motivation as fuel for starting a habit. It calls settling for good enough a form of loss aversion, while the form rule here treats good enough as what keeps a person training.
