# The Treadmill Library

The Treadmill Library is a collection of audio made for one listener to play while walking or on a treadmill. Each piece is written at a density that can be followed during a workout, and once it has been played it joins a list that only grows. The library is the listening half of Movement as Accretion, a plan on this desk to make daily movement add to something that lasts.

## Takeaways

- Time spent walking is used for listening.
- Each piece is written for one listener.
- Each piece is paced to be followed while moving.
- The played list only grows, so a missed day removes nothing.
- Walking costs little mental energy, which leaves attention for listening.
