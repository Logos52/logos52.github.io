# 阮草 → the Tender (canonical)

After a long re-derivation (2026-07-17/18), we scrapped 阮草's old provision/receive arc and rebuilt her from scratch as **the Tender** — the Kiki of the cast, a burnout-and-recovery arc. Signed canonical by Wedge 2026-07-18. Authoritative card: `tsumugu-core/docs/companion/craft/RUANCAO-MASTER.md`.

**What she is now.** The Vietnamese student whose hands make living things thrive (mát tay, the real Vietnamese folk "cool hand"), staged on real expert mushroom cultivation. Her magic is powered by **love as the why**: tending from love makes the hand flow (effortless) and gives joy. Moral core, the Tender's creed: (1) everything alive can be kept alive, and love keeps it alive; (2) tend from love, or it dies; (3) you are a living thing too — let yourself be tended.

**The lost-magic mechanism (the hard-won part).** Internal-first, clear causality — the load-bearing law Wedge kept restating: challenges derive from within; external factors are symptoms, never causes. The internal loss is the why **curdling from love to obligation** (the grind, the envelope) → the hand grips and forces → flow dies → joy dies → and a forcing hand cannot fruit a mushroom (*you cannot force a pin*), so **the barren blocks are the symptom, never the cause.** Outward faces: absence, worth-collapse, withdrawal. Recovery runs why→flow→joy: rest stops the forcing, being fed/seen loosens the grip, the first fruit comes when she tends from love again (clumsy). Permanent cost, internal: the innocent-effortless love is gone; adult-*chosen* love replaces it.

**Why this shape.** Mapped from Kiki's actual film losses (D joy, G flow/effortlessness, F the authentic why, with A/E/H as relational and self-worth faces) — Wedge ruled "all of it," and the three core losses resolve into one fire (love-as-why) with a single clean causal spine. Grounded in 322k-token research on the real du học sinh experience (`RUANCAO-MASTER.md` §, `DU-HOC-SINH-GROUND-TRUTH.md`).
