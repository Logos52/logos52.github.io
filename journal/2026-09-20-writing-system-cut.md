# The writing system, cut down

On 2026-09-20 the owner read the list of rules that applied when a model wrote a wiki page and said there were too many of them and that they were hurting the writing.

## What he ruled

- "only thing i really care about is no mannered prose."
- "i don't want human steps in my writing." No writing step waits for him. He reads the finished page.
- A page follows his style. It carries a chat text word for word only when he asks for that.
- Layout does not need checking. The prose does.
- Codex is gone. The models are Claude and Grok.
- "default mode is cut." "if you are not sure if it's necessary, it probably isn't." "everything we work on adds to bloat. it's time to change strategies and go lean."
- He keeps the Selfhood and two-egos idea on its wiki pages, "but maybe not as a generator."

## What is left

`02 - System/` went from 39 files and 69,355 words to 7 files. A writer reads `02 - System/Writing Standards.md` and `02 - System/Owner Writing Samples.md`. Political pages pass `02 - System/Bias QA.md`. Claude's steps are in the Explain First skill, and Grok's rules are in `/Users/n1/.grok/AGENTS.md`. `AGENTS.md`, `GROK.md` and `02 - System/Research Pipeline for AI Agents.md` were cut to what is still true.

Every generator file, Accepted Texts, Rewrite Prompt, Cold Read, Writing Pipeline, the QA docs and their scripts, the strike records, and the finished Grok handoffs were deleted. The tracked ones are in git history. `01 - Workbench/WRITING-PIPELINE-CATALOG.md` records what each writing method was and what he said about it. Four stale files that were not about writing are in `_archive/system-stale-2026-09-20/`.
