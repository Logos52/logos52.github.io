# The Story-Card Catalog

The Form card makes a character make sense; the Story card makes the arbitrary remainder stick. One master rule governs every entry below: the Story encodes only the residue the Form leaves — binding the components into one drawable gestalt, and pinning the part of the sound the Form's series fails to predict. [[journal/first-principles-of-the-story-card|The First Principles of the Story Card]] derives that rule and the two axes — 30 character situations crossed with 42 memory levers — this catalog resolves into 34 card-types. The levers come from the Modified Method of Loci and [[wiki/Learning Craft/Rules of Effective Memorization|Rules of Effective Memorization]]; the course material stays private, synthesized here. Five of the 34 are the built showcases from [[journal/story-cards-as-memory-scenes|Story Cards as Memory Scenes]]; the rest are open.

**How to use it.** Read the character's situation off its Form — composition type, how far the reading drifted, which component is empty or worn or doing double duty — and pick the archetype whose residue matches. A character often fits more than one (a phono-semantic with a heavy drift and a worn radical at once), so compose them. The delivery mechanics in the last group set card FORMAT and are orthogonal to glyph type; they layer on top of any content archetype. This is a living list, kept for a future authoring agent to select from per character and to extend the day a situation arrives that none of these fit.

## By how the glyph is composed

**Pictograph see-it-whole** (NEW) — *Residue:* one shape, nothing to bind; fix abstract strokes as one recognizable picture, anchor its own sound only if it donates one. *Levers:* ancient-pictograph recovery + draw-as-anchor. *Example:* 木 splays roots and branches into one still; no binding, no sound-pin.

**Indicative mark-placement** (NEW) — *Residue:* where the locating mark sits and what it points at; the meaning lives in a position. *Levers:* salience on the mark's placement (the placement-salience sub-lever is a design extrapolation; loci sources only "absurd") + spatial distinctness. *Example:* 本 — stage the tree, then make a glowing stroke at its foot the whole point.

**Meaning-meaning interdependence** (Showcase 4) — *Residue:* the interdependent action that fuses two meanings; arbitrary glue, no sound to pin. *Levers:* bind into one gestalt + interdependence. *Example:* 美 — the 羊 crown is worn and danced with by 大 in one moment; recall is meaning only.

**Depictable sound + salience** (Showcase 5) — *Residue:* bind a meaning-radical to a drawable sound-piece and pin a family-crossing drift. *Levers:* salience on the answer-bearer (a design extrapolation off loci's "absurd") + absurdity. *Example:* 動 — 重 is a house-sized boulder shoved by 力 the flexed arm; the giant piece flags itself as the {d,zh} sound-bearer.

**Bare-glyph fix** (Showcase 1) — *Residue:* a sound piece with no native picture, plus a heavy drift to pin; skipping the phonetic loses the series and is forbidden. *Levers:* fabricate a picture for a picture-less piece + residual-sound pinning. *Example:* 衙 — 吾 becomes a self-important official thumping his chest saying "ME," and the scene pins wú→yá.

## By the sound residue

**Sound-silent bind** (NEW) — *Residue:* binding only; the reading is fully predicted by the donor, so the Story has zero sound residue. *Levers:* arbitrary-residue-only scoping + bind into one gestalt. *Example:* 然 — bind 肰 + 灬, fire under meat, and spend zero beats on the reading, since 肰 gives rán exactly.

**Series-member sound-near-free** (NEW) — *Residue:* the binding plus the swapped meaning-radical; the sound is shared family knowledge. *Levers:* connection density + bind into one gestalt. *Example:* 戴 — bind the distinctive top to 𢦏 and lean on 載栽裁 so the reading arrives near-free; name the series.

**Phonetic-donor sound-anchor** (NEW) — *Residue:* anchor this character's sound cleanly because daughter characters depend on it, even where its own modern meaning is a loan. *Levers:* connection density as series machinery + salience (design-extrapolated). *Example:* 方 — pin its sound as the root of 放房訪防, kept separate from its own contested boats/plow picture.

**Auditory / jingle sound-series card** (NEW) — *Residue:* sound-series cohesion carried without a drawing. *Levers:* auditory/verbal mnemonic + connection density. *Example:* a short rhyme stringing same-series characters gives the group an auditory retrieval structure; the depictability gate does not block it.

## By the meaning residue

**Attribute encoding** (Showcase 2) — *Residue:* a sense feature — size, color, manner — that none of the components encode. *Levers:* dual coding / attribute encoding + multisensory/affective salience. *Example:* 麋 — the deer is staged oversized so "large marsh deer" recalls with the scene; 米 carries the near-free tone.

**Historical meaning-bridge** (NEW) — *Residue:* an opaque sense-drift where a component looks unrelated to the modern meaning. *Levers:* historical meaning-bridge dramatization + interdependence. *Example:* 漢 — run 氵 through river → its people → dynasty as one visual progression so the water earns its place.

**Phonetic-loan rebus card** (NEW) — *Residue:* the picture predicts nothing about the modern meaning; a pure sound-borrow. Bridging the picture to the loaned meaning is forbidden. *Levers:* historical meaning-bridge + 假借 loan-mark discipline. *Example:* 來 — draw the wheat-plant, MARK "come" as a loan sense (Outlier's 〇) so no false bridge forms; the rebus-chain itself is the mnemonic.

**Polyphone single-scene** (NEW) — *Residue:* one drawable scene carrying two sound-and-sense targets. *Levers:* bind into one gestalt + one recall target per card. *Example:* 便 — one scene on the shared sense, careful not to lean toward only biàn or pián; the reading split lives in `meanings.byReading`.

**Non-redundancy re-angle** (Showcase 3) — *Residue:* the redundancy risk itself; the Form pre-spent the obvious scene, with an opaque sense-drift left over. *Levers:* arbitrary-residue-only scoping + interdependence. *Example:* 領 — drop the Form's collar-derivation; restage 令 as a barked command-tablet whose order turns the 頁 head.

## By a problem component

**Empty-component card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a stroke-group carrying neither meaning nor sound. Three legal moves: drop it, repurpose it as a free prop, or render it as glass. *Levers:* glass/transparent rendering + salience triage. *Example:* 射 — render the empty "body" as a see-through glass figure so its non-functional status is in the image; 沒 — drop the worn 殳.

**Worn-component card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a component the student cannot find in the modern glyph, worn into other strokes. *Levers:* known-host anchoring + spatial distinctness. *Example:* 春 — use 屯 only because the Form's detail callout pins where it wore to; the scene rides the three top strokes as drawn, sun beneath.

**Dual-route component card** (NEW) — *Residue:* one piece doing double duty, meaning AND sound; show only one role and the other retrieval route is lost. *Levers:* dual coding + multi-directional reconstruction. *Example:* 堅 — 臤 grips so hard it reads "holds firm" while its qiān is flagged as the sound; one piece, both routes.

**Recurring-radical persona consistency** (NEW) — *Residue:* the radical's reusable identity, valid only when cast from the component-sense and only when it survives every sibling. *Levers:* fixed personas + component-sense casting + cross-sibling consistency check. *Example:* 亻 is always a standing person across 休/便, cast from component-sense, validated against siblings, recombined into fresh action each card.

**Same-shape false-friend / positional-allograph card** (NEW) — *Residue:* the same shape with a different origin per character, or per position; the risk is blindly reusing one persona. *Levers:* same-shape guard + component-sense casting. *Example:* 阝 — a hill when on the left (阜), a city when on the right (邑); never inherit one persona across the false friend.

## Whole-glyph special cases

**Repeated-component multiplicity card** (NEW) — *Residue:* the multiplicity itself is the meaning — many, forest, crowd. *Levers:* decompose, one element per piece, + distinctiveness. *Example:* 森 — three 木 at different sizes and angles so the count reads as "forest"; the multiplicity is the recall target.

**Function-word use-slot card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a grammatical function with no picturable meaning. *Levers:* permutation / use-case mapping + reverse explanation. *Example:* 的/了/把 — stage a tiny acted use-case, the slot the particle fills in speech; the recall target is the function.

**Confusable-pair discrimination** (NEW) — *Residue:* the risk of mistaking the glyph for a near-twin; the distinguishing stroke is the residue. *Levers:* contrastive encoding + salience. *Example:* for a glyph one stroke off its twin, dramatize the distinguishing stroke as the decisive prop so the difference is the recall route.

**Simplified-divergence independent scene** (NEW) — *Residue:* a second, independent set of components to bind; the Simplified glyph can parse completely differently. *Levers:* self-contained scene (design-extrapolated) + fabricate a folk picture. *Example:* 辦/办 — author the 办 scene from its own pieces (八 = sweat, folk), never referencing 辦's frame.

**Folk-parse encoding card** (NEW) — *Residue:* the true parse leaves an unmemorable residue and a folk picture binds better. Folk-picturing is product editorial policy, not a sourced lever. *Levers:* fabricate folk picture + cross-sibling consistency check. *Example:* 动 — read 云 as a cloud (folk:true) heaved by 力, marked folk, no corrective language, kept only if it survives the series.

## Effort economy, anchoring, and verdicts

**Story-null verdict** (NEW) — *Residue:* near-zero; the Form owns both binding and sound. Story-null is product policy built on the sourced salience-triage principle. *Levers:* salience triage + arbitrary-residue-only scoping. *Example:* 百 — the numeral diagram plus sound leave no honest residue, so story:null; a forced scene would manufacture redundancy.

**Leech re-encode** (NEW) — *Residue:* after repeated failures the single scene is a demonstrated encoding defect that resists default treatment. *Levers:* lapse escalation / rule-of-3 + connection density. *Example:* a character failing ~3 reviews gets escalated to its sound-series root, contrast set, and use cases rather than its original scene re-run.

**Known-host anchoring card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a sticky component the learner already met inside a daily-use character. *Levers:* known-host anchoring + connection density. *Example:* 嫩 — pin the stubborn right side as "the top of 整," a character written daily, importing its stability.

**Live-vocabulary sound-pin** (NEW) — *Residue:* a reading best locked through a word the learner already speaks. *Levers:* live-vocabulary sound-pinning + kinesthetic/articulatory salience. *Example:* build the scene around a daily phrase, say it aloud, and feel the mouth-place to lock a cross-family reading.

**L1 cognate-bridge card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a component whose meaning or sound maps onto the learner's first language. *Levers:* L1 metaphor / cognate bridge + historical meaning-bridge. *Example:* 力 — route through English "plowed through his work" so the plow→effort extension rides a phrase the learner already owns.

## Delivery and format mechanics

These set card FORMAT and combine with any content archetype above.

**Production / writing-direction card** (NEW) — *Residue:* recall of the written FORM from speech, the reverse of every glyph→meaning card. *Levers:* production / form-generation + multi-directional reconstruction. *Example:* given the spoken word, cross-match a candidate sound-component against a candidate meaning-component to produce the glyph (the IME method).

**Attribute-edge multi-card set** (NEW) — *Residue:* one character delivered as separate edges. *Levers:* attribute-edge split + one recall target per card. *Example:* one character yields a form→meaning card and a form→sound card, staggered across days by pipelining.

**Drawn-image card** (NEW, constrained) — *Residue:* a card shipping a real sketch as content. *Levers:* draw-as-anchor + generative construction, held in tension. *Example:* ship an actual sketch of the scene, against the sourced tension that a supplied image risks reception over the representation the learner constructs.

**Guess-gated reconstruction card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a card delivered as a prompt-face against an answer-face. *Levers:* pretest / committed guess + generative construction. *Example:* show 衙's parts, the learner commits "courtyard? office?", then reveal "government office" plus the chest-thumping 吾; the miss cements it.

**Forward-planting two-way card** (NEW) — *Residue:* a phono-semantic wanting both recall directions. The forward-planting lever has no source basis and is kept as a design option. *Levers:* forward-planting (design extrapolation) + multi-directional reconstruction. *Example:* plant the meaning-radical early as an unexplained "why is that here?" that the arriving sound-piece resolves.

## What is new beyond the 5

The five showcases all bind components and pin a sound. The catalog opens room the showcases never touched:

- Non-redundancy on the SOUND axis — the exact-match phonetic (然) spends ZERO sound beats, since the series owns the reading entirely.
- Dual-role components (堅's 臤) encoding both the meaning route and the sound route from one piece.
- Empty (空) components get three legal moves, not two: drop, repurpose, or render as glass (射).
- Worn/invisible components (春's 屯) ridden through the Form's detail callout for a piece the student cannot see.
- Donor-vs-member series logic: anchor a productive donor's sound as machinery (方) versus letting a member inherit it near-free (戴).
- A fixed-persona registry for recurring radicals, bounded by three guards: component-sense ≠ character-sense (口 = "sound," not "mouth"); same-shape ≠ same-identity, identity by position (left 阝 = hill, right 阝 = city; the 𡗗 family); and the cross-sibling consistency test (a persona must survive every sibling, which also gates folk parses).
- Contrastive cards built around the distinguishing stroke of a confusable pair.
- Known-host anchoring (嫩→整) and live-vocabulary sound-pinning.
- The auditory channel — rhyme, alliteration, meter, jingle — a non-drawable mnemonic the strict depictability gate wrongly excluded.
- Kinesthetic/articulatory salience (the {d,zh} same-mouth-place self-test) and L1 cognate bridges as distinct sound-pinning and elaboration levers.
- Phonetic loan (假借) handled precisely: mark the loan sense (〇), forbid bridging the picture to the loaned meaning, let the rebus-chain carry it.
- Polyphone single-scene handling (便) and historical meaning-bridge dramatization (漢: river → people → dynasty).
- Story-null as a first-class authoring VERDICT (百, 智力) — product policy built on the sourced salience-triage principle.
- The Simplified-edition axis (辦/办) — a second independent scene from the Simplified pieces only.
- Three new situation classes: repeated/multiplied identical components (森, where the meaning IS the multiplicity), function-word characters (的/了/把, encode a use-slot), and positional allographs (role selected by position).
- Delivery mechanics: guess-gating with hypercorrection, reverse-explanation reveal order, the attribute-edge split into multiple staggered cards, the production/writing-direction card, and shipping an actual drawn image.
- Effort-economy moves: salience triage, the lapse-escalation/rule-of-3 loop, and permutation/use-case mapping as routine elaboration.

## Recommended next showcases

The eight cards that earn building next, each demonstrating a capability the first five lack:

1. **Sound-silent bind (然)** — the single most load-bearing demonstration: a card that deliberately spends zero sound beats, proving non-redundancy on the sound axis. High-frequency, the cleanest counterweight to the five.
2. **Empty-component glass card (射)** — the third, source-named treatment of 空 components the synthesis originally missed.
3. **Dual-role component (堅's 臤)** — one piece carrying both routes; easy to author wrong by silently dropping a route, so high value to standardize.
4. **Story-null verdict (百 / 智力)** — teaches restraint: when not to author a scene at all.
5. **Confusable-pair discrimination** — contrastive encoding around the distinguishing stroke of a near-twin, a lever absent from the five.
6. **Cross-sibling-validated persona card (the 阝 hill/city false friend)** — exercises the persona guards the single-character showcases never reach.
7. **Auditory/jingle sound-series card** — a deliberately non-drawable mnemonic for a phonetic family, correcting the over-strict depictability gate.
8. **Production/writing-direction card (IME method)** — the reverse recall direction, speech → glyph, the glyph→meaning showcases never enumerate.
