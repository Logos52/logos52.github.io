# Pages Read as Posts

Ruling (Wedge, 2026-08-10): the blog register's voice — working notes made legible — becomes the default register for wiki pages and personal pages going forward. The opening is the post's whole: a normalizing run-up with the thesis landing inside the opening paragraph, never a cold first-sentence claim. Flow outranks compression. WPW stays, everywhere.

The trigger was the first Personal-section draft (The Two Meanings of Ego), whose wiki-register opener read too heavy. The stated reason reaches past that page: the reader served is the one not versed in the dense style, and the dense style was making the whole site read like anyone could have built it. First scoped to personal pages, then widened the same hour: "shift to blog post style writing as a global setting for all of these pages moving forward aside from specific instances like Condensed pages, MOC etc."

Scope as codified in Writing Standards §The Default Register: voice shifts, honesty stays — the High-Signal content disciplines (Specifics, Case Against, Boundaries, Quit Signals, Checkable Expectations, No Scaffolding, Delete List) still bind wiki pages. Forward only, on the WPW clause: new pages and substantial rewrites; no retrofit pass.

Keeping their own registers: Condensed Pages, MOCs and hub/index pages, decision documents (Verdict First), chrome and taglines (catalog lines).

Ruled later the same day, the continuous-generation repair law. The ego page's first literature build read as mechanical — research-bank entries showing through, authorities filing past at one sentence each, paragraph joints announcing their own function. The accepted fix was a full re-telling: every section regenerated as one continuous explanation and filtered after, with the warmer on-ramp Wedge picked from two candidates. On his direction ("write down your fix… i kind of want all posts to follow this format") the method is codified into Writing Standards §Blog Posts Are Working Notes Made Legible: regenerate, never patch; terms after the experience they name; sources enter a telling; joints pick up threads. It binds every post and, through the default register, wiki and personal pages with them.

That closes the exemption question — the exception class is the special pages, Condensed the named example, and the four listed above stand as its members.
