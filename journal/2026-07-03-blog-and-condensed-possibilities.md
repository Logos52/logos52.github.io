# Blog and Condensed possibilities — the full menu

A picking list, not a queue. Everything here is unwritten; nothing starts without Wedge choosing it. Two earlier lists feed the same pipeline: the original Learning Science × Chinese backlog (2026-06-30, all 18 now authored) and the first additions list (2026-07-02, 21 candidates, none authored). This list adds the rest of the vault's teachable material. Format: title — takeaway · owner page(s). Items with no public owner are flagged; per source discipline, an owner page comes before the post.

## Cluster D — Deep processing and encoding

**D1. Commit Before the Content** — a real attempt before the answer flips practice transfer from roughly 1:1 toward 10:1; the general version, beyond characters · Problem-First Learning, Prestudy.

**D2. The Bear Hunter Loop** — aim, shoot, skin as the general study cycle: questions first, answers hunted, the map cleaned into retrievable structure · Bear Hunter System (the language version is already authored; this is the study-wide one).

**D3. Hipshot** — the fast variant of the loop for material that doesn't warrant the full hunt · Hipshot.

**D4. Thinking on Paper** — representations you construct are the encoding; visible activity that bypasses the thinking is the trap · Thinking on Paper, The Shortcut Problem.

**D5. The Shortcut Problem** — highlighting, re-reading, and copying feel like work precisely because they skip it · The Shortcut Problem.

**D6. Relationships, Not Collections** — encoding is grouping, connecting, ordering; a pile of facts is not a schema · Schema Construction, Importance-Based Chunking.

**D7. Shape It Before You Store It** — memory handling: information gets reshaped for encoding and retrieval before any drilling starts · Memory Handling.

## Cluster E — Retrieval and review

**E1. A Session With No Failures Was Confirmation** — spacing exploits partial forgetting to reveal true gaps; zero failed recalls means the test taught nothing · Spaced Interleaved Retrieval, Retrieval.

**E2. Reconstruction Sits Above Recall** — forming and judging new relationships among known nodes; curveball immunity lives there, not in replayed answers · Reconstruction: Retrieval Beyond Recall.

**E3. Real Life Schedules Reviews Too** — retro-label each real-world rep, patch what use missed, study around upcoming work · Opportunistic Retrieval.

**E4. Repair Gaps, Don't Re-Rep Them** — an item that keeps failing indicts the encoding, not the schedule · SIR gap repair, Retrieval.

## Cluster F — Regulation, mindset, energy

**F1. The Mt-Stupid Test** — two questions plus a conditions check calibrate confidence; judge multi-week trends, never one session · Confidence Calibration.

**F2. Mindset Trains Behaviorally** — fixed versus growth moves through smaller cheaper mistakes, never affirmations · Fixed vs Growth Mindset.

**F3. Spend Attention on Process Only** — outcomes are symptoms; the controllable layer is where attention pays · Locus of Control.

**F4. Silly Mistakes Have Causes** — execution errors are diagnosable events, not character flaws · Silly Mistake Syndrome.

**F5. Detachment Is a Skill** — psychological detachment during rest decides whether rest restores · Recovery, How to Maintain Sustainable Energy Under Pressure.

**F6. Measuring Learning Without Fooling Yourself** — what to track when scores mislead · Measuring Learning, Opening the Black Box of Learning.

## Cluster G — Self-management systems

**G1. Priority 0+1** — protect one to three priorities absolutely; the plan must survive a bad day · Priority 0+1 System, Building a Schedule That Survives.

**G2. Procrastination Is a System Problem** — the fix is structural, not moral · Procrastination: a System Problem.

**G3. The Energize Test** — a filter for commitments · The Energize Test.

**G4. Flow Is Protected, Not Summoned** — attention management as defense · Flow State, Attention Management: Preserving Flow.

**G5. The Unimpressive Checkpoint** — 14-day targets sized so the worst case still hits them; momentum over ambition · The 30-Day Plan.

**G6. Unlearning Comes First** — old habits get dismantled before new ones hold · How to Unlearn Old or Bad Habits Efficiently.

## Cluster H — Language beyond characters

**H1. The Noticing Game** — giving attention a concrete target when input is barely comprehensible · Noticing Game.

**H2. Comprehension Is Adjustable** — subtitles, transcripts, repeats, and familiar topics move the same material closer to reach · Hacking Comprehension Menu.

**H3. Lookups With a Destination** — interactive immersion: what separates a lookup that sticks from one that evaporates · Interactive Immersion.
