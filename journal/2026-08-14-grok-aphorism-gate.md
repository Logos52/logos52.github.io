# Grok needs its own aphorism gate (2026-08-14)

**Claude's 2026-08-13 stance works for Fable. It does not control Grok.** Grok compresses the bank's "operating claim" into a quote card, passes its own pillow test, and ships. The checker only counted words. That is why the class came back after we thought it was dead.

Owner named eight, then kept finding the same object. Fifty-two intros held, rewritten from inventory, promoted. The durable fix is not more stance prose in the write contract.

**Grok-specific gate, installed the same day:**

- Live models: `/Users/n1/Projects/llm-knowledge-base/01 - Workbench/regen-2026-08/GROK-OPENERS.md` (struck → replacement).
- Assembly: droplog `## Inventory` of five CARRY nouns *before* any page sentence; sentence one must use one. Operating claim is not sentence one.
- Checker: `opener.aphorism_specimen` (hard, validated on the eight named strikes / eight replacements) and `opener.inventory` (hard when a droplog exists).
- Skeletons may no longer write "the operating claim is [epigram]." They write `s1-material:` as five nouns.

Flip condition: if Grok starts stuffing inventory nouns into a still-shapely card ("The chair earns its place"), the inventory check is being gamed and the specimen list has to grow from a new named strike, not from reasoning.

Cost: one extra droplog section and a checker fail that Fable never needed. Cheaper than another 52-page rewrite.
