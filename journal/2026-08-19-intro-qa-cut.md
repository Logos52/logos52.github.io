# Intro QA cut: five tests, a script, on-ramp off the battery

Verdict: the intro gate is now five judgment tests and a script. Who-cares and transition stay. On-ramp comes off the battery. The other fourteen names were the same habits in other clothes, or they fought a test we are keeping. The loop that minted them is the prohibition loop. The page is [[02 - System/Intro QA|Intro QA]]; the script is `scripts/intro_qa.py`.

## What is kept

Who-cares. Transition, with referent and ellipsis inside it. Stacking — his word, not "one claim," because a paragraph can have one thesis and still be a brick of biography. Pillow — one epigram test. Selfhood — brag, "I hold," criterion leak, answering a charge nobody made.

A script, fail-closed, no model: `100%`, fully/completely/entirely, life-on-the-line, `I hold` / `I believe` / `I treat it as`, `rather than` and `not X but Y` except in an owner quote, the smoothed white/Asian pair, money words. Those reappeared after being named because a model critic would not fail them.

## What is killed, and why

On-ramp as a named test. It fights who-cares. Who-cares kills the empty ease-in; on-ramp kills the blunt true sentence; together they produce the avoidant wording already struck (*was the hardest thing on offer, and it ended second in the class*). Ease-in stays in the generator. *Why should I care* is who-cares. *Too compressed / caveman / big step* is stacking.

Folded, not forgotten: delete and setup-and-payoff into stacking; specific-beats-generic into who-cares; stance verbs, signposts, defensive, criterion leak into selfhood or who-cares; antithesis, cadence, metaphor into pillow. Cadence as its own detector already failed on 2026-08-13: it scored the accepted openings highest.

## Steelman of keeping nineteen

Each name was minted from a real strike. Cutting them means a costume can reach his eye. That is the cost, and it is cheaper than another day of obedient new failures. A killed test returns if the same surface happens twice on work that already passed the five.

## What this is not

Not a license to ship a Positions intro. There is still no accepted generated intro in that domain. Not a change to the locked 16:18 wiki generator. Not a claim that CLEAN equals good.
