# Blog backlog — additions

New post candidates beyond the 2026-06-30 Learning Science × Chinese set, in three clusters: Tsumugu-adjacent (the dictionary's doctrine as teachable ideas, never as product copy), learning science from the wider wiki, and systems/agents/money/design. Each line: working title, the takeaway, the owner page(s). Voice: the locked blog register in `02 - System/Writing Standards.md`.

## Cluster A — Tsumugu-adjacent (character craft)

**1. Two Tellings**
When scholarship carries two usable pictures for a character's origin, tell both declaratively; two vivid tellings beat one suppressed one, and hedge words beat neither. The 方 precedent (boats and plow). · Grounded in: tsumugu PRD-Entry-Authoring uncertainty ladder; Form Components (contested origins).

**2. The Wrong Story That Works**
A historically wrong parse that encodes the character faster can be the better story, provided it is marked as folk and survives the sibling test's damage check. Where encoding power and accuracy part ways, and how marking reconciles them. · Grounded in: tsumugu STYLE-CARD (stories judged by encoding power); Meaning Components (cross-character consistency).

**3. The Unnamed Phonetic Is Lost Cargo**
Skipping the sound component loses the character's whole prediction series: 戴's 𢦏 carries 載, 栽, and 裁. Naming the phonetic is not pedantry; it is keeping the machinery. · Grounded in: Sound Series; Sound Components; tsumugu QA offenders list.

**4. One Character, Two Trees**
Polyphones stop being chaos when each reading gets its own meaning tree: 長 as cháng and zhǎng runs two clean genealogies from one graph. · Grounded in: Meaning Trees and Original Meanings.

**5. X的X, Y的Y**
How Chinese speakers disambiguate homophones aloud, and why a dictionary entry for a spoken word should carry its disambiguation formula. · Grounded in: tsumugu PRD-Entry-Authoring (form.sayText); Three Attributes.

**6. Sentences Worth Learning From**
A vocabulary entry's example sentences serve three different jobs: producible now, common company, and sense extension; a set that only does one job teaches less than it looks. · Grounded in: tsumugu PRD-Entry-Authoring §2.5 sentence mix.

## Cluster B — Learning science (general reader)

**7. Teaching an Empty Chair**
Whole-part-whole reteaching to an imaginary student tests all six levels of understanding at once; expect it to be hard, and treat ease as the warning. · Grounded in: WPW.

**8. Explain First, Name Last**
The reverse explanation: build the relevance from what is already known, drop the label at the end, and the term lands connected instead of orphaned. · Grounded in: Reverse Explanation.

**9. Encoding Sets the Ceiling**
Review cannot rescue what first contact never structured; when an item keeps failing, suspect the encoding before the schedule. · Grounded in: Learning, Condensed; Retrieval.

**10. One Error Costs Four**
An error costs roughly 4x the time of doing the step right, and speed gains come from changing the process, never from rushing. · Grounded in: Accuracy Before Speed.

**11. Rest Before It Hurts**
Rest ≈ work ÷ 3, taken at the first dullness rather than at collapse; recovery scheduled early is cheaper than recovery demanded late. · Grounded in: OFF-Rest Timing.

**12. Decide Before the Session**
Repeated what-next decisions bleed a study block dry; reduce vague choices to closed questions before the block starts. · Grounded in: Decisional Delays; Choice Throttling.

**13. The Comfort Gauge**
Felt difficulty is the instrument: none means comfort-zone drift, and the reflex retreat when it spikes is the signature failure to train against. · Grounded in: The Learning Zone and the Reversion Response.

**14. Train the Return**
Attention drift inside a block is normal; what compounds is how fast it comes back, so the practice target is the return, not the streak. · Grounded in: Focus Management.

**15. Five Minutes Is a Session**
Prime, map, explore, dive, consolidate works in 5–30 minute fragments; small bursts are real sessions when one consolidated retrieval mission repairs the seams. · Grounded in: Microlearning System.

## Cluster C — Systems, agents, money, design

**16. Outsource Thinking, Never Understanding**
Agents generate options, code, and summaries; what cannot be delegated is the internal model that knows what is worth building and what is true enough. · Grounded in: Understanding Bottleneck; Agentic Engineering.

**17. Name What Would Change Your Mind**
A decision document that cannot state its flip condition has not finished deciding; steelmanning the rejected option is what separates choosing from rationalizing. · Grounded in: High-Signal Decision Writing; Positional Decisions and Expected Value.

**18. The Reversible Branch Wins**
When options converge on outcome, decide on retreat cost: what does backing out of each branch cost later. · Grounded in: Positional Decisions and Expected Value.

**19. The Price Is the Entry Fee**
Objects keep charging after purchase, in attention, space, maintenance, and future decisions; storage is deferred decision-making with interest. · Grounded in: Ownership Cost; Minimalism as Systems Design.

**20. Enough Is a Written Number**
Without a written "enough," every milestone resets the goalpost; the win condition has to exist before it can be reached. · Grounded in: Money, Condensed owners.

**21. Blame the Design**
A capable person defeated by an everyday thing is evidence against the design, and reading difficulty as data changes what a struggling user means. · Grounded in: Design of Everyday Things.
