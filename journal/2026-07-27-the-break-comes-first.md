# The Break Comes First

Three days of the same fight on the podcast production, wearing different clothes each day: the model made writing choices that collided with each other, details that did not ground in the research, drafts that read as if the authoring rules had never been written. Today produced a system proposal instead of another draft. The diagnosis underneath it is small: every one of those fights happened in the gap where structure and lines were being made at the same time, by the wrong party.

The proposed system borrows the television room's oldest discipline. The story is broken before it is written — Vince Gilligan puts roughly three quarters of the writing labor in the break — and the break happens on a board that is the only place structure lives. Each beat is a card carrying its function, its causal connective to the card before it, its source, and its weight; a card with no source is a hole holding a question, never something the model fills. Lines enter only after the board locks, supplied or directed by the human, with the model's connective stitches marked so they can be struck on sight. The anti-whack-a-mole mechanism is a reverse outline: after every drafting session, the draft's actual beat functions are re-derived and diffed against the board, so drift surfaces the day it happens instead of three drafts later. The full proposal lives as HOW WE WORK in the tsumugu-core craft tree, unsigned until ruled.

The research behind it folded into the wiki as three Story Craft pages: [[wiki/Story Craft/Breaking the Story|Breaking the Story]] on the room discipline, [[wiki/Story Craft/Structure for the Ear|Structure for the Ear]] on audio-only design, and [[wiki/Story Craft/The Beat Board|The Beat Board]] on the card as a unit of structure. One deliberate omission: the production's own structural laws were cited in place rather than copied into the system doc. This week's canon audit found 71 duplicated facts across the production tree, 16 of them already disagreeing with each other — a second home for a law is how a retired thing comes back.

Open, and staying visible: whether the system survives contact with day four. The doc records its own flip condition — structure rejected twice for one reason means the system failed at its own game, and the fallback is narrower, with the human dictating structure too and the model keeping only the bookkeeping and the machines.
