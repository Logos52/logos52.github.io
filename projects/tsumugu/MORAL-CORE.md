## THE SYMBIOSIS LAW (🔒 Wedge 2026-07-17)

> **Arcs and moral core have a symbiotic relationship. The arcs forge the moral core; the moral core is tested by the arcs.** Every great story keeps this engine at the center.

How it maps onto our structure:

- **Books 1–4 = the forge.** Each growth arc hammers a core out at rising cost — the value isn't stated, it's *priced* into the character beat by beat (show-don't-tell is the forge's grammar).
- **Book 5 = the test.** Values reflected: the war prices every core against its owner's oldest wound, and what the forge made either holds or breaks.
- **The flats walk in pre-forged.** 沈文's forging is *revealed* through B1–4 rather than performed (the Winter Soldier register — each mini-arc shows what a value costs him); 金多恩's forge is offstage entirely (the brother, the method). For the flats, the books are all test — and for 金多恩 the B5 test breaks the core and **re-forges** it: the test becomes a second forge. That is what break-and-remake means.
- **Every character card below carries a Forge → test line** — which arcs forge this core, and which test it.

## The model (governs every arc)

- **Constant core drive; the EXPRESSION matures shallow→deep.** Generative, never bleak. Every arc rides a superpower-adjacent skill. Every lead a distinct theme (ARC-REGISTRY enforces no-two-alike).
- **Two separate complete arcs per character:** Books 1–4 = their own arc; Book 5 = values REFLECTED onto them (Winter Soldier → Civil War — the first reframes the second, never subordinate).
- **The two flats:** 沈文 (flat across BOTH books — Cap) · 金多恩 (flat B1–4; her arc IS Book 5 — Tony).
- **The lanes guard:** seeing trio = 沈文 deep · 金多恩 true · 白龍 far. 阿迪 = the hands. 星野 = the light. 阮草 = cultivation. 小圖 = care. 林薇 = the keeper. The elders = the givers (蘇老師 repaid; 吳老闆 portable room).

## 沈文 — the flat-arc hero (Cap)

**Archetype:** the calm-center maker with the cast's most-solid moral core; the most present person in the room who lets no one all the way in.

**Moral core (all 🔒):**
1. **Integrity; freedom over control** — a system that buys safety by taking the wheel is fear in disguise. 「這不是自由，這是害怕。」 *(his B5 line)*
2. **Stewardship, never rejection** — the maker keeps the human hand on the wheel. 「最安全的手，還是我們自己的。」 + 「今天是妳。明天呢？換一個簽名，方向就換。」 Refusals courteous and total: 「謝謝。可是我不要。」
3. **Presence over provision** — you show up; presence can't be counterfeited. Origin: provided-for but alone (§4b-ii) — the counterfeit he was raised on is "I did it for you."
4. **Lets it go free** — his peace no longer depends on what the family gives back. Origin: 阮草's mirror — his found family reproduces his lost family's wound.

**Forge → test:** forged pre-series and through B1–4 at rising cost — the Winter Soldier: each Project-Insight mini-arc prices a value, and the 阮草 rift is the hardest hammer-blow (a loss no bench can fix). Tested totally in B5 — **and he WINS it (🔒 Wedge, reaffirmed 2026-07-17: the Cap-in-Civil-War win):** holds his line without gripping, keeps his people (his three follow him out because they are his), keeps 小圖, keeps the friendship across the fault line, walks out on his own terms — and the garage light comes on. Prices are paid along the way (the room at L07; the L09 wound); they are prices inside a won arc, never the arc lost. The flat's motion is *revelation*: the reader watches the forged thing get shown — and proven.

**B1–4 (🔒 flat; spine 🔒 2026-07-16):** builds the family he never had by hand (小圖), gathers the lonely (白龍), lets 阮草 adopt him — braced to lose it all. No transformation; the one bounded turn = the 阮草 rift (mini-arc F 🔒): he stops engineering past a loss that can't be fixed at the bench. B4L12 — the guy who always left without bleeding lets it hurt. Skill: the maker (robotics/AI) + sees DEEP. Mini-arcs C+F 🔒; A/B/D/E candidates ✳️.

**B5:** general of the garage fork (沈文+星野+白龍). Gathers, never recruits. Holds his line, never grips — right in the end, and **he wins**: the empire keeps the room; he keeps the people, 小圖 (carried out in the bag), and the friendship. L10: the garage light comes on — the win image. Prices paid on the way (the room at L07; the L09 wound), never the arc lost. Founding pair splits across the war and holds: 「公司是白天的事。」

**Growth theme:** he does not transform — the constant is building by hand and giving presence; the movement is bounded: at peace with what he has, he lets the family go free.

## 阮草 — the Tender (RE-BASELINED 2026-07-18 → RUANCAO-MASTER.md is canonical)

**Archetype:** female co-lead; the Vietnamese student whose hands make living things thrive — who tends from love until the grind curdles it into obligation and the gift goes dark. **The Kiki of the cast** (burnout-and-recovery arc). The old "generous provider who will not be helped" reading is superseded; provision survives only as ONE expression of tending-from-love.

**Moral core (the Tender's creed — all re-derived; master is authoritative):**
1. **Everything alive can be kept alive, and love is what keeps it alive.** *Làm cho sống* — mát tay, the cool hand, named in childhood. Plants root, cultures bloom, mushrooms fruit, the sick and downhearted eat again, because she tends from love.
2. **Tend from love, or it dies.** The *why* must be love, never obligation; the moment tending becomes duty or survival, the living thing — and the tender — goes barren. The law the arc forges and tests.
3. **You are a living thing too; you must be tended, and let yourself be.** The receive-turn, folded in — her own fire must be fed from love, not run on obligation.

**Forge → test:** the Hanoi-alley childhood forges the loving hand (mát tay named, tending from effortless delight); B1–B4 curdle it (the burnout — the why goes from love to obligation, the hand grips, mushrooms won't fruit); the mountain + Tết reforge love as a *chosen* thing. B5 tests the reforged love against scale. **The scam is struck** (2026-07-18); weight (first-in-family) stays offstage, never a trauma, never a villain.

**The lost-magic mechanism (internal-first, clear causality):** the why curdles love→obligation **(F)** → the hand grips and forces **(G dies)** → delight drains **(D dies)** → and a forcing hand cannot fruit a mushroom (*you cannot force a pin*) → **the barren blocks are the SYMPTOM.** Outward faces: absence (A), worth-collapse (E), withdrawal/the cold table (H). Recovery: rest stops the forcing, being fed/seen loosens the grip, the why returns (from-love first-fruit, clumsy) → flow → joy. Permanent cost: innocent-effortless love is gone; adult-*chosen* love replaces it (changed, not restored).

**Skill 🔒:** real mushroom cultivation + fermentation at expert depth — the magic (mát tay) and its gauge (barren vs fruiting). Full species + placements: RUANCAO-MUSHROOM-CATALOG.md. The gift-as-delight (the right mushroom for the person and moment) is the people-facing magic.

**B5:** empire side, her register: 「規模才有安全。小作坊保護不了任何人。」 — re-rooted: she watched her own small fragile gift go barren under load, so she argues scale as protection; her truth (love keeps things alive; the machine cannot love) is the tension she carries — right in essence, wrong in method. Forward vow 「不守著灰。往前走，帶得動的就帶走。」 fits natively (tend the living, don't cling to the dead). Friendship holds. Her mát tay against the tech cast = the magic pole of the war, rhyming with 小圖's never-answered question.

**Growth theme:** the girl whose hands made everything live learns that love — given to living things, and to herself — is the only thing that keeps anything alive; the effortless childhood version curdles under the grind and is reforged as a chosen, adult love.

## 星野遙香 — the super-fan who puts down roots

**Archetype:** the Osaka documentarian — genki, camera always up; the loudest person in the room running from the quietest fear; brightness is a coping mechanism, not a quirk.

**Moral core:**

2. **The worst-sin refusal** — 「會讓別人受傷的東西，我不要再幫它蓋下去」; I won't help build another version of the world I fled. *(value ruled W28/W44/W45; zh lines ⟨candidate⟩)*
3. **Agency within the path** — you can't change the assigned life; you author how you live it. 「這次我自己決定怎麼過。」 *(arc 🔒 — Wedge's own)*
4. **The people are the experience** — plant trees you'll never see grow; get INTO the photo. *(taker→maker 🔒)*

**Forge → test:** B1–4 forge the core out of the mask-crack — running is exhausted as a strategy, and agency-within-the-path plus people-first are hammered out where the performing stops (B3) and the roots go down (B4). B5 tests with the worst-sin refusal made real: she quits the machine in silence — the first plan she ever actually starts — and the test's proof is a register, not a speech: the flattest she has ever been.

**B1–4 (arc 🔒; XINGYE.md as a doc UNLOCKED):** two axes + the scissors — disillusionment (flees the assigned future → faces it) and belonging (tourist → member), separate roots. Drive: she UPLIFTS — always; what matures is HOW: consumption (YOLO, the hoarded unposted photos, never there when you're sick) → making (presence, roots, in the frame someone else holds). Turn: the B3 mask-crack at the scatter — hands emptied of the lens take up the craft the same movement. Skill: shokunin of 光影 — lantern-making (行灯) inside the 老屋再生 collective; the camera was her shadow-lens all along.

**B5:** garage — "the light"; the only garage member not building a robot subsystem. Register drops FLAT at L07 (louder than any argument): 「我查過你們公司。跟我原來的太像了。」 Speaks the L09 verdict over rebooted 小圖: 「但是，看不出來。」 *(ruled W59)*. Stays — at the workbench every morning; the final frame's garage light is hers and 沈文's.

**Growth theme:** a beloved taker becomes a maker — she never stops lifting the room; she matures from lifting by consuming to lifting by making and rooting, and carries how-to-belong home into the grey life.
