# cos retired; LifeOS rebuilt in Obsidian

Retire cos as a daily surface; the LifeOS (finances, tasks, dates) is rebuilt natively in Obsidian. cos's idea was sound but it lived outside Obsidian and created friction in both reading and capture — a separate surface didn't get visited daily. Continuing cos and a Cowork artifact for the life layer were ruled out as variants of the same separate-surface failure on a different platform.
