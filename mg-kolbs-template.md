# MG & Kolbs Template

A learning-system template built around **Kolb's reflective cycles**, **skill-level tracking**, and **goal anchoring** — adapted to Obsidian (on Bases) from Pan's Notion original. This page is a public demo; the data below is illustrative.

## What's inside

- **Skills** — track current vs. final level and competency stage (CI → CC → UC) for each skill you're developing.
- **Kolbs** — short reflective cycles (Experience → Reflection → Abstraction → Experimentation), chained one to the next.
- **Tasks** — with deliberate break timing and a do-date board.
- **Goals** + weekly **Goal tracking** — anchored medium-term goals and a recurring evaluation cadence that feeds back into your skills.
