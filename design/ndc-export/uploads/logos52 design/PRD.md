# Logos52

The source of truth is the two reference pages. Match them.

- `site/index.html` — Home
- `site/about.html` — About / How this wiki works

Open the files from disk. Fonts and, on About, images are embedded.

## Copy

Use the text already on those pages.

## Images

Every picture must come from the content it represents. About has the Ramelli book wheel with the wash in the image, two diagrams, the WNAC screenshot, and a lightbox that opens figures at full size. Home has no pictures.

## Density

Most of the page should be visible on the first screen.

## Type

Body text is IBM Plex Sans, weights 400 and 700. Diagram labels are Monaspace Neon. Font files are in `fonts/`. Diagram and picture language is in `visual-language/`, with generator scripts also in `scripts/`.
