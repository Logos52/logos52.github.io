# Ascent III - Skinning and Refinement

Ascent III is the evaluate phase of the climb. After multiple Aim-Shoot cycles have built a working map, Skin consolidates the current understanding into a final, retrievable chunk structure and creates the certainty needed for later retrieval and application.

## The Skin Step

Skin is exclusively for refining at the evaluate level. Your chunk structure evolves as relationships become clearer through repeated passes. The goal is to consolidate, integrate, judge priority, rename vague chunks, merge or split as needed, and produce a clean backbone that supports strong retrieval prompts.

Perform Skin the same day when possible, or schedule a dedicated weekly pass. Remove clutter, keep only important arrows, and convert the map into SIR prompts.

## Stage-Specific Pressures and Adjustments

In Ascent III the main pressures are:

- The desire for premature certainty before the full picture emerges from multiple cycles.
- Over-attachment to early chunk labels that no longer fit the refined relationships.
- The volume of detail accumulated during Shoot that now needs ruthless prioritisation.

Counter by treating the current map as a hypothesis to be stress-tested. Use GRINDE maps or intuitive chunking criteria (importance, function, contrast) to decide what stays and what is demoted. The Final checkpoint – Ascent 3 confirms you can state the refined structure from memory and identify exactly which relationships required the extra Skin pass to stabilise.

When the checkpoint shows persistent vagueness, the fix is another targeted Aim-Shoot cycle on the weak area rather than more polishing of a flawed structure.

## Integration With the Climb and BHS

Ascent III completes the first full BHS cycle (Aim in I, Shoot in II, Skin here). The refined artifact becomes the stable input for Camp I retrieval practice and later reconstruction work. Strong Skin here reduces the load on every subsequent retrieval and revision session.

Carry forward the final chunk structure and the explicit retrieval prompts it generates. These are the exact assets Base Camp and Summit will rely on under pressure.
