# Ascent II - Shooting and Encoding

Ascent II is where the directed questions from Aim meet live material. You answer them in real time, expand the map with precise detail, and keep the structure alive while the source is still flowing.

## Sustaining the Map Under Load

During the learning event, treat every new piece of information as potential fuel for an existing question or chunk. Add detail only under the chunk it supports. Change chunk boundaries the moment the old grouping no longer fits the evidence.

Draw relationships as they become obvious. Do not wait until later. The map is a working document, not a finished product.

Layers of learning appear here: you move between surface facts, functional relationships, and structural patterns within the same session. Note which layer each addition serves.

## Handle Stage-Specific Pressures

Return frequently to the Aim questions to keep encoding relational. Two common pressures in Ascent II are the pull toward source order (it feels safer) and the flood of detail that can bury the backbone.

Only add information that answers an Aim question or extends a priority chunk. Park or drop the rest for now. The map stays a working document; update boundaries as understanding changes. This produces effective, selective notes rather than transcripts. ## Final Checkpoint for Ascent II

You know the stage is solid when:

- The map contains both the high-priority relationships and the supporting details needed to explain them.
- You can reconstruct the main flow from the notes without the original source.
- Retrieval passes immediately after the event expose only small, fixable gaps rather than wholesale collapse.

When the checkpoint reveals large holes, the fix is almost always more rigorous Aim before the next block rather than more volume during Shoot.

## Connection to Later Stages

The quality of the living map created in Ascent II directly determines how much work Skin will require in Ascent III. Clean, prioritized structure now means faster consolidation later. Weak or linear maps here become expensive to repair downstream.

Carry forward every strong relationship and every marked open question. They become the exact targets for the refinement pass.
