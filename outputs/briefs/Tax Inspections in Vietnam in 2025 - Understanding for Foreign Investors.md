# Tax Inspections in Vietnam in 2025

**Source:** Alitium YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4qAM12Tkck) — Nov 2025  
**From:** Clippings/

## Core Insight
2025 Law 56 changed the rules: one inspection per agency per period. Fewer audits, but every one is deeper, more data-driven, and higher-stakes. Authorities arrive with a 360° digital view from e-invoices, customs, banks, and filings. Most adjustments come from missing documentation, not fraud.

## 2025 Framework
- **Single-shot rule**: One chance per agency/period for the same matters.
- Two types: planned (annual risk program) and unplanned (data anomalies, tips).
- Scope typically 3–5 years (CIT, VAT, PIT, FCT). Can be local, provincial, or General Dept level.

## Selection — Digital Risk Scoring
High-risk signals that trigger inspection:
- Multiple loss years despite continued investment
- Revenue or profit growth out of line with peers or history
- Margins well below industry benchmarks
- Large/unusual VAT claims or refunds
- Related-party transactions (esp. services) without clear support
- Sector priorities (manufacturing, logistics, e-commerce, construction)

## Inspection Process
1. **Notice**: 3–5 working days + detailed document list.
2. **Production**: Primary records + supporting evidence (board approvals, policies, cost allocations, service delivery proofs, contracts).
3. **Execution**: Short on-site visit (legitimacy check) + mostly remote digital review (10–30 days theoretical).
4. **Draft**: Receive draft report and respond — last real opportunity to add evidence.
5. **Final**: Pay assessed amounts before you can appeal.

## Common Problem Areas
- Non-deductible expenses (insufficient justification docs)
- Transfer pricing / intercompany services (no proof of delivery or arm's-length pricing)
- VAT input credits (blacklisted suppliers, timing errors, allocation issues)
- Foreign contractor tax & overseas payments (weak documentation)
- PIT misclassification (contractor vs employee)
- Accounting vs tax data gaps ("two books")
- Ongoing losses + rising investment or volatile profits

## Annual Preparation System
1. **Tax Inspection Master File**  
   Living digital repository: reconciliations, ledgers, returns, governance records, policies (TP, cost allocation, expenses), service proofs, time tracking. Pre-audit it internally every year.

2. **Response Protocol**  
   Designate one experienced liaison for the tax office and a separate person for documents. Brief external Vietnamese tax advisor in advance. Never switch spokespeople mid-inspection.

3. **Digitize Everything**  
   Eliminate paper/digital hybrids. Scan and tag all records into the master file now.

**Mindset**: Treat inspections as forced governance health checks. Well-documented files often make inspectors move on quickly — not worth their time.

## Immediate Actions
- Review intercompany agreements and supporting evidence for the Vietnam entity this quarter.
- Build or refresh the Master File before next fiscal year-end.
- Lock in liaison + advisor protocol.
- Audit foreign contractor classification and overseas payment documentation.
