# Wabi-Sabi

An aesthetic stance that finds beauty in naturalness, simplicity, and subtle imperfection — age, wear, and the unfinished rather than industrial gloss.

In this vault it is catalogued as a high-signal **Human-track** aesthetic (taste & judgment), not a learning technique.
