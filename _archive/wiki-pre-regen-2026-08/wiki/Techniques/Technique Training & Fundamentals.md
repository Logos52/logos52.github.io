# Technique Training & Fundamentals

Technique Training and Fundamentals form the practical foundation for building reliable higher-order learning skills. This area focuses on the core methods and mental models that make consistent deep work possible.

## Core Orientation

Technique Training teaches the specific mechanics of high-quality encoding and thinking. Fundamentals provide the checkpoints and baseline standards that prevent technique errors and cognitive overload as complexity increases.

The goal is to reach a level of conscious competency where the methods become reliable tools rather than things you constantly have to think about.

## Key Areas

- Inquiry-Based Learning & Traffic Light System (TLS)
- Note-Taking Variants (Processed, Reflective, Visual)
- Chunking Methods
- Revision & Retrieval Foundations

## Integration

These fundamentals underpin everything that comes later in the Growth Phases. They also directly support the Five Dimensions by providing the practical methods that turn abstract goals into repeatable actions.
