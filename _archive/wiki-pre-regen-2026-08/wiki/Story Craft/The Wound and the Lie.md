# The Wound and the Lie

Interiority is one causal chain — a specific past wound breeds a false belief, the false belief produces a surface want that fights a buried need, and a character reads as a person to the exact degree that this chain drives an observable choice under pressure. Everything else on the page is the mechanics of that chain and the price of running it.

## The chain

Four links, in order (K.M. Weiland's synthesis in *Creating Character Arcs*, which packages John Truby's earlier engineering in *The Anatomy of Story*):

**The Ghost** is a specific past event that explains why the character holds the Lie. The term is Truby's — a past wound haunting the hero — and Weiland popularized it; do not credit either alone. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s Ghost is concrete: the Hanoi family shop was wiped out by a scam, her parents defrauded, and she is The One Who Made It, the family's whole hope carried abroad. [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s Ghost is an unhappy household where he was provided-for and unseen, so "I did it for you" registers to him as counterfeit love. A Ghost stays a specific event, never a mood or a vague sad childhood.

**The Lie** is the false belief the Ghost bred. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s Lie: *I carry them alone, and a hand is shame.* [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s Lie: *provision is not love; presence is the only real thing, and I have never had it.* The Lie is legible from choice long before anyone can state it.

**The Want** is an external, concrete goal that expresses the Lie. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s Want is countable — take secret shifts after the language center's six-month work rule, fill the monthly envelope home, refuse every offered hand, including [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html)'s free food. [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s Want is equally external — find and reconnect with his grandmother in Taiwan, write her old-school paper letters so some version of him stays in her life.

**The Need** is the internal truth the character must accept to grow, and it sits at war with the Want. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s Need is to let herself be carried; every shift she takes to pay the family out is the same wall that shuts [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) out, so pursuing the Want actively blocks the Need. [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s Need is presence-love and a chosen family that stays. Truby's warning is the load-bearing one: keep Want and Need distinct. If the external goal and the internal truth are the same thing, there is no inner conflict and no arc — the character simply gets what they wanted.

The founding pair *is* this collision embodied. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s provision-love and [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s presence-need read each other's love as its absence, and the arc closes the gap through choices rather than statements.

## Two weaknesses, one argument

Truby doubles the flaw. A **psychological weakness** hurts only the character — [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) works herself raw, cracked and burned hands, no day off in a year. A **moral weakness** means the character hurts others — her wall of self-sufficiency shuts out the person reaching for her. The moral weakness is what sets up the **moral need** and gives the story an ethical argument to make.

The argument lands as event, never as speech (Truby and McKee both insist on this). The controlling claim here — two languages of love, provision and presence, neither wrong, both grieving — states once as design intent and then is carried wordlessly by a chosen object rather than a line of dialogue. No character states the theme; the object does.

**Self-revelation is changed behavior.** Truby's climactic order: the crucible forces the revelation, then the character acts on the new truth. The founding pair's self-revelation lands the same way — not a confession spoken aloud, but a witnessed cost: one reads what the other has been carrying, and both act on it changed. Watch McKee's rule underneath it: true character shows in the choices made under pressure, and the greater the risk, the truer the choice. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s breaking the work rule to take hidden shifts is a choice with real stakes — exhaustion, rule-breaking, hiding it from friends — which is why it reveals her. A choice with nothing at risk would reveal nothing.

## The web: everyone runs their own engine

In an ensemble, each member runs a wound→lie→want/need chain, and the frictions between them are value-clashes that expose several people at once (Truby's character web: define each figure by contrast on one shared moral question). The shared questions here are *provision versus presence* and *being-seen versus being-carried*, and the cast answers them differently:

- **[星野遙香](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) ↔ [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html)** are foils on the same question of the assigned cage. [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) fled a dreaded Japanese office-lady future and wants to cram a lifetime of freedom into Taiwan; [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html) is the local tethered to family, aching to leave, helping six foreigners fly somewhere she never gets to go. Each romanticizes the other's trap.
- **[金多恩](/tsumugu/cast/kim-da-eun.html) + [小圖](/tsumugu/cast/xiao-tu.html)** are two honest angles on one invariant — care and meaning are the human thing that capability cannot substitute for: her grief at AI displacing human meaning, his care built into a machine that was never designed to give it.

Design the companions as a system, one shared moral question refracted, so a single conflict reading illuminates two interiors at once.

## Roundness, and the surprise that was latent all along

A lead reads as round when one **defining contradiction** — an outer trait against an inner truth (McKee's dimension) — drives behavior. [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html)'s brightness is armor over dread; [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html)'s deadpan is armor over a family split by internet-radicalized politics; [林薇](/tsumugu/cast/lin-wei.html) is the fixer who is tired of being leaned on. The contradiction shows as behavior early and only gets named once the reader has the vocabulary to hold it.

Forster's test governs the payoff: "The test of a round character is whether it is capable of surprising in a convincing way. If it never surprises, it is flat. If it does not convince, it is a flat pretending to be round." [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html)'s deadpan cracking to reveal the family rupture must be pre-loaded by small consistent specifics — the binoculars, [將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html) the cat, the rooftop above the café — so the surprise reads as inevitable in hindsight and needs no new exposition at the payoff. Consistent-yet-surprising is a single constraint: the specifics that make a character knowable are the same ones that make the late turn convincing.

Flatness is a deliberate budget tool. Forster's economy — flat characters are recognizable on entry and memorable after — lets you spend scarce roundness budget where it pays. [蘇老師](/tsumugu/cast/teacher-su.html), [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html), and the cat [將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html) each carry one memorable note (the too-quiet house, the boba-and-cats hospitality, the military-rank gag) so the arc-bearing leads get the depth. Under a hard word ceiling, running every ensemble member as a full engine burns budget the leads need.

## Voice, and what is never said

Idiolect — one individual's total speech variety across diction, rhythm, and syntax (linguistics; McKee applies it in *Dialogue* as personal diction) — lets a reader identify the speaker without a tag. Even at the earliest, most vocabulary-constrained stage, three or four speakers can each carry a fingerprint through sentence length, tics, and register: [將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html)'s rank-gag formality, [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html)'s clipped deadpan, [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html)'s exclamation-heavy over-enthusiasm. [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) is the cleanest voice, so readers hear her most.

The most exploitable tool at low vocabulary is **the unsaid** (McKee's said / unsaid / unsayable). What a character never says costs zero vocabulary. [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s evasions about home and [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s refusal to admit strain are built entirely from topic-avoidance and short non-answers early; the withheld thing is finally spoken later, once the words to carry it exist. Silence is the cheapest and most durable characterization a graded reader has.

## The vocabulary curve is the reveal curve

The governing constraint is vocabulary itself, and depth arrives exactly as fast as the reader earns the language to hold it. Early in a graded-vocabulary story the reader has words for actions only, so a wound is shown and never explained — [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html) flinches from warmth, [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) over-controls money, the Lie visible in choice while the wound-in-words waits. The next stage gains words for reasons, so a backstory can finally explain itself and the underlying conflict surfaces. The stage after that gains words for interiority — a story's most vocabulary-hungry material has to wait for it, because the reader's capacity, not the writer's pacing preference, sets the clock.

Split Want and Need across the ramp on purpose. The external Want is fully sayable early in concrete nouns and verbs — fill the envelope, keep the café. The abstract Need — be carried, be seen — only becomes expressible once vocabulary has grown considerably, so self-revelation naturally coincides with the reader's peak vocabulary. The concrete-object spine does the early carrying: the food, the envelope, the cracked hands, the empty chair.

Seed every payoff. The PLANT→SPARK→PAYOFF engine wants each wound confided in one or two quiet two-handers well ahead of its arc beat, drawn out by a curious friend, so the later crisis hits a nerve the reader already knows. Campfire, then battle — sized the way audio drama caps its speakers per scene, so voices stay distinguishable.

## Price

The chain charges setup before it pays. Every wound owes one or two quiet two-hander confidences well ahead of its arc beat, and in a format with a hard ceiling on scenes and speakers per unit, those plant slots compete directly with the shine and banter the ensemble also needs. Run the full engine on an entire large cast and the setup tax alone exceeds the free slots.

The wound must exert **present cost** or it is decoration. A backstory that never pressures a choice on the current page is only a fact — it hasn't earned Ghost status; the Lie has to distort a decision now — [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html)'s hidden shifts, [沈文](/tsumugu/cast/shen-wen.html)'s ache toward a grandmother he keeps writing. And the deferred-payoff load is real: the abstract Need is unwritable for a long stretch, so a single prop has to hold it (the envelope, the empty chair) from early on until the words to name it finally exist. That is a long promise to keep by object alone.

## The case against

The chain is a machine, and a machine can grind a character into a solved equation. If every trait traces cleanly to one wound, the surprise goes out of the character and Forster's test fails — a fully-explained person cannot surprise convincingly. Genuine roundness needs slack the causal chain does not supply.

It also tempts two specific failures. **Announcing the wound** — a line like "ever since my father left, I can't trust anyone" — kills the reveal and spends at a sentence what an object could carry for the whole length of the story. **Collapsing Want into Need** removes the inner conflict Truby built the distinction to protect. Both come from trusting the chain to do the work that choice-under-pressure is supposed to do.

And the positive-change arc is one mode of three (Weiland). A flat/testing arc holds the truth and changes the world around it; a negative arc deepens or is destroyed by the Lie. [蘇老師](/tsumugu/cast/teacher-su.html) runs closer to a flat giver who is finally repaid; [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html) runs disillusionment into maturation. Some resolutions here are deliberately incomplete: real gains that land without the underlying crisis fully closing. *Small wins are big — the arc closes changed, at a price left standing.* Forcing a clean self-revelation onto a character the design wants left partly open over-resolves the arc and cheapens the cost the reader watched accrue.

## Quit signals

- A character's wound never changes what they choose in the reading in front of you. The chain is not load-bearing here — cut it to one flat memorable note and reallocate the roundness budget (make them [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html), not a half-built lead).
- The wound only surfaces when someone narrates it. The vocabulary is not there yet — hold the wound-in-words, let object and action carry it, and move the naming to the point where the words exist.
- Want and Need keep landing in the same sentence. The Need is mis-specified — the Want should be concrete and reachable, the Need the thing pursuing the Want blocks. If they do not fight, respecify the Need.
- A payoff lands flat. Check for its plant; a crisis with no prior confided moment hits a nerve the reader has not been given.

## Checkable expectations

- A reader at the earliest reading level, shown only [阮草](/tsumugu/cast/ruan-cao.html) counting the envelope and turning down [吳老闆](/tsumugu/cast/boss-wu.html)'s free food, should be able to say she carries something alone before any reading states it. If they cannot, the behavioral tell is too faint.
- A reveal built as a witnessed object should land its full weight in the beat that follows it, with no explanatory line. If it needs a sentence of narration to land, the plant failed upstream.
- Strip the dialogue tags from an early-level group scene; a reader should still name most speakers from idiolect alone ([將軍](/tsumugu/cast/general.html)'s rank formality, [白龍](/tsumugu/cast/bai-long.html)'s clipped lines, [星野](/tsumugu/cast/hoshino-haruka.html)'s exclamations). Tags that turn out to be load-bearing mean the voices have collapsed toward one.
- A reader who re-reads the early chapters after a later turn should find it already latent in the small specifics, needing no new exposition. A turn that reads as a cheat on re-read means the surprise had no prior latency.
