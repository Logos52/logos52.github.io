# Front-Facing Pages Cleanup

Cleaned up the public surface of the site so each page has a clearer job.

Home now acts as the short Start Here page. Index remains the full catalog. Journal carries current questions, current focus pages, and only the most recent log items. Calendar holds the longer chronology. About explains the project and keeps the bio secondary.

The most important update was making today's work visible near the front: [[wiki/Self Management/Focus Management - How to Enter & Recover Inside a Work Block|Focus Management: Training the Return Mechanism]] and [[wiki/Concepts/Social Media - Curvilinear Design & the Theft of Time|Social Media - Curvilinear Design & the Theft of Time]] now sit closer to the entrance of the site because they represent the current direction of the system: attention protection, return speed, and avoiding passive consumption loops.
