# Techniques in School

Parkinson's Law claims work expands to fill the time given to it. The school move is a hard stop on hours that have not earned the right to grow: when the box ends, that work stops. Teacher-set methods often lag the better ones; protect the better method on unweighted work, and on weighted work satisfy the gate, then learn in the leftover time.

## Inside the room

Mainstream training can leave classroom methods outdated or anecdotal, and occasionally harmful. That is a possibility, not a rate, and it is not a licence to pick a fight that costs the grade.

If notes are required in a linear or passive form and nobody inspects them, run the better method anyway. The moment the notebook is marked against the teacher's format, that workaround is spent — satisfy the gate.

On unweighted work, do the minimum that meets the requirement. Perfectionism on low-value mandatory tasks is how the evening disappears. The price is whatever marks that assignment carried. A workaround that spends the grade is the fight the bound forbids.

Turn class into a quiz. Hunt what is still unclear. Ask the deeper question. Retrieval in the room beats sitting through a pass that already landed. If the material is already known, homework during class is an option, not a rule, and it fails the moment the room forbids it.

## The cap

Time-blocking reserves a slot that may run long if the work is still live. Time-boxing is the opposite: when the hour ends, the work stops. The cap is what the proverb is good for. A tiny box will not finish a real exam; the limit is reasonable hours, not a squeeze that makes the work impossible.

Boxing fits two opposite cases at once. Very important work that is not urgent, and cannot finish in one sitting, gets a recurring box so it is not crowded out — self-study already in hand, the encoding methods already chosen. Very unimportant work that is still compulsory gets a box so it reaches a sufficient standard without bleeding past its value. Unweighted homework is the named case. The goal is the best outcome in the time available, not a perfect one.

Order: schedule the important work first. Leftover time, smaller than habit, is the box for the compulsory-unimportant items, finished as fast as that box allows. [[wiki/Self Management/Study Scheduling|Study Scheduling]] is where boxed study time goes once the cap has protected it. The same hard stop is a counter to [[Procrastination - a System Problem]] — open-ended delay ends when the hour ends.

A first week where the box overruns and the important work is what slipped has inverted the order.

## What a week looks like

The hour ends. Work that earned more hours still has them. Unweighted work finished inside the box. The graded format was satisfied, and the gate was not picked as a fight.
