Tsumugu

Tsumugu is a series of experiments in making language learning fun, engaging and effective, and in removing the parts of it that do not serve the student or bog them down, which learning Chinese tends to do, given how complicated the language is.

The projects are educational language learning tools wrapped in a long-form story, meant to hook the reader and motivate them to improve their language ability in order to progress the story.

Tsumugu Story

Six students in one language-learning class.

Tsumugu Core

A graded reader that writes its own pages from known words.

Tsumugu Encoding Dictionary

A Chinese dictionary. Each character is explained by its form and by a story.
