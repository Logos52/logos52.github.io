Tsumugu
Tsumugu Core

Tsumugu Core is a graded reader web app that serves as the foundation of the other Tsumugu apps. Each text is written so that 80 to 95 percent of its words are ones the reader already knows, which is the comprehensible-input range. Its primary features are waveform playback, which supports the chorusing and shadowing techniques, and a popup dictionary: click any word or character to see its translation. The popup is tied into the Tsumugu Encoding Dictionary, which breaks characters down into their components.
