## The style target, stated concretely

`The Generator.md` works because of how it is written, not only what it says. Match these:

- **It is a stance, in speech.** "Every page in this vault is something you own — lived, tested, or built — being handed to a reader you respect." A person saying that out loud. Not a specification a finished page passes.
- **It is short.** The act itself is one paragraph. Everything after the first horizontal rule is scope, sound, and provenance.
- **Prohibitions appear only as consequences.** "Inside that stance, the gotcha, the epigram, the disease-door never occur — not because they're banned, but because they aren't things a person doing *that* actually says."
- **Horizontal rules separate the act from the commentary about the act.** The reader can stop after the act and have the whole thing.
- **It names live models rather than describing quality.** Three page links, no adjectives.

## What the new file must add

**The substitution.** The locked act describes handing over a tool from your own workshop. On a selfhood page the thing handed over is a judgment — a position held, a line drawn, something declined. A judgment has no use to a reader until they can see the situation that made it necessary, so the opening whole is that situation, in ground the reader already stands on, and the judgment lands once the ground exists to hold it. Write this as an act, not as the rule Opus just stated.

**The WPW shape, whole page.** Whole, parts, whole advanced. The close mirrors the opening — the same ground, the problem now answerable. Returning is not restating; a close that re-says the opening was struck 2026-07-02.

**Step height is density.** Seven plain words are a step if they are a thesis. This is why a cold claim fails on these pages even when true and plainly worded.
