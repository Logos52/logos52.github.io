# Readers

This file says who is reading, for each kind of writing in this vault. It is read before a draft is started and it is the only picture of the reader the writer may use. A writer who checks a sentence against his own head is checking it against a reader who already knows everything. This file is the reader he checks against instead.

Every reader below has one thing in common. They have not read the page. Everything they know about its subject comes from the sentences above the one they are on, or from ordinary life. They do not know the words this vault made up. They do not know the owner. They do not know what was said in the conversation that produced the page.

## A wiki page

A person who has read no other page in this vault. They know ordinary English and ordinary life. They do not know learning science, the vault's concepts, the owner's projects, or any term coined here. A link on the page tells them what is on the other side; they will not follow it to find out what a word means.

## A personal page, a positions page

The same person. They do not know the owner, his history, his politics, his family, or his work. They have not read the profile, the positions page, or the argument pages. If the page needs a fact about the owner, the page states it.

## A podcast or story script

A learner of Mandarin at the level of the episode's vocabulary gate. They know only the characters and words the gate says they know. The gate is the list on disk, not the writer's sense of what is common. They do not know the cast unless the episode introduces them, and they do not know anything from an earlier episode that this episode does not restate.

## A dictionary entry

A learner who can see the entry row. The row carries the character, the reading, and the meaning. Prose in the entry may use what the row shows. It may not use what another entry shows.

## A blog post

A stranger on the internet who arrived from a search. They know nothing about the site, the owner, or the tools. They know ordinary English.

## A reply, a report, or an explanation to the owner

The owner. He reads English and not Chinese. He has not run a website before. He does not hold the vault's house words, the rule numbers, or the names of its files unless the reply says what they are. He was in the conversation, so he knows what was asked. He was not inside the writer's work, so he does not know what the writer found until it is said.
