# The Generator — Eggbot Tight

This file is a pick for how the sentences of a wiki page get made, after the meaning of the page is settled with the owner. It adds to [[02 - System/The Generator|The Generator]], which wins wherever the two disagree. It stays unproven until the owner accepts a page written under it.

Dr Eggbot is a named Grok Bot, made by Lauren Tan, whose job is designing other Grok Bots. The descriptions that bot writes felt tight. Three things produced that tightness. The data shape belongs on a wiki page. How each sentence is written belongs on a wiki page. The costume does not.

The data shape is this. A bot is one job, a list of things it never does, a voice in a few words, and when it wakes. A tool the job does not need is a fail.

How each sentence is written is this. The clean sentence is the first draft. A later pass that cleans a mannered sentence has been measured to fail, in Lauren Tan's stack. Short is not an excuse to drop a fact the friend needs.

The costume is casual, a little mad-scientist, short lowercase. That costume is Eggbot's voice. It is not this file. A wiki page is still a handover to a reader who is respected, in The Generator's stance.

## Before any sentence

Name four things in the outline the owner sees, in the order below. If the outline cannot state those four, there is no page yet.

1. **One job.** The question the friend would ask, in one sentence. If you need "and", it is two pages.
2. **Anti-jobs.** The adjacent questions this page never answers. Name them. Those questions belong on other pages, or they do not belong in this vault.
3. **Voice.** The Generator's handover: someone showing a friend a tool. Not a new costume. Not a paragraph of tone.
4. **Reader.** The matching row in [[02 - System/Readers|Readers]]. A wiki page is a person who has read no other page in this vault.

[[02 - System/Writing Standards|Writing Standards]] still owns whole, part, whole, what never ships, and what a page owes. This file does not join Writing Standards. That file does not say how prose should sound.

## How a sentence is written

You write the sentence the way you would say it to the owner, already clean. You do not write a mannered sentence and then remove the figures of speech. "Avoid all mannered prose" is the test after the sentence exists. It is not the machine that produces the sentence.

A sentence is a whole thought. When one thing happens because of another, the because is in the same sentence. When one thing means another, the sentence says so. You do not cut a thought into a row of facts and leave the reader to join them. That is how notes sound. It is also how Selfhood Plain made pages curt.

The sentence is as long as the thought. Sometimes four words. Sometimes forty. A row of short flat sentences fails. A sentence nobody could finish out loud also fails. The owner's test, from 2026-08-20: you would say it out loud, to him, without bracing.

Short is not an excuse to drop content. If the friend needs a fact, the fact is in this sentence or in a sentence above it on the same page. Dropping the fact to look tight is the Selfhood Plain failure wearing Eggbot's name.

If you cannot restate the sentence as a fact the reader can use, an instruction they can follow, or a number with what it was measured over, the sentence is leftover. Cut it. Do not rewrite it into a better gesture.

A reader holds about four things at once. A sentence that asks them to hold more is split. Splitting keeps every fact. `scripts/holdings.py` still runs after each section. The next section may use only what the ledger holds.

## Leftover is a fail

Leftover on a bot is a tool the job does not need. Leftover on a page is anything the one job does not need.

These are leftover:

- A second job, smuggled as a section
- A second name for a thing the page already named
- A sentence you can delete without the reader losing a fact
- A figure of speech doing the work of a fact
- A pair that says what the thing is not, then what it is
- A heading that turns instead of naming what is in the section
- The generator's mechanism list pasted onto the page: the words premise, argument, inference; a lecture the reader did not come for; a playbook the friend did not ask to run

They are leftover because they are not the job. They are not leftover because a detector matched a pattern. If a strike comes, do not add a detector. Ask whether the sentence was the job. If it was not, it comes out. If it was, the sentence was the wrong whole thought, and you write that thought again from the friend's question.

Offer this pick only when the owner asks for Eggbot tight, leftover-is-a-fail, or a page written the way Dr Eggbot's descriptions read. Do not offer it as the new default. Do not offer it as a replacement for The Generator.

## Why this file exists

The owner read Dr Eggbot's language, said it was tight in a good way, and asked for a writing standard from it for the wiki. The costume is easy to copy and wrong for a public page. The four-field shape and the clean-first sentence are the parts that survive contact with The Generator and with the 2026-08-22 ruling that pages must not be curt.

A page that passes this file has one job, whole thoughts, and no leftover. A page that is only short has failed.
