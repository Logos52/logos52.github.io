The kind of thing is one of these six.

- A product or tool.
- A school, district, or government policy.
- A teacher or student describing what they actually do.
- A study or measured result.
- A prediction for the next five to ten years.
- A claim that AI harms learning.

4. Predictions for five to ten years from people who build the tools or run the schools. At least 10 rows.
5. The counter-side: posts saying what AI does to the learner, with evidence rather than mood. At least 10 rows.
