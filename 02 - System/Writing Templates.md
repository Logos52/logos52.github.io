# Writing Templates

Five templates, one per thinking habit. Each block below is the template with its slots written out, so a writer fills the slots instead of inventing a shape. Each block says when it is a section and when it is a whole page, and which slot of whole, part, whole it fills (Writing Standards §2). The public page that derives them is [[wiki/Writing Craft/Five Writing Templates|Five Writing Templates]]; the habits themselves are on [[wiki/Concepts/Five Thinking Habits - Conclusion First|Five Thinking Habits - Conclusion First]]. Owner's direction, 2026-09-07: each habit gets its own writing template; it can be a section or the entire page; it sits with whole, part, whole or apart from it, page by page.

A template is an instrument. It says what goes where. It says nothing about how the sentences sound; that is the write-act's job ([[02 - System/The Generator|The Generator]]).

## How to pick

Pick by what the writer holds when sitting down.

| The writer holds | Template | Size |
|---|---|---|
| A conclusion | T1 Start at the end | The opening whole of any page, or the whole page |
| Observations and no settled conclusion | T2 Observations before conclusions | A block under any claim, or a record page |
| A fuzzy ask | T3 Play the question back | The opening of any page, or a routing page |
| A decision or a position | T4 Say what would change your mind | A block after any conclusion, or a position page |
| A finished subject | T5 Compress it first | The description line of any page, or a condensed page |

On an ordinary wiki page all five run at once: T5 is the description line, T1 or T3 is the opening whole, T2 is each part, T4 is the closing whole.

## T1. Start at the end

Slot in the shape: the opening whole.

```text
[ANSWER]      One sentence. The page's or the section's answer. Written from the fact list with the
              source closed. If it will not come, there is no outline yet; go back to the source.
[UNCERTAINTY] Only if the answer is unsettled: what is settled and what is not, in the same sentence
              as the answer. The status line in the frontmatter does not carry this; the sentence does.
[REASONS]     In the order that supports the answer. One reason per paragraph or per part.
[CLOSE]       The answer again, with the reasons now behind it. Not a replay of [ANSWER].
```

Section: [ANSWER] then [REASONS] inside one section. Page: [ANSWER] is sentence one, [REASONS] are the parts, [CLOSE] is the closing whole. Pipeline step it changes: Outline. The outline's opening whole is [ANSWER], written before any paragraph.

## T2. Observations before conclusions

Slot in the shape: the parts.

```text
[SETTING]      What was watched, where, and when. Who was doing the watching if it matters.
[SEEN 1..n]    What was seen, in the order it came. Each with what it was measured over.
[READING]      What it means. Marked "not settled" when it is not. Never the reading alone.
```

Section: a block dropped in at the point where a claim is made; [SEEN] then [READING]. A takeaway or a claim with no [SEEN] under it is a verdict and gets its observation or goes. Page: a record; [SETTING] is the opening whole, [SEEN] are the parts, [READING] is the close. A record with no [READING] yet stands apart from the shape and is kept as material. Pipeline step it changes: Writing. After the essay, every core takeaway is pointed at the paragraph or figure that holds its [SEEN].

## T3. Play the question back

Slot in the shape: the opening whole, from the question's side.

```text
[ASKED]     The question as asked, in the asker's words.
[REALLY]    What it is really asking. The crux. The purpose behind the ask.
[PARTS]     Its parts, if more than one. Each part named.
[ANSWER i]  The answer to each part, or the page that answers it.
[WHOLE]     The question answered as a whole.
```

Section: [ASKED] [REALLY] [PARTS] at the top of a page or a section, before the answer. Page: a routing page; [ASKED] and [PARTS], each part routed to its page; [WHOLE] closes. Pipeline step it changes: Content and Outline. The friend's question is [ASKED]; when it has two parts the outline shows both, or the page becomes two pages.

## T4. Say what would change your mind

Slot in the shape: the closing whole, or the last part before it.

```text
[POSITION]   What is held.
[COUNTER]    The strongest case against, stated so its holder would recognize it.
[FLIP]       The condition under which [POSITION] would be wrong. One or two sentences, next to [POSITION].
[THIS TIME]  Page size only: is this case different from the ones the writer has seen before?
```

Section: [POSITION] [COUNTER] [FLIP] as a block after any conclusion, on any page. Page: a position page or a decision document; all four. Pipeline step it changes: Writing. Every wiki page's Conclusion section carries [FLIP]. Action pages and decision documents already owe the fuller form in Writing Standards §4 and keep it.

## T5. Compress it first

Slot in the shape: the page at its shortest; the description line.

```text
[LINE]       One or two lines that carry every important point and none of the fluff. Written first,
             from the fact list, before the essay.
[EXPANSION]  The rest of the section or page, expanded from [LINE].
[CHECK]      Two ways. Everything in [LINE] appears in [EXPANSION]. [EXPANSION] adds no point that
             [LINE] does not carry; a point found only there goes up into [LINE] or comes out.
```

Section: [LINE] at the top of a section, then [EXPANSION]. Page: a condensed page; one paragraph holding the whole subject, then numbered lines each standing alone (Writing Standards §4, Condensed pages). Pipeline step it changes: Writing. The description line and the core takeaways are [LINE], written before the essay; [CHECK] runs after.
