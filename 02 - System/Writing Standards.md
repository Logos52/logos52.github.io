# Writing Standards

There is one rule about style. Avoid all mannered prose. Say the thing literally. No figure of speech doing the work of a fact, no feeling or idea acting like a person, no twist, no balanced pair, no sentence built for its sound.

The target is the owner's own writing in [[02 - System/Owner Writing Samples|Owner Writing Samples]]. A writer reads it before writing a page. No other file says how prose should sound, and no rule of that kind joins this file. Owner, 2026-09-20: "only thing i really care about is no mannered prose."

## What the reader has

A sentence may use only what the reader already has, from the sentences above it or from ordinary life. A term this vault coined is said in plain words before its name is used. A count is given with the things counted.

## Facts

Every fact on a page comes from the owner's page or his research, or follows from them. Nothing comes from a model's training or from a web search. Where his research and a model's memory disagree, his research stands.

## What never ships

- A private-corpus path (`raw/`, `raw/private/`, `raw/sources/`, `raw/processed/`), or course machinery a reader cannot reach.
- A paid course, program or product named or linked on student-facing material. Free resources may be linked.
- Internals of an unpublished story: production coordinates, beat ledgers, registry names, private-repo pointers.
- An invented claim about the owner. No personal fact ships without his sign-off.
- A pointer to where a fact came from, anywhere outside the Sources block. The body says what is true about the world. A named survey, study or report is a fact about the world and can stay in the body. The medium a page was compiled from, such as a video, a transcript or an episode, appears only in Sources. `scripts/source-words-check.py` lists the hits.
- A political page that has not passed [[02 - System/Bias QA|Bias QA]].
- Offhand chat filed as documentation. Write the ruling, not the vent.

## Page layout

A page opens on the whole, goes into the parts, and returns to the whole before it stops. The opening says why the subject matters before it names the parts. Owner, 2026-09-18: "always start with Why, not what" and "always start with Whole, not parts."

Beyond that, the content leads the format. **Related pages** and **Sources** come last.

On a page where the owner is the subject, the I owns events, wants and judgments, and never qualities. The reasoning is on [[wiki/Concepts/The Two Meanings of Ego|The Two Meanings of Ego]].

## Decision documents

A PRD, proposal, decision note or memo gives its verdict inside the first two sentences, and says what was being decided and what it was decided against. It states the strongest case for what it rejects and names what would flip the decision. It prices its own recommendation. Its success criteria can be checked without asking what was meant.

## Strikes

A struck text is regenerated from its facts and never patched line by line. No rule is added after a strike unless the owner says "make this a rule".
