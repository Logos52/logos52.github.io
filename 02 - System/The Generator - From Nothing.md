# The Generator — From Nothing

This file is read before the opening of a page whose subject is a term the reader does not own, or a familiar word used in a sense the page gives it. It says how a definition is built for a reader who arrives holding nothing. It adds to [[02 - System/The Generator|The Generator]], which wins wherever the two seem to disagree, and it says nothing about how sentences sound.

It was written on 2026-09-10 from one opening that passed on its shape after twenty that failed, on the page Trained Contempt. The owner's direction that produced it: the intro flows from nothing, and the writing flows from the intro. The passing opening is in [[02 - System/Openings on the Record|Openings on the Record]] as N. The twenty are in [[02 - System/Rejected Specimens|Rejected Specimens]], 37 to 50. The file stays unproven until a page written under it is accepted by the owner.

---

## What the reader holds at sentence one

Nothing from the page. They hold the everyday sense of every common word, and their own life. That is the whole inventory. A term the vault coined, a familiar word in the page's own sense, a case the page will use, a comparison the page will draw: none of these exist for the reader until the page has put them down.

The writer checks sentence one against that inventory and not against his own head, where every sense is already loaded. `scripts/holdings.py` lists every reference the page has not given. Each flag is real until the writer can point at the line above that gives it.

## The order of the opening

1. **The word in the sense the reader owns.** Sentence one uses the key word the way the reader already uses it. "Contempt, in everyday use, means looking down on someone." Nothing in this sentence is new to the reader, so nothing in it can be ungiven.
2. **The page's sense, in plain words.** The page says it will use the word more narrowly, and then says the narrower sense in words the reader owns. "It is the feeling of looking at a thing you made or use and not being able to stand it as it is."
3. **Named things that carry the sense.** Two or three things the reader can picture, each one an instance of the sense just given. A page that says less than it could. A tool that fails at a job it ought to handle. They come after the sense, never before it, and they are things, not kinds.
4. **Why the sense matters, if it does.** One plain sentence with the reason inside it. "That feeling is where every better version of anything comes from, because nobody remakes a thing they are content with."
5. **Every other special word, the same way.** A second word the page uses in its own sense gets steps 1 and 2 from something the reader owns. "Trained means what it means for a dog or a reflex: done so many times that it now happens without you deciding."
6. **The definition, last.** The term is defined using only words the paragraph above has already put down. Nothing new enters the defining sentence. "So trained contempt is that feeling of not being able to stand a thing, repeated until it comes on its own, before the thing has given you a reason."

The name of the page is in the opening, in the sense the reader owns at step 1 and in the page's sense at step 6. It is not withheld.

## What never happens in the opening

- The definition does not carry a twist. It says what the thing is and nothing surprising. A paradox in the defining sentence, a thing arriving before its cause, a sneer that outlives its wall, is a performance, and the twenty struck openings each had one.
- The opening does not assert what the reader knows, feels, or will forget. "You know the shape of it," "you forget you are wearing them," "anyone who has fixed something knows": each tells the reader about themselves, and that is rude.
- The opening does not build context from a claim the reader has to accept on contact. "Contempt can become a habit" before contempt has been given is a claim, not context. Context is something the reader already holds.
- The opening does not open on an example in place of the sense. A named case is step 3, after step 2. A case standing where the sense should be is the example wearing a definition's grammar.
- The opening does not count things it has not named, and does not talk about the page.
- The opening does not end on a punch. The last sentence is the definition, and it is plain.

## How the body flows from the opening

Every section after the opening uses the senses the opening gave and gives every new thing the same way before using it. A case the body introduces is introduced in a sentence of its own. A comparison the body draws is drawn in a sentence of its own, from something the reader owns, before it does any work. A coined word the owner used in conversation reaches the page only after the page has given its sense, or not at all. The check is the same as at sentence one: what does the reader have to already hold for this sentence, and is every item above it on the page.

## When a strike says "same problem"

The first move is to assemble every struck opening for the page in one list and read them together before writing another. The fault is in what they share, and it is visible only side by side. The second move is to say the fault in plain words and check it against the owner's words. The third move is one fresh opening from this file, not a variant of the last one.
