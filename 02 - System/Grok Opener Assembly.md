# Grok openers — live models and the assembly gate

Fable inhabits a stance. Grok compresses a claim into a quote card and then grades the card as a pass. This file is the Grok-specific replacement for that loop.

Re-read two of the **Replacements** immediately before writing sentence one. Do not re-read the struck column as a model.

## Why Claude's fix did not travel

Claude's 2026-08-13 gate (`generate-from-what-he-wants`, Fable write lane §§ opening): openings are a handover, written from a material inventory, pillow-test kills the quote card. Fable can run that. Ratified 2026-08-13.

Grok ran a different machine:

1. Banks and skeletons said **owned term → operating claim is the payload** and then *wrote the operating claim as an epigram*. Attention Management's bank: "the day's losses are concentrated in the seams between tasks." That sentence *is* the struck opener.
2. Assigned move **plain causal fact** rewarded a thesis-atom in sentence one.
3. `regen-check.py` scored word count, no wikilink, and "Everyone…". Register was invisible. Every struck line **PASS**ed.
4. Grok's self-administered pillow test always passes. Decisional Delays logged the leak-line as "a report about minutes."

Pasting Claude's stance into `GROK-WRITE-CONTRACT.md` after the wave was more prose for Grok to satisfy while still composing the claim. Stance is not a Grok control.

## Assembly (Grok only)

Before any page sentence exists, the droplog has:

```
## Inventory
- <countable object, act, number, name, or glyph from the bank's CARRY examples>
- …five items. Not abstractions (loss, charge, bet, seam, thread, place, insight).
```

Sentence one is built by putting at least one inventory item in the sentence. The operating claim, if the page has one, arrives *after* the reader is holding that item. It is never the first breath.

`regen-check.py` fails the draft when the Inventory section is missing, has fewer than three items, or shares no 4+ letter content token with sentence one.

## What banks and skeletons may no longer do

Do not write: "The operating claim is that [epigram]." That line becomes sentence one.

Do write, in the opening brief: `s1-material:` and five CARRY nouns. The writer is not allowed to invent a sixth abstraction to open on.
