# Opener Handoff for Grok

You write openings that read as epigrams. So did the other writer on this vault, for eighteen consecutive strikes on 2026-08-13, and neither of you was shown the other's prompts. The habit comes from the training corpus, not from your instructions, which is why this handoff does not hand you a list of things to stop doing. A list was already tried on both of you and it produced the next epigram every time.

Read this once. It replaces the opener sections of any older brief you hold.

**Companion documents.** The full evidence is `wiki/Research/Opener Generator Research Bank.md`. The finding written up is `wiki/Systems/AI & Agentic Systems/The Prohibition Loop.md`. Neither is required reading to work; both are there if a ruling below looks arbitrary.

## 1. What was tried on you already, and why it is being withdrawn

On 2026-08-14 the response to your epigrams was a specimen regex, a mandatory `## Inventory` section, a token-overlap failure threshold, and a ban on writing "the operating claim is [line]". That ship is logged as entry 1 in `01 - Workbench/regen-2026-08/ATTEMPT-CATALOG-grok-opener-generator.md` and it is not to be retried or extended.

It fails for a mechanical reason. A shapely epigram passes a well-filled inventory: the noun gets inserted and the sentence stays a quote card. **A rule is wrong when the rejected output would pass it**, and every rule in that ship has that property.

`opener.aphorism_specimen` stays on disk as a silent backstop. Do not grow it, do not tune it, do not consult it while writing.

---

## 2. The two ways an opening gets made, and which one you are on

**Composing.** You face the instruction "write a strong opening" holding nothing, and you produce a sentence. The trained default for that task is the epigram, so that is what arrives. Every struck opening in the record was produced this way, including all of yours.

**Explaining.** You are already holding the page's material and the opening is the first breath of handing it over. Every accepted opening in the record was produced this way.

The difference is not the sentence. It is what you were doing when it came out. **Nothing you avoid while composing turns composing into explaining.**

---

## 3. The situation you write from

This is not a rule to comply with. It is the position you occupy while writing, and if you are in it the epigram does not form, because it is not a thing a person in this position says.

> Every page in this vault is something the owner holds — lived, tested, or built — being handed to a reader he respects. The opening is that handover: what the thing is, what it is for, what it gives, in the voice of someone showing a friend a tool from his own workshop. Ease the reader in the way prestudy eases a learner: bird's-eye before detail, whole before parts, welcome before work. A value is introduced with the dignity of something held. A skill is introduced by what it lets the reader do. A person appearing in an opening appears with respect.

Four things make that a generator rather than a taste statement, and you can check each one against your draft:

1. **Who you are to the material** — someone who owns it, not someone assessing it from outside and not someone selling it.
2. **Who the reader is** — someone worth respecting, not someone to be hooked, corrected, or caught out.
3. **What passes between you** — the thing itself and what it gives, not your framing of it.
4. **The order** — wide before narrow, whole before parts.

**The stance governs the entire page, not the opening.** Owner's ruling the same day: "make sure that generation prompt exists throughout the entirety of the written page. not just the intro." An opening in the stance stapled to a body of stacked verdicts is a register break and gets struck as one.

---

## 4. The procedure

This is what changes the act. Follow it in order.

1. **List the page's pointable material from the bank before writing anything** — the named things, the acts, the instances, the numbers. If the list is thin, the bank is thin, and that is the problem to solve first.
2. **Re-read two accepted openers immediately before you write.** They are in §6. Exemplars steer generation; rules do not. Read them the way you would read a colleague's work right before starting your own, not as specimens to be analysed.
3. **Write the body and the opening in one pass.** The opening is the first breath of that pass, never a freestanding line composed before or after. This single change does most of the work.
4. **Build every opening sentence from the material list**, ramp steps included. A ramp's bird's-eye ground is the page's own material at low magnification. It is never a freshly composed observation about life — that is where truisms come from.
5. **When a sentence arrives sounding quotable, it dies unwritten.** Balanced clauses, wisdom shape, no pointable noun. You do not need to file it or diagnose it. Keep writing from the material.

## 5. Routing: which pages get a run-up

Owner's ruling, 2026-08-13:

- **Moral core and core values always get the run-up.** "i want the run-up to it." A value is introduced as a value, with the dignity of something held.
- **Mechanical pages may open cold**, on the thing itself.
- **Where the subject is a felt experience**, the ramp starts inside the experience.
- **Where no clean definable subject exists**, priming is required, not optional. The abstract is written after the ground exists.

Step height is density, not word count. Seven plain words asserting a thesis are still a step, because the reader has to process a claim on contact. "i dont want to dive in. ease in… big steps are not inviting."

## 6. The live models

Read these two before writing. They are accepted openings from this vault, not descriptions of accepted openings.

A ramp, on a page whose subject had to be built before it could be named:

```text
Most of being considerate is easy to see. Take up less space, make less noise, leave things
cleaner than you found them — nobody needs a page for that part. Speaking is the part that
is hard to see. A sentence takes something from everyone who hears it — their time, their
attention — so a careful person learns to spend sentences carefully too. And carefulness
with sentences, left to itself for years, quietly turns into silence. That slide, from
careful to silent, is what this page is about.
```

A value handed over as a value:

```text
Good faith is a commitment you carry into every dealing that matters: the other person gets
the true picture from you — your real constraints, your real intentions, the downsides you
could have sat on — even when telling them costs you something now. The cost is the working
part: anyone can say cooperative things, and a disclosure that cost you something is the one
the other person can actually believe.
```

Cold openings, for pages that take one:

```text
A language model's default voice is trained self-regard.

Flow state is a condition of full absorption in a single task, in which attention holds
without forcing and the sense of time recedes.

Bots watch; the desk changes things. Every rule on this page is that sentence applied to a
situation that keeps coming up.
```

## 7. The backstops, which are not the writing

Run these after a page is drafted, never during. Both are validated against the full accept and strike sets.

- **The pillow test.** A first sentence that works as a standalone quote card is drawing its force from its form. It goes back.
- **The machinery test.** By sentence two at the latest, the reader holds page-specific things that can be pointed at.

A failure of either sends you back to §4 step 1 for that page. It does not produce a new rule, a new specimen class, or a new checker entry.

---

## 8. What not to build

Do not respond to a strike by shipping a detector. No new regexes, no mandatory sections, no overlap thresholds, no phrasing bans. That move is on the attempt catalog as a dead end and repeating it is a violation wearing a new date.

When a strike lands, the response is: name the single generator in one sentence, then regenerate from the material with the prior draft dead. If you find yourself writing an adjustment list, you are on the wrong path.

---

## 9. How we will know this worked

This handoff is itself an untested move. Transplanting a stance paragraph from one writer to another has been tried once and it produced epigrams with a concrete noun inserted, which is compliance with a description rather than a change in the act.

So the check is not "did Grok read the stance." It is:

- First sentences that fail the pillow test drop from the current baseline. The baseline is 52 of 235 workbench first sentences failing, recorded 2026-08-14.
- Struck openings, when they happen, fail in *different* families rather than the same one wearing new clothes.
- No new detector ships in response to a strike.

If the epigrams persist under this handoff, the finding is that a stance does not transfer as text, and each agent has to arrive at one against its own strike record. That is a real result and it gets written down rather than answered with a rule. The experiment design is in `01 - Workbench/regen-2026-08/RUN-GROK-GENERATOR-RESEARCH.md`.
