# What a Label Does

A label is real information. Withholding the true ones to avoid being sorted is its own kind of dishonesty, and it reads as exactly that — a page with no affiliations anywhere on it has told you something too, and what it told you is that the person was managing you.

What changes is when the label arrives. Coming first, it stops being information and turns into an instruction. Everything after it gets filed as evidence for or against the category, and the sorting finishes well before the reading does. The same word arriving after the events that produced it lands as a conclusion the reader had already half-drawn.

Decades on the receiving end is what makes that difference obvious rather than theoretical. A face is a label that arrives before a word is said, and [[wiki/Travel/Reading as Local|the reception runs on it]] regardless of who turns out to be standing there. Nothing about that arrangement waits for evidence, which is the part worth knowing: the sorting is not a judgment that got made badly, it is a judgment that got made early.

So the affiliations stay, and they stay where they are earned. [[personal/2026-08-17-positions|What I hold politically]] is a page of its own, and getting there is a decision rather than an accident. The [[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Worldviews & the Political Order|arguments underneath those positions]] are twenty-two more. None of it is hidden and none of it is the first thing.
