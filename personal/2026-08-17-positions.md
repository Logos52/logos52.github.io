# Positions

## Belonging

- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Mass Immigration - Cohesion|Mass Immigration - Cohesion]]** — The people who came in during the 2020 wave should be sent back, and people who arrive should not get a vote over local policy. Whether someone who arrives belongs here depends on how far they have taken on the way of life here, and that is a matter of degree, not a yes or no. The argument against mass immigration that works in public is that it pushes down wages for the people already here. Making that argument does not make someone anti-business. The page asks who gets let in and whether they can live under the same rules once they are.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association|The Civil Rights Act - Freedom of Association]]** — Nobody should be forced by law to do business with anyone. Being free to associate means being free not to, and the act took that away to get what it got. The page weighs the trade.

## Gaps, and what gets read into them

- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference|Racial Egalitarianism - The Disparity Inference]]** — A gap in outcomes between two groups does not, on its own, show that anyone discriminated against the group that came out behind. Most of the gap comes from differences in culture, in what families expect and what people value. The page examines the step from a gap to an accusation.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/DEI as Anti-Discrimination|DEI as Anti-Discrimination]]** — Hire and promote on merit, and drop DEI. A fix that sorts people by race in order to undo sorting by race keeps race as the thing that decides, so it spreads the thing it was meant to cure. The page weighs the business case and the moral case separately.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/White Guilt|White Guilt]]** — Nobody is guilty for what their ancestors did. Guilt comes from what a person does, and what a person can do is something they build in their own lifetime, so it cannot be handed down any more than a skill can. Guilt by descent is not just unfair. It does not make sense. The page asks whether an inherited debt of that kind can make sense at all.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Who Gets Called Racist|Who Gets Called Racist]]** — Calling someone racist is mostly a way of ending an argument, not a description of the person. The page looks at what the accusation does and what answering it takes.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Grooming Gang Cover-Up|The Grooming Gang Cover-Up]]** — It happened, and the institutions hid it. Anyone who named the pattern lost their job, so nobody named it, and the record stayed empty. The British press works the same way. The page traces why staying silent paid and what it cost.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Moral Language as Leverage|Moral Language as Leverage]]** — Moral words only move people who still hold the values behind them. On anyone else they are noise, and it does not matter who is saying them. The page is about how a society keeps order with that kind of language.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Schooling - The Format Filter|Schooling - The Format Filter]]** — Sitting still at a desk all day suits girls better than boys, and when boys fail at it, schools blame the boys instead of the format. The page follows what the format taught each sex about the other.

## Faith

- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/What Faiths Demand of the State|What Faiths Demand of the State]]** — The question that matters about a religion is what it would have the state do, more than whether the religion is true. Not Christian, secular and agnostic, and some Christian values are good ones without the belief behind them. A quote from the Bible does not count as an argument here. Islam's reformers are worth watching for the same reason, from outside: what they would have the state do. The page sets out what Christianity and Islam each ask of the state.

## Money and the state

- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Socialism as Moral Correction|Socialism as Moral Correction]]** — Socialism points at real problems and then makes the state bigger, and the state is what caused the problems. The page takes the moral case underneath the economic one.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/Financial Slavery|Financial Slavery]]** — A debt that pays the lender more when you miss a payment, and that bankruptcy cannot cancel, is a way of taking people's work without owning them. The page describes how that works on a whole population.
- **[[wiki/Worldviews & the Political Order/The Organized Minority|The Organized Minority]]** — No settled position. Whether an organized few always end up running things is still an open question. The page argues that they do.
- **The top and bottom two percent.** Remove the top two percent of a society and it stops working. Remove the bottom two percent and it does better on income, crime, and how much people give. No page yet.

## Positions with no page behind them

- **Being American.** Being American comes from what a person holds and does, and it can be taken on by someone whose parents came from somewhere else.
- **Givers and takers.** America has people who give more than they take and people who take more than they give. The difference is what a person wants to do, and a person can move from one to the other, so it is not a fixed kind of person.
- **Who a person is.** Who you are is something you acquire by what you learn to do, not something you are born as. Nothing you acquire can be inherited by anyone else.
- **Abortion.** Stop it after the first trimester rather than ban it outright, and still not a fan of them.
