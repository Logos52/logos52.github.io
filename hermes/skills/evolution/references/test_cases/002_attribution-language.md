# Test Case: Attribution Language

**Source Type:** Agent-generated summary from article
**Related Wiki Area:** General (multiple areas)

## L3 Input

The source explains that self-regulation is different from self-management. According to the author, self-regulation is the real-time monitoring and adjustment of learning, while self-management focuses on building external systems. The text states that without strong self-regulation, even good techniques will fail under pressure. It claims that the Core Loop of Self-Regulation is what allows people to keep improving even when conditions are imperfect.

## User's Feedback & Corrections
- Removed multiple instances of attribution language: “According to the author”, “The text states”, “It claims that”.
- These phrases add distance and reduce signal. The ideas should stand on their own without constantly referencing the source.
- Rewrote sentences to speak directly about the concepts rather than what the source says about them.

## Target L2 Characteristics
- Direct, confident language that presents ideas without meta-commentary.
- No unnecessary references to “the source”, “the author”, or “the text”.
- High signal density — every sentence should carry useful weight.
- Maintains the substance of the original while removing the distancing language.

## Notes
This pattern appears frequently in agent summaries of articles or books. The user has repeatedly flagged this as one of the most common issues that needs cleaning in L3 → L2 work.
