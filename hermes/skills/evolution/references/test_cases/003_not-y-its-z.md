# Test Case: "X is not Y, it's Z" Pattern

**Source Type:** Agent draft from technique discussion
**Related Wiki Area:** Self-Regulation / General Techniques

## L3 Input

Recovery is not just resting or taking a break. It is an active process of psychological detachment and targeted relaxation that allows the nervous system to reset. Self-regulation is not the same as self-management. Self-regulation is the real-time monitoring and adjustment during learning, while self-management is about designing the external conditions that support consistency over time.

## User's Feedback & Corrections
- Removed the “X is not Y, it’s Z” structure in multiple places. This pattern feels repetitive and slightly defensive.
- Rewrote the distinctions to be direct and positive rather than defining things by what they are not.
- Preferred phrasing that stands on its own merits instead of contrasting against something else.

## Target L2 Characteristics
- Clear distinctions are made without relying on the “not X, but Y” construction.
- Language feels affirmative and direct.
- Avoids the slightly academic or argumentative tone that repeated “X is not Y” patterns create.
- Still maintains useful contrast when needed, but frames it more cleanly.
