# MG & Kolb's Template

> A faithful Obsidian conversion of Pan's Notion "MG & Kolb's" template. Original system & method by **Pan** ([@pan.ps](https://panstemplates.notion.site/)). See the README.

> [!info] Pan's usage notes
> This is the template Pan uses (as of Jan 1, 2025). A few things to point out:
> 1. Tasks go on a calendar with start/end times; clicking ✅ on a task generates the OFFrest break timing.
> 2. Some pages are created automatically (recurring tasks: *weekly eval + goal tracking*, *Priority 0 check-in*, *Skills audit → feedback request*). You can turn these off, but the weekly eval + goal-tracking cadence is recommended.
> 3. The purple ☑️ button in Kolbs sets the current Kolbs to **Done** and creates **and opens** the next one (same name — renumber if you number them). Cycling only.
> 4. Goals isn't optimised (Pan hasn't used it in a while).
> 5. Duplicating can leave a "(1)" suffix on some relation names — remove it if present.
> 6. Filler example pages are included as examples — delete freely.

## Anchored goal

### Dissected processes

> [!note]- How to dissect
> What do you need to be good at to achieve this goal?
> - Be as specific as possible.
> - Focus on **processes, not outcomes**. Processes lead to outcomes and are controllable; outcomes are symptoms of the process.
>
> **Topics to address:** Mental Health · Focus & concentration · Time management & procrastination · Study skills & efficiency · Retention & time spent studying

### Evaluation of level

> [!note]- How to evaluate
> 1. Recheck the target level (what's needed for 10/10) and stage of learning (CI, CC, UC).
> 2. Rate your current level /10.
> 3. Justify this rating as objectively as possible — why do you think you've improved?
> 4. Find the 1% gains for next week.
