## Anchored goal

### Dissected processes

> [!note]- How to dissect
> What do you need to be good at to achieve this goal?
> - Be as specific as possible.
> - Focus on **processes, not outcomes**. Processes lead to outcomes and are controllable; outcomes are symptoms of the process.
>
> **Topics to address:** Mental Health · Focus & concentration · Time management & procrastination · Study skills & efficiency · Retention & time spent studying

### Evaluation of level

> [!note]- How to evaluate
> 1. Recheck the target level (what's needed for 10/10) and stage of learning (CI, CC, UC).
> 2. Rate your current level /10.
> 3. Justify this rating as objectively as possible — why do you think you've improved?
> 4. Find the 1% gains for next week.
