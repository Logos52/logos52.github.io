# 🎯 Goals

> Anchored, medium-term goals. **Frontmatter:** `type: goal`, `status` (Not started / In progress / Archived), `start-date`, `end-date`. Each goal page carries the goal-setting checklist — see [[Example Goal]].

> [!note]- Goal-setting process
> 1. **Identify** the potential goal (stay open-minded — goals are "potential").
> 2. **Critically examine** the goal and your motivations.
> 3. **Remain open** to better, unevaluated alternatives.
> 4. **Gather information** — focus on the *skills* needed, not just hard requirements.
> 5. **Create periodic, medium-term goals** (SMART).
> 6. **Create a short-term action plan** (SMARTER) between now and the next medium-term goal.
